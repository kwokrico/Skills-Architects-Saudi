# KSA Concept Design: Strategic Authority & Climate Integration

## 1. Critical Early Decisions (Risk Mitigation)
- **Authority Jurisdiction (AHJ) Mapping:** Define if the project falls under National (Baladiya/MoMRAH), Regional (e.g., Royal Commission for Riyadh City), or Giga-Project Authority (e.g., NEOM, Red Sea Global) frameworks.
- **SBC Occupancy & Fire Strategy:** Lock initial Occupancy Classification (SBC 201/301) and "Life Safety" concept (SBC 501). Identify high-level requirements for Fire Department Access Roads and fire water tank spatial needs early.
- **Envelope & Climate Performance:** Establish the Window-to-Wall Ratio (WWR) limits and shading strategies to meet SBC 601 (Energy Conservation) and *Mostadam* sustainability targets.
- **Sovereign Requirements:** Identify any specific setback, privacy (Majlis/family zoning), or cultural requirements unique to the specific Saudi region.

## 2. KSA-Specific Concept Deliverables
- **Massing & Solar Optimization:** Qualitative and quantitative shade studies to mitigate the heat island effect and reduce cooling loads (HVAC sizing).
- **Logistics & Emergency Access:** Preliminary SCD-compliant site circulation, including turning radii for heavy fire apparatus and dedicated service/delivery zones.
- **Code & Authority Basis of Design (BOD):** A concise document framing the project against SBC 201, 501, 601, and 1001, intended for early stakeholder alignment.
- **Modular/Precast Feasibility:** Assessment of Design for Manufacture and Assembly (DfMA) suitability to meet aggressive Vision 2030 delivery timelines.

## 3. Essential Inputs Required
- **Site Plot Plan & Krooki:** Including precise coordinates, setbacks, and neighbors.
- **GFA & Program Matrix:** Target Gross Floor Area, Plot Ratio, and specific typology (e.g., Luxury Hospitality, ROSHN-style Residential).
- **Authority Context:** Any existing NOCs or Preliminary Design Approvals (PDA) already in place.
- **Procurement Route:** (e.g., Design-Bid-Build vs. Fast-track Design & Build/EPC).
