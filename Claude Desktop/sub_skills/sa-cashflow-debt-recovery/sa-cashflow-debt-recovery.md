# KSA Commercial Management & Debt Recovery (Architecture)

## 1. Core Saudi Commercial Principles
* **Milestone Alignment:** Ensure billing is strictly tied to objective "Evidence of Submission" (e.g., SBPS Portal upload confirmation or Baladiya preliminary approval) rather than subjective "Client Satisfaction."
* **Variation Discipline (Clause 13):** On Saudi fast-track projects, "Design Creep" is a major risk. Maintain a live Variations Tracker and issue 'Notice of Claim for Additional Services' the moment a client brief deviates from the agreed SBC-compliant scope.
* **The "NOC" Lever:** In the KSA market, the issuance of No Objection Certificates (NOCs) and final IFC (Issue for Construction) sets are critical leverage points. Ensure payments are current before transitioning from Design Development to Authority Submission stages.
* **Proactive Ageing Management:** Given the 60-90 day payment cycles common in the region, escalate "uncertified" invoices early to the Client’s Finance Director or the PMO leadership to avoid the "dispute by default" trap.

## 2. Required Context for Consultation
To provide actionable advice, I require the following project particulars:
* **Contractual Framework:** (e.g., FIDIC 1999 Red/Silver Book with Saudi Particular Conditions or a bespoke Giga-Project Consultant Agreement).
* **Current Ageing Profile:** Total outstanding, days overdue, and the specific "Hold Point" (e.g., pending Ministry approval, lack of signed Change Order, or budget reallocation).
* **Submission Status:** Detailed list of deliverables uploaded to the Common Data Environment (CDE) vs. those formally accepted by the Engineer/Client.
* **Stakeholder Hierarchy:** Is the client a Government Entity (PIF-backed), a private developer, or a Main Contractor (under a D&B/EPC route)?
