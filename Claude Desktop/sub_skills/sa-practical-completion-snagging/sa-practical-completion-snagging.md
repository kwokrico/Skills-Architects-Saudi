# KSA Practical Completion & Snagging (The Rico Kwok Standard)

## 1. Critical Path to Handover (The "No-Go" Blockers)
- **Life Safety & Saudi Civil Defence (SCD):** Ensuring all active/passive systems meet SBC 501. Handover cannot occur without the SCD Inspection and subsequent NOC for Occupancy.
- **SBC Compliance Documentation:** Verification of the "Golden Thread"—Material Approval Requests (MARs), Inspection Requests (IRs), and test reports required for the final Building Completion Certificate (BCC) through the Baladiya/SBPS portal.
- **MEP Commissioning & Extreme Climate Performance:** Verification that HVAC systems handle peak summer loads and that façade integrity prevents sand/dust ingress (SBC 601/SBC 301).

## 2. Strategic Snagging & Closeout Methodology
- **Tiered Categorization:** 1. **Type A (Operational/Safety):** Must be cleared for TOC (e.g., life safety, water-tightness, MEP functionality).
    2. **Type B (Cosmetic/Minor):** Logged for the Defects Liability Period (DLP) under Clause 11 of FIDIC.
- **Zone-Based Handover:** Implementing a "staggered" closeout (e.g., floor-by-floor in high-rise) to manage subcontractor resources effectively under fast-track pressures.
- **De-snagging Cadence:** Established 48-hour turnarounds for re-inspections to maintain momentum during the "Pre-Commissioning" phase.

## 3. Required Project Data for Analysis
- **Contractual Framework:** Is this FIDIC Red Book (1999/2017) with KSA Particular Conditions, or a bespoke EPC/Turnkey contract?
- **Authority Status:** Current status of SCD preliminary inspections and SASO certifications for key materials.
- **Handover Schedule:** The "Target TOC" date vs. the current status of the Testing & Commissioning (T&C) plan.
- **Snagging Data:** Access to the Common Data Environment (CDE) or snagging software (e.g., Procore, Aconex, Dalux) for trend analysis.

## 4. Risks & Mitigations
- **DLP Management:** Planning for 365-day (or 730-day) maintenance cycles considering the harsh Saudi environment (thermal expansion/corrosion).
- **Decennial Liability:** Addressing the 10-year structural guarantee requirements under Saudi Law.
