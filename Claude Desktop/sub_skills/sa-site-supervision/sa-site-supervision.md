# KSA Site Supervision: Design Integrity & Statutory Risk Control

## 1. Core Supervision Objectives
- **SBC & Life Safety Continuity:** Ensuring site-level execution matches the approved **SBC 501 (Fire Protection)** and **SBC 1001 (Accessibility)** strategies to prevent bottlenecks during Saudi Civil Defence (SCD) inspections.
- **Envelope & Durability:** Monitoring high-performance façade assembly to mitigate thermal bridging, sand ingress, and UV degradation in accordance with **SBC 601 (Energy Conservation)**.
- **Audit-Ready Documentation:** Maintaining the "Golden Thread" of RFIs, Material Submittals (MAS), and Mock-up approvals to facilitate a smooth handover and **Building Completion Certificate (BCC)** issuance via the Baladiya/Momrah portal.

## 2. High-Priority Risk Vectors in the KSA Context
- **The "Service Gap":** Critical coordination of MEP penetrations through fire-rated assemblies (SBC 501) and ensuring fire-stopping systems are UL/FM listed and SASO approved.
- **Fast-Track VE Pressures:** Identifying "Value Engineering" proposals that compromise long-term performance, specifically regarding waterproofing membranes, stone cladding fixings (corrosion resistance), and HVAC redundancy.
- **Civil Defence (SCD) Alignment:** Verifying that site deviations haven't invalidated the approved Fire Life Safety (FLS) drawings, which can lead to significant delays in final occupancy permits.
- **Environmental Management:** Oversight of dust control and concrete curing protocols during extreme summer peaks (June–August) to prevent structural micro-cracking.

## 3. Required Project Data for Assessment
To provide actionable advice, I require the following:
- **Contractual Framework:** Is this a FIDIC Red Book (Consultant-led) or Yellow Book (D&B/EPC) setup? Who holds the "Designer of Record" (DOR) liability?
- **Authority Status:** Current status of Saudi Civil Defence NOCs and any pending Baladiya/Municipality inspections.
- **The "Critical Log":** A summary of open Non-Conformance Reports (NCRs), high-priority RFIs, and any pending Material Submittals affecting the critical path.
- **Giga-Project Specifics:** Any bespoke client requirements (e.g., NEOM, Red Sea Global, or ROSHN-specific design guidelines) that supersede standard SBC applications.
