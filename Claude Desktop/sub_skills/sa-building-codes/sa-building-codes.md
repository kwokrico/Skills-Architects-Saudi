# KSA Building Codes & Authority Compliance (SBC + SBPS)

## 1. Summary
This module governs the technical alignment of design deliverables with the **SBC National Committee (SBCNC)** standards and the **Saudi Building Permit System (SBPS)**. It ensures that the "Golden Thread" of compliance is maintained from Concept through to the **Occupancy Certificate (Is’har)**, preventing late-stage Civil Defence (998) or Municipality (Baladiya) rejections.

## 2. Essential Project Parameters
To provide a precise compliance roadmap, the following must be defined:
- **Location & AHJ:** Is this a standard Municipality (Riyadh, Jeddah, DMA) or a Special Authority (NEOM, RSG, DGDA, Royal Commission)?
- **Occupancy Classification:** Primary and secondary uses per **SBC 201/801** (e.g., Assembly A-3, Business B, High-Rise Residential R-2).
- **Construction Type:** (e.g., Type I-A Fire-Resistive) and total built-up area (BUA).
- **Current Milestone:** Concept (Pre-consultation), Schematic (Preliminary Approval), or Detailed Design (Final Building Permit).

## 3. Core SBC Regulatory Framework
*All submissions must reference the latest SBC editions:*
- **SBC 201 (Architectural/Structural):** Focus on occupancy, heights, and area limitations.
- **SBC 501 (Fire Protection):** The critical path. Integration of **Saudi Civil Defence** requirements and NFPA-aligned life safety.
- **SBC 601 (Energy):** Envelope performance, U-value compliance, and integration with **Mostadam** or LEED requirements.
- **SBC 1001 (Accessibility):** Universal design compliance, critical for MoMRAH (Ministry of Municipalities and Housing) audits.

## 4. Professional Delivery Workflow
1. **AHJ Pre-Consultation:** Validate the "Code Basis" with the AHJ before finishing Schematic Design. Avoid "designing in a vacuum."
2. **Unified Code Narrative:** Generate a single **Statement of Compliance** document. Architecture, MEP, and Structure must sign off on the same fire/life safety strategy to avoid "siloed" SBC 801 vs. SBC 501 conflicts.
3. **SBPS Portal Management:** Ensure the **Qualified Saudi Consultant (Local Consultant)** is engaged early for the portal upload to avoid administrative bottlenecks.
4. **Change Management:** Any Design Change (Variation) must be re-vetted against the issued **Preliminary Approval** to ensure the Building Permit remains valid.
