# KSA Professional Indemnity & Design Liability Control

## 1. Critical Risk Pillars in Saudi Practice
- **SBC & Civil Defence Compliance:** Liability often shifts when "Equivalent Compliance" (NFPA/International) is rejected by Saudi Civil Defence in favor of strict SBC 501. We must define who carries the risk for redesign due to evolving SBCNC interpretations.
- **The "Fast-Track" Gap:** Documenting the risks of early procurement packages (Long Lead Items) issued before the Building Permit (SBPS) is secured.
- **Standard of Care:** Moving away from "Fitness for Purpose" (often pushed by Saudi developers) toward the RIBA/ARB standard of "Reasonable Skill and Care" practiced by an international consultant in the KSA market.

## 2. Common PI Triggers on Vision 2030 Projects
- **Interface Ambiguity:** Overlaps between the Lead Consultant, Specialist Lighting/Façade consultants, and the Contractor’s detailed design team on D&B packages.
- **Unregulated Third-Party Reliance:** Issuing reliance letters to Saudi lenders or Giga-project entities (NEOM, RSG) without back-to-back indemnity or defined liability caps.
- **Material Substitutions:** Unauthorized site-level changes to fire-rated assemblies or thermal insulation (SBC 601) that void the design's code compliance.

## 3. Required Data for Assessment
- **Contractual Framework:** Specify if we are operating under a FIDIC Red/Yellow Book (with Saudi Particular Conditions) or a bespoke EPC/Giga-project agreement.
- **Liability Particulars:** Current aggregate liability caps, exclusion of consequential losses, and any "Decennial Liability" (Muqawala) considerations under the Saudi Civil Transactions Law.
- **Authority Status:** Current status of the Building Permit (SBPS) and any unresolved "Observations" from the Municipality or Civil Defence.
