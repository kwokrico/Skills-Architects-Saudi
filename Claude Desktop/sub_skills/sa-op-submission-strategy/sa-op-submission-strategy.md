# KSA Authority Submissions & Permitting Strategy (SBC/SBPS/SCD)

## 1. Summary
This skill optimizes the regulatory roadmap for Saudi projects. It ensures alignment between the **Saudi Building Code (SBC)** requirements and the specific submission portals (SBPS/Baladiya or Giga-Project bespoke systems). The goal is to mitigate "Technical Refusal" risks by synchronizing the Fire Life Safety (FLS) strategy with architectural and MEP deliverables before the final upload.

## 2. Regulatory & Authority Mapping
* **Standard Municipal Route:** Riyadh/Jeddah/DMA Municipality via **SBPS** (Baladiya). Requires MoMRAH-certified consultants.
* **Special Authority Regimes:** **NEOM, Red Sea Global (RSG), Diriyah (DGDA), and RC (Royal Commission)**. These often have bespoke "Building Regulations" that augment the SBC.
* **SCD Interface:** Alignment with **SBC 501** (Fire Protection) and the **Saudi Civil Defence Manual**. Early engagement for high-hazard or high-rise occupancies is non-negotiable.

## 3. Minimum Information Required
To provide a viable submission strategy, please provide:
* **Project Profile:** City, Plot Area, Occupancy Classification (per SBC 201), and Building Height/Total GFA.
* **Submission Stage:** Concept (NOC), Preliminary Design Approval, or Final Building Permit (BP).
* **Procurement Route:** Traditional (DBB), Fast-track Design-Build, or EPC (common for Giga-projects).
* **Technical Constraints:** Presence of atria, high-rise (over 23m/30m triggers), or deep basements.

## 4. Enhanced Submission Components
* **FLS Strategy Report:** A dedicated **SBC 501** compliance narrative. This is the "Golden Thread" for Saudi Civil Defence approval.
* **SBC Compliance Matrix:** A discipline-specific checklist (SBC 201, 301, 501, 601, 801) demonstrating how the design meets the National Committee's standards.
* **Calculations & Models:** Structural (SBC 301), Thermal (SBC 601/Mostadam), and Hydraulic (SBC 701) data as required by the specific Baladiya.
* **Comment Response Shield:** A formal tracker for addressing previous AHJ/Third-Party Reviewer (TPR) comments to prevent iterative rejections.

## 5. Rico’s Expert Recommendations
* **Fire Strategy Lock:** Freeze the Fire Life Safety (FLS) strategy during Schematic Design. Any late-stage changes to egress or compartmentation after SCD "Initial Approval" can delay a Saudi project by months.
* **The "Mostadam" Link:** Ensure **SBC 601 (Energy Conservation)** documentation is aligned with **Mostadam** (or LEED) credits to avoid conflicting MEP specifications.
* **Certified Consultant (Office) Role:** Ensure the local Consultant of Record (CR) is engaged early for the SBPS portal upload; administrative errors regarding "Engineering Office" credentials are the leading cause of permit delays.
* **Civil Defence Early Look:** For complex assemblies (The Line, Qiddiya, etc.), seek a "pre-consultation" with Civil Defence to validate performance-based designs.
