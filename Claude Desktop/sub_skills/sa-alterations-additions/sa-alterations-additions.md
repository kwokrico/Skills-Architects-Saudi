# KSA Alterations & Additions (Existing Asset Strategy)

## 1. Regulatory & Compliance Baseline
- **Permit Audit:** Verify the "As-Built" status against the original **Building Completion Certificate (Occupancy Permit)**. Identify any unauthorized deviations that must be regularized via the **SBPS (Saudi Building Permit System)**.
- **SBC 501 Triggers:** Define if the scope constitutes a "Change of Use" or "Substantial Alteration" under **SBC 501 (Fire Protection)**, which may mandate upgrading the entire life-safety system to 2026 standards.
- **Structural Integrity:** Assess if proposed loads exceed the original design intent of **SBC 201**, requiring carbon-fiber reinforcement or structural steel bracing.

## 2. High-Risk Technical Factors in KSA
- **Passive Fire Sealing:** Existing MEP penetrations in older Riyadh/Jeddah assets often lack compliant fire-stopping. A&A must include a full audit of fire compartments.
- **Sand & Thermal Ingress:** Ensure new openings or envelope penetrations maintain the integrity of the vapor barrier and dust-seals, critical for KSA’s extreme temperature differentials.
- **Authority Phasing:** Coordination with **Saudi Civil Defence** for partial occupancy permits if the building remains operational during construction.

## 3. Essential Project Inputs
- **Existing Documentation:** Original IFCs, As-Builts, and the current **Civil Defence License (Sijil)**.
- **Scope Definition:** Detailed delta between existing and proposed GFA, occupancy load, and hazard classification.
- **Site Constraints:** Logistics plan for "Live Environment" working, including noise/dust mitigation and temporary egress routes during the holy month of Ramadan or peak summer periods.
