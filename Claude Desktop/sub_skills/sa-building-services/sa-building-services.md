# KSA Building Services: Architectural & Regulatory Coordination

## 1. Core Architectural Focus Areas
- **Spatial Strategy & Efficiency:** Optimising Gross-to-Net ratios by vetting MEP plant requirements and riser distributions against **SBC 301** occupancy loads.
- **Life Safety & Fire Protection:** Ensuring architectural layouts (compartmentation) align perfectly with **SBC 501** and **SCD** requirements for smoke management, fire pumps, and emergency egress lighting.
- **Envelope Integrity:** Mitigating thermal bridging and air leakage at MEP penetrations to meet **SBC 601 (Energy Conservation)** and **Mostadam** requirements.
- **Desert Durability:** Coordinating sand-trap louvres, multi-stage filtration (MERV ratings), and AHU positioning to prevent dust ingress and premature system failure.

## 2. High-Risk Saudi Coordination Conflicts
- **Headroom vs. Gravity Drainage:** Managing tight ceiling voids in high-end residential/hospitality pods where structural beams and MEP crossovers conflict.
- **Façade Integration:** Balancing aesthetic intent with the massive intake/exhaust requirements of Saudi cooling loads and the structural impact of large-scale louvre systems.
- **Acoustics & Vibration:** Isolation of roof-top chillers and basement pumps in proximity to luxury "Majlis" or sleeping areas, particularly in heavy RC structures.
- **Shaft Pressurisation:** Integrating staircase and lift shaft pressurisation fans within the constraints of Riyadh/Jeddah high-rise floor plates.

## 3. Essential Inputs for Design Progression
- **MEP Basis of Design (BOD):** Cooling load estimates, system type (District Cooling vs. VRF), and water storage requirements (domestic + fire).
- **Authority Constraints:** Specific **Civil Defence (SCD)** NOC status, **Mostadam** target level (Green/Bronze/Silver), and local utility (SEC/NWC) connection points.
- **Structural Constraints:** Transfer beam locations and slab thicknesses that dictate horizontal distribution zones.
- **Maintenance Strategy:** BMU (Building Maintenance Unit) coordination and clear access paths for heavy plant replacement in congested urban sites.
