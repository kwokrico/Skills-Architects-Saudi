# KSA Heritage Conservation & Adaptive Reuse Strategy

## 1. Conservation Philosophy & Regulatory Alignment
- **Significance Mapping:** Define the 'Statement of Significance.' Identify Grade A (untouchable fabric/Sadu motifs/traditional masonry) vs. Grade C (reversible modern interventions).
- **SBC Integration:** Developing "Equivalency Strategies" for **SBC 501 (Fire)** and **SBC 1001 (Accessibility)** where traditional geometries (e.g., narrow 'Sikkas' or steep stairs) conflict with modern life safety.
- **Authority Liaison:** Managing the interface between the **Ministry of Culture (Heritage Commission)**, **Saudi Civil Defence**, and regional giga-project authorities (DGDA, RCU, Soudah Development).

## 2. Technical & Climate Resilience
- **Thermal Performance:** Enhancing traditional thick-wall (adobe/stone) performance to meet **SBC 601** energy targets without compromising breathability or causing interstitial condensation.
- **Structural Stabilization:** Strategies for modern MEP integration (concealed VRF/fire suppression) within fragile historical floor diaphragms and roofs.
- **Material Authenticity:** Specification of local lime-based plasters, Riyadh stone, or Athul wood in compliance with **SASO** standards while maintaining historical patina.

## 3. High-Value Risks in the Saudi Context
- **The "Discovery Gap":** Unforeseen structural instability or soil conditions (common in Al-Balad or Diriyah) leading to Variation Orders under **FIDIC Clause 13**.
- **Authority Reversals:** Changes in preservation grade post-Concept approval due to evolving Heritage Commission mandates.
- **Fast-Track Pressure:** Balancing the painstaking nature of restoration with the aggressive 'Vision 2030' handover milestones.

## 4. Required Inputs for Advice
- **Asset Profile:** Location (e.g., Turaif, Al-Balad, AlUla), heritage grade, and current legal protection status.
- **Technical Baseline:** Availability of Point Cloud surveys, 3D scans, or previous 'Mostadam' sustainability assessments.
- **Operational Intent:** Proposed use (Boutique Hotel, Cultural Museum, F&B) and the target **Commercial Opening Date (COD)**.
