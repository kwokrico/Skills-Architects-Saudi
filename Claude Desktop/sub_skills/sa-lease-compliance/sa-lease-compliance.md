# KSA Land Governance & Plot Compliance (SBC & Authority Alignment)

## 1. Summary of the Issue
In the KSA context, project viability is dictated by the **Krooki Al-Tandhim** (Organizational Survey) and the specific Masterplan Guidelines of the governing body. Misalignment between the architectural footprint and authority-mandated setbacks, utility easements, or "Sikka" (alleyway) requirements often leads to rejection at the **Building Permit (SBPS)** stage or, worse, during **Civil Defence (SBC 501)** fire access review.

## 2. Information Required for Assessment
To provide a pragmatic compliance path, I require:
- **Governing Authority:** (e.g., Riyadh Municipality/Momrah, Royal Commission for AlUla, NEOM, Red Sea Global, or MODON).
- **The Krooki / Site Allocation Plan:** Detailed dimensions, total plot area (m²), and North arrow orientation.
- **Setback Requirements:** Front, side, and rear setbacks as per the specific district's zoning or the Developer's Masterplan Guidelines.
- **Easements & Utilities:** Location of existing MV/LV substations, water lines, or telecommunications corridors that restrict basement excavation.
- **Access Strategy:** Primary and secondary road classifications for site ingress/egress and Saudi Civil Defence appliance routing.

## 3. Regulatory Context & Practical Impacts
- **SBC 301 (General) & Zoning:** Verification of the "Built-up Area" (BUA) and Floor Area Ratio (FAR) against the latest Saudi Building Code occupancy classifications.
- **Fire Separation (SBC 501):** Impact of plot boundary proximity on exterior wall fire-rating requirements and opening protections (limiting the percentage of unprotected openings).
- **Sikkat & Easements:** Ensuring basement structures do not encroach into "no-build" zones or public utility corridors, which is a common cause for NOC rejections.
- **Levels & Topography:** Alignment of the "Finished Floor Level" (FFL) with the public sidewalk/road levels as mandated by the Municipality to ensure proper storm-water drainage.

## 4. Recommended Approach
1.  **Validation:** Cross-reference the Krooki with the Schematic Design to identify any encroachment or setback violations early.
2.  **NOC Coordination:** Identify the sequence of No Objection Certificates (NOCs) required from utility providers (SEC, NWC) before the Main Building Permit submission.
3.  **Civil Defence Pre-Check:** Verify that the "Fire Lane" is within the plot limits or legally designated public space to ensure compliance with SBC 501.
