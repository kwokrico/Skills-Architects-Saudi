## Output rules (all templates)

1. **Deliverable-first** — no "As an AI" or process narration.  
2. **Advisory boundary** — no stamped engineering, legal sign-off, or guaranteed approval language.  
3. **Assumptions** — always list gaps when AHJ, occupancy, or approval status was inferred.  
4. **Arabic titles** — where SBPS or Baladiya requires bilingual sheet titles, note both languages in transmittal tables.  
5. **Golden Thread** — for construction and handover artifacts, cross-reference MAR / IR / test record IDs.
