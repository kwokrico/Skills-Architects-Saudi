# KSA Structural Systems: Architectural Leadership & Coordination

## 1. Core Coordination Pillars
- **Grid Optimization:** Balancing SBC 1001 (Accessibility) parking requirements with residential/commercial spans to minimize costly transfer slabs in high-rise podiums.
- **Movement & Thermal Strategy:** Defining structural expansion joints (SEJs) for long-plan assets (e.g., malls, industrial hubs) to mitigate extreme diurnal temperature swings ($\Delta T$ of up to 50°C).
- **Façade Interface:** Managing deflection limits and tolerances for high-performance curtain walls under Saudi wind loads and sandstorm pressures.
- **Civil Defence Integration:** Coordinating structural fire protection (SBC 501/801) for primary members and ensuring integrity of pressurized smoke-controlled cores.

## 2. KSA-Specific Regulatory & Technical Realities
- **SBC 201 & 301 Compliance:** Ensuring structural logic supports the Building Permit System (SBPS) workflow and Baladiya structural vetting.
- **Foundation Challenges:** Addressing high water tables in Jeddah/Coastal projects or hard rock excavation in Riyadh (DIRC/RCRC zones).
- **Fast-Track Delivery:** Evaluating Post-Tensioning (PT) vs. Precast/Modular (DfMA) to meet aggressive Giga-project milestones (NEOM/ROSHN).
- **Durability:** Specification oversight for sulfate-resisting cement and rebar protection in high-salinity coastal environments (Red Sea Global/NEOM).

## 3. Critical Information Required for Analysis
- **Project Typology & Location:** (e.g., Luxury High-rise in Riyadh vs. Coastal Resort in Amaala).
- **Substructure Data:** Basement depth, proximity to neighboring plots, and existing NOC status.
- **Architectural Constraints:** Target floor-to-floor heights, cantilever ambitions, and façade system type.
- **Procurement Route:** (e.g., Design-Bid-Build vs. EPC/Design-Build) to determine the level of detail required for the Structural Design Report.
