# KSA Professional Services Strategy (Scope, Risk & SBC Compliance)

## 1. Scope Definition & Deliverable Alignment
- **Stage Mapping:** Explicitly align RIBA Stages 0-7 with Saudi project milestones (e.g., Preliminary Design Approval, Structural Permit, Final Building Permit).
- **SBC Technical Narratives:** Inclusion of mandatory specialist reports for SBC 501 (Fire), SBC 601 (Energy), and Mostadam/LEED sustainability statements.
- **BIM & Digital Twins:** Definition of LOD (Level of Development) requirements per ISO 19650, tailored to the specific giga-project CDE (Common Data Environment).
- **Site Supervision (AOR):** Clear distinction between Lead Design Consultant (LDC) duties and the Architect of Record (AOR) role required for Baladiya/SBPS sign-off.

## 2. KSA-Specific Commercial Risk Controls
- **Authority Cycle Caps:** Defining the number of "Standard" resubmissions for Saudi Civil Defence and Municipality approvals before Additional Services clauses trigger.
- **Fast-Track Overlap:** Managing the 'Design-to-Tender' compression; pricing for "Early Works Packages" (piling/shoring) separate from the Main Works permit.
- **Giga-Project Standards:** Accounting for bespoke Design Guidelines (e.g., NEOM, Red Sea Global, or Diriyah Gate) that exceed baseline SBC requirements.
- **Escalation & Currency:** Addressing Saudi-specific labor market volatility and "Saudi Particular Conditions" under FIDIC White Book/Red Book.

## 3. Critical Project Inputs Required
- **Authority Jurisdiction:** Is the project under a Municipality (Baladiya) or a Special Entity (e.g., Royal Commission, NEOM, or RSG)?
- **Procurement Route:** Design-Bid-Build, D&B, or EPC/Turnkey? (This dictates the depth of Tender Documentation vs. Construction IFC sets).
- **SBC Classification:** Occupancy group and construction type (e.g., High-rise/SBC 501 requirements) which drastically alters the specialist sub-consultant load.
- **Target Milestones:** Is there a fixed "Opening Date" (common for Riyadh Season or Vision 2030 targets) requiring an accelerated manpower surge?
