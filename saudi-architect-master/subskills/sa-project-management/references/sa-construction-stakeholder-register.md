## Programme risk flags

- **Ramadan / Hajj:** Reduced hours and authority processing (Western region).
- **Summer heat:** Midday work restrictions; programme float.
- **Sandstorm season:** Façade and earthworks exposure.

Update this register at Stage 1 and revise at mobilisation (Stage 5).
