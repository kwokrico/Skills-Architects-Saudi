# Telecom licensed works — KSA (STC / Mobily)

Use with `sa-site-establishment`. Excavation near active plant requires licensed undertaker coordination.

---

## Scope

- **Not** general ICT building design — route to `sa-building-services` for in-building systems.
- **This ref:** External fibre/copper protection, diversion, and excavation permits.

---

## When triggered

| Condition | Action |
|-----------|--------|
| Excavation within utility corridor | Request as-built from STC/Mobily |
| Piling / deep foundation near street | Slant drilling or protection slab |
| New permanent road crossing | Duct bank in civil drawings |
| Hoarding footing near pavement | Hand dig / trial pit with undertaker witness |

---

## Licensed works framework

1. **Site meeting** with undertaker — mark plant on survey.
2. **Method statement** for hand dig zones, spade-and-vac rules.
3. **Licensed contractor** from undertaker approved list — verify before award.
4. **Permit to dig** — often 48–72h notice for urban fibre.

---

## Construction coordination

| Phase | Requirement |
|-------|-------------|
| Mobilisation | Protection plan on PCI pack |
| Earthworks | Undertaker standby for critical crossings |
| Permanent works | Duct reserve capacity per client ICT brief |
| As-built | Record duct routes for O&M |

---

## Risk hotspots

- **Unrecorded plant** — highest strike risk on older urban corridors (Jeddah Balad, central Riyadh).
- **Parallel SEC trenching** — coordinate joint trench only where authorities permit.
- **Delay cost** — programme float before piling start if diversion not complete.

---

## BD / SBPS referral context

Telecom diversion drawings may be required in civil permit package where public ROW affected. Index on SBPS transmittal (T-04).

---

## Output checklist

- [ ] Undertaker contact and ticket reference
- [ ] Protection plan drawing rev
- [ ] Licensed contractor named
- [ ] Dig permit dates aligned with piling programme
