# FIDIC on KSA projects (reference)

Use with `sa-tender-contract-administration`. **Advisory only** — not legal interpretation.

## Common forms

- Red Book (employer design) with KSA particular conditions
- Yellow / Silver Book (D&B / EPC) on giga-programmes

## Architectural touchpoints

- Employer's Requirements aligned to SBC 501 / 601 clauses
- Variations triggering re-approval (fire, occupancy, envelope)
- Defects notification and TOC linkage

## Claims hygiene

- Contemporaneous records (RFI, NCR, site minutes)
- Authority delay vs contractor delay separation
