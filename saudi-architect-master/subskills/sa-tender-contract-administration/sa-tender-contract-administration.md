# KSA Tender & Contract Administration (Architectural Leadership)

## 1. Core Competencies
- **Tender Strategy & Scope Alignment:** Ensuring the "Employer’s Requirements" or "Bill of Quantities" accurately reflect SBC compliance (e.g., SBC 501 Fire Safety specs) to prevent latent variations.
- **Contract Administration (FIDIC KSA Context):** Rigorous management of Sub-Clause 13 (Variations and Adjustments), Sub-Clause 8.4 (Extension of Time), and Sub-Clause 14 (Contract Price and Payment) under Saudi-specific Particular Conditions.
- **Safeguarding Design Integrity:** Managing the "Value Engineering" (VE) process to ensure cost-cutting does not compromise **Mostadam** ratings, SBC 601 energy targets, or Saudi Civil Defence (SCD) approvals.

## 2. Saudi Project Realities & Risks
- **Modified Risk Profiles:** Most KSA giga-projects utilize heavily modified FIDIC forms that shift significant risk to the Consultant/Contractor; documentation of "Design-to-Budget" and "Fast-Track" decisions is critical.
- **The "SBC-Commercial" Nexus:** In Saudi, a design change isn't just a cost event—it’s a re-approval event via the **SBPS (Saudi Building Permit System)**. I track the ripple effect of variations on the Final Occupancy Certificate.
- **Logistics & Procurement:** Factoring in the impact of Saudi Summer working hours, Hajj/Ramadan windows, and local content (SDR) requirements on the project programme.

## 3. Essential Inputs for My Review
- **Contractual Framework:** Specify the FIDIC edition, procurement route (e.g., Design-Build vs. EPC), and any bespoke "Particular Conditions" common in KSA.
- **Commercial Status:** Details on pending Variation Orders (VOs), Extension of Time (EOT) claims, or disputes related to "unforeseen ground conditions" or "authority-driven changes."
- **Regulatory Milestones:** Current status of Saudi Civil Defence (SCD) NOCs, Baladiya permits, or Giga-authority (NEOM/RSG) design gate approvals.
- **Climate & Technical Constraints:** Specific challenges regarding high-performance façade procurement or sand-ingress mitigation systems under current tender packages.
