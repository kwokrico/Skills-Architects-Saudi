# KSA Procurement Routes — Comparison

Use with `sa-procurement-strategy`. Advisory only — confirm client legal review for Particular Conditions.

---

## Side-by-side matrix

| Criterion | Traditional DBB | Design-Build (D&B) | EPC | Management contracting |
|-----------|-----------------|-------------------|-----|------------------------|
| **Design ownership pre-tender** | Consultant | Employer’s Requirements | Performance spec | Packages by trade |
| **Single point contractor** | No | Yes | Yes | CM coordinates trades |
| **Price certainty at award** | High (lump sum BoQ) | Medium–high | High (lump sum) | Lower until packages let |
| **Programme speed** | Moderate | Fast | Fastest | Fast if packages parallel |
| **SBC/SCD change risk** | Employer via VO | Contractor if ER gap | Contractor | Shared at interfaces |
| **Variation culture** | Controlled | Limited employer change | Very limited | High interface VO risk |
| **Typical FIDIC** | Red Book | Yellow Book | Silver Book | Red + trade contracts |
| **KSA sector fit** | Baladiya mid-rise | Hotels, residential | Industrial, giga utilities | Large mixed-use |

---

## Risk allocation summary

| Risk | DBB | D&B | EPC |
|------|-----|-----|-----|
| Design errors (pre-award) | Consultant | Employer ER / consultant | Employer spec |
| Authority rejection / resubmit | Employer | Employer unless ER defect | Contractor if performance spec |
| Sandstorm / heat productivity | Shared (EOT arguable) | Contractor pricing | Contractor |
| Ramadan productivity | Often employer programme | Priced in tender | Priced in tender |
| Unforeseen ground | FIDIC 4.12 | FIDIC 4.12 | Contractor |
| SDR / local content | Employer spec | Employer ER clause | Employer spec |

---

## Giga-project notes

- **NEOM / RSG:** Often D&B or EPC with heavy Particular Conditions; NEC on selected packages.
- **ROSHN:** Standardized residential prototypes — D&B with repeat ER modules.
- **MODON:** EPC common for industrial shells.
