# Weather & Seasonal EOT by Procurement Route — KSA

Use with `sa-procurement-strategy` and `sa-tender-contract-administration`. Not legal advice.

---

## KSA seasonal factors (programme + EOT)

| Factor | Typical impact | Documentation |
|--------|----------------|---------------|
| **Extreme heat** (Jun–Sep) | Midday work bans; concrete pour windows | Site records, temperature logs |
| **Sandstorms** | Crane/façade shutdown; air quality | MET records, site diary |
| **Ramadan** | Reduced hours (~6h); authority slowdown | Calendar + labour histogram |
| **Hajj** | Western region logistics; labour availability | Programme baseline |
| **Prayer times** | Daily stops (contractor programme) | Local labour practice |

## Treatment by contract type

### FIDIC Red / Yellow / Silver

| Event | Sub-Clause pointer | Typical position |
|-------|-------------------|------------------|
| Exceptional adverse weather (if defined) | 8.4(c) | Measure against 10-year stats if PC define |
| Authority delay (permit) | 8.4 / 8.5 employer risk | Employer event if not contractor-caused |
| Change in law (SBC edition) | 13.7 | Employer risk if mandated mid-contract |
| Ramadan not in baseline | 8.4 | Often employer unless priced |

**KSA practice:** Particular Conditions often **exclude** generic weather — contractor prices productivity; employer carries authority delay if ER complete.

### NEC4 ECC

| Event | Mechanism |
|-------|-----------|
| Weather | Compensation event if weather recorder thresholds in contract data |
| Authority hold | Early warning + CE for client-caused delay |

### DBB (no FIDIC)

JCT-style "exceptionally adverse weather" rarely used in KSA; rely on bespoke VO/EOT clauses.

---

## Architect / CA advisory role

1. Distinguish **authority delay** (SBPS/SCD) from **site productivity** loss.
2. Require contemporaneous records (site diary, MET, labour returns).
3. Flag design changes that reset permit clock — not weather EOT.
4. Quantum of EOT → QS support; legal interpretation → counsel.

## Procurement-stage mitigation

| Route | Mitigation |
|-------|------------|
| DBB | Float in employer programme; authority risk event in PC |
| D&B | Require contractor meteorological productivity assumptions in tender |
| EPC | Performance dates with LDs; force majeure narrowly drafted |
| All | Pre-order SASO long-leads before seasonal shutdown |
