# KSA Project Resource Levelling & Delivery Protection

## 1. Core Focus Areas
- **Submission Gate Alignment:** Synchronizing resource spikes with KSA-specific milestones: Preliminary Design Approval, SCD Fire Safety NOC, and Final Building Permit via the SBPS portal.
- **Critical Role Identification:** Ensuring dedicated "Lead SBC Coordinator" and "Fire & Life Safety (FLS) Interface" roles are staffed early to prevent downstream rework.
- **Burn vs. Value Progress:** Moving beyond simple hour-tracking to measure "earned value" against technical maturity (e.g., Have the SBC 501/601 requirements been baked into the BIM model before the surge?).
- **Mitigating "Review Debt":** Tracking the volume of unaddressed comments from MoM/Authorities that threaten to derail the next submission phase.

## 2. Practical Delivery Levers
- **Design Freezes:** Enforcing "hard freezes" on core elements (SBC 201 Structural framing, SBC 501 Egress routes, and Façade modules) prior to the high-intensity Production/Tender stages.
- **Sprint-Based Delivery:** Transitioning from calendar-based schedules to "Submission Sprints" that align with the rapid iteration cycles typical of NEOM, Red Sea Global, or ROSHN.
- **Interface Management:** Utilizing dedicated resource pools for "External Authority Coordination" to shield the core design team from the administrative churn of NOC tracking.
- **Modular Efficiency:** Pivoting resources toward DfMA (Design for Manufacture and Assembly) to reduce on-site supervision intensity and simplify SBC compliance documentation.

## 3. Required Inputs for Assessment
- **Master Programme & Deliverable List:** Including specific Saudi Authority submission dates and Client "Decision Gates."
- **Team Matrix:** Current headcount, including local KSA-based staff vs. international reach-back support.
- **Bottleneck Analysis:** Current status of SCD approvals, Baladiya comments, or unresolved MEP/Structural clashes that are consuming disproportionate man-hours.
- **Contractual Baseline:** Relevant FIDIC clauses or Particular Conditions regarding "Acceleration" or "Extension of Time" (EOT) related to resource constraints.
