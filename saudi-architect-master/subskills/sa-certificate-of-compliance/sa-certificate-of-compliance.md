# KSA Completion & Occupancy Compliance (SBC Standards)

## 1. Summary of the Issue
In the Saudi market, "Completion" is a multi-tiered milestone. Physical completion must be mirrored by **Regulatory Compliance Verification**. Failure to align the **As-Built** records with the **Saudi Building Permit System (SBPS)** data and **SBC 501 (Fire Protection)** testing results often leads to rejected Occupancy Certificate applications, triggering liquidated damages under FIDIC Clause 8.7 or 14.15.

## 2. Regulatory & Authority Position (SBC/MOMRAH)
* **Occupancy Permit:** Per Saudi Law, no building can be occupied without a Final Occupancy Certificate issued via the Baladiya portal.
* **SCD Approval:** Final inspection by Saudi Civil Defence is mandatory for Life Safety systems. Systems must operate exactly as per the approved 'Safety Drawings'.
* **Mostadam/SBC 601:** Evidence of Energy Conservation compliance and sustainability credits (if mandated by the Giga-project authority) must be submitted at this stage.

## 3. Practical Checklist (Architect-Led)
* **SBC Compliance Statement:** A formal declaration from the Designer of Record (DoR) and Supervision Consultant that the works comply with the Saudi Building Code.
* **As-Built vs. Approved Permit:** Verification that any "Minor Variations" during construction were captured via the 'Revision' process in the Baladiya portal to avoid inspection mismatches.
* **Life Safety Matrix (SBC 501):** Validated Testing & Commissioning (T&C) reports for smoke management, fire suppression, and emergency lighting, ready for Civil Defence audit.
* **Material Warranties (SASO):** Confirmation that all installed materials match the SASO-certified submittals approved during the construction phase.
* **Operational Readiness:** Coordination of the "Golden Thread" of information for the Facility Management (FM) team.

## 4. Inputs Needed for Review
* **Contractual Milestones:** The specific definition of "Taking-Over" (Section 10 of FIDIC) and any Saudi-specific Particular Conditions.
* **Authority Status:** Have the Civil Defence and Structural inspections been scheduled? Are there any open 'Observations' from the Municipality?
* **Snag Status:** A categorized list identifying 'Life Safety' vs. 'Cosmetic' snags.
* **Giga-project Specifics:** Any additional requirements from NEOM, Red Sea Global, or ROSHN-specific Handover Manuals.

## 5. Implications
* **Programme:** Delays in the Occupancy Certificate directly delay the "Taking-Over Certificate" (TOC), extending the Contractor’s insurance and security obligations.
* **Risk:** Financial exposure due to delayed commercial operations (especially in Hospitality/Retail sectors).
