# KSA Renovation, Fit-out & Change-of-Occupancy (SBC Delivery Playbook)

## 1. Professional Mindset: "Minor" is a Risk
In the Saudi market, "minor works" are frequently scrutinized as strictly as new builds if they impact the **Life Safety Masterplan**. The primary objective is to protect the **Occupancy Certificate** and ensure the **SCD NOC** remains valid.

The technical baseline must establish:
* **Approved Design Basis:** The original permitted use vs. current **SBC 201** classifications.
* **SBC 501 Trigger Points:** Any modification to fire compartments, travel distances, or suppression systems.
* **Authority Jurisdiction:** Identification if the project falls under standard **Baladiya (Municipality)** via SBPS, or a **Giga-Project Authority** (e.g., NEOM, Red Sea Global, Diriyah) which may have bespoke "Design Guidelines" alongside the SBC.

## 2. Technical Triggers for Authority Review
Any of the following necessitates a formal **Building Permit (Restoration/Renovation)** or **Change of Occupancy** filing:
* **Occupancy Reclassification:** e.g., converting Business (B) to Assembly (A-2) for a F&B unit, triggering higher occupant load factors and ventilation requirements.
* **Egress Geometry:** Any change to exit widths, travel distances, or the introduction of "dead-end" corridors beyond **SBC 501** limits.
* **Fire Engineering Systems:** Relocation of more than 5-10 sprinkler heads or any fire alarm device modification typically requires **SCD-licensed engineer** sign-off.
* **Structural Loading:** New heavy finishes (e.g., stone cladding/flooring) or mezzanine additions requiring **SBC 301/201** structural validation.
* **SBC 601 (Energy):** Alterations to the thermal envelope or significant lighting density increases.

## 3. The "Saudi-Ready" Submission Package
To avoid repetitive "Incomplete" status on the SBPS portal, the package must include:
* **Red/Blue/Black Layouts:** Clear demolition (Red), New (Blue), and Existing-to-Remain (Black) drawings.
* **SBC Compliance Matrix:** A concise table demonstrating adherence to relevant SBC chapters (notably **SBC 501 Chapter 10** for Means of Egress).
* **SCD Licensed Consultant Endorsement:** Required for all fire-related drawings and the **Fire Safety Strategy Note**.
* **Material Certificates:** Data sheets proving **UL/FM** listing or **SASO** compliance for all interior finishes (Flame Spread/Smoke Developed indices).
* **Asset Owner NOC:** Formal letter from the Landlord/Developer verifying the fit-out meets the base-build's MEP capacity and structural limits.

## 4. Strategic Delivery Options
| Feature | Option A: Interior Refresh (Fast-Track) | Option B: Major Retrofit / ChOO |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Scope** | Cosmetic, non-structural, no fire strategy change. | Structural mods, occupancy change, MEP overhaul. |
| **SBC Impact** | Minimal; focus on **SBC 601/1001**. | High; full **SBC 501/201** compliance required. |
| **Authority Route** | Simple renovation permit (Baladiya). | Full Building Permit / Change of Occupancy. |
| **SCD Involvement** | Maintenance-level notification. | Full Technical Review and Site Inspection. |
| **Recommendation** | Use for retail/office "plug-and-play" fit-outs. | Essential for repurposing older assets for Vision 2030. |

## 5. Commercial & Programme Realities
* **The "SCD Bottleneck":** Allow 4–6 weeks for SCD approvals in peak periods. Factor this into the "Long Lead" procurement for fire-rated doors and MEP equipment.
* **SBC 1001 (Accessibility):** Renovations often trigger "Path of Travel" upgrades to ensure the main entrance and restrooms are KSA-disability compliant.
* **Summer/Ramadan/Hajj Slumps:** Be mindful of site labor restrictions and authority working hours during these periods which can extend the "Handover & Defects" phase.
