# Handover Golden Thread (reference)

Use with `sa-practical-completion-snagging`.

## Evidence chain

- MAR → IR → test reports → O&M manuals
- SCD inspection records
- SBPS BCC / Is’har pathway documents

## Snag tiers

- **Type A:** Blocks TOC (life safety, weather-tightness, critical MEP)
- **Type B:** DLP cosmetic / minor items

## Contract touchpoints

- FIDIC TOC / performance certificate dates
- DLP duration and decennial liability awareness (advisory)
