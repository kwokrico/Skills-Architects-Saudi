# SCD Compliance & Safety License Closeout (KSA)

## 1. Project Context & Regulatory Baseline
To provide a precise compliance path, please confirm:
- **Authority Jurisdiction:** (e.g., Momraha/Baladiya via SBPS, NEOM Building Office, RCJY, or Red Sea Global).
- **Occupancy Classification:** Per **SBC 201/501** (e.g., High-rise Mall, Assembly, Residential Tower).
- **Current Milestone:** (e.g., Concept Fire Strategy, Civil Defence Preliminary Approval, Construction Stage, or Final Testing & Commissioning).
- **Technical Conflicts:** Any specific "Safety Observations" from SCD or the Third-Party Fire Consultant (OFEC).

## 2. The SCD "Fast-Track" Workflow (SBC 501 / NFPA)
In my experience, the "Golden Thread" in Saudi projects requires:
- **Approved Fire Strategy:** Ensuring the Architect of Record (AOR) and Fire Consultant have a stamped NOC on the **Fire Life Safety (FLS)** report before MEP procurement.
- **Material Compliance:** All passive and active systems (fire doors, dampers, intumescent paint, pumps) must have **SASO** certification and **SCD Approval Certificates**.
- **Integrated Testing (IST):** A clear **Cause & Effect Matrix** is the most common blocker—this must be locked before 80% construction completion.

## 3. Critical Handover & Occupancy Killers
We must proactively manage these "SCD Red Flags":
- **Passive Fire Integrity:** Unsealed MEP penetrations or non-compliant fire-stopping in shafts (SBC 501, Chapter 7).
- **Egress Deviations:** Site changes to travel distances or exit widths not reflected in the **As-Built** amendment via SBPS.
- **Smoke Management:** Failure of the smoke extract/pressurization systems to meet the specific air-change rates or pressure differentials during live SCD testing.
- **Documentation Gaps:** Missing **Civil Defence Contractor Licenses** for the installers or incomplete maintenance contracts (a prerequisite for the Safety License).

## 4. Required Documentation for Review
Please provide the following for a technical audit:
- **SCD-Stamped Fire Strategy** & Key Compartmentation/Egress Plans.
- **SBC 501 Compliance Statement** (prepared by the Fire Engineer).
- **Material Submittal Log** for life safety items (with SASO/SCD status).
- **Commissioning Schedule** & Integrated Systems Test (IST) procedures.
- **Current Civil Defence NOC** or recent inspection punch-list.
