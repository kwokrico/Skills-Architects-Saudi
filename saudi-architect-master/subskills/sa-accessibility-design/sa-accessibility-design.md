# KSA Accessibility Design (SBC 1001 & Universal Design)

## 1. Core Compliance Pillars
- **SBC 1001 Technical Rigor:** Ensuring all "Accessible Routes" meet the minimum clear width of 915mm and slope gradients (max 1:12 for ramps) per Chapter 4.
- **The "Continuous Path":** Prioritizing an uninterrupted accessible journey from the site boundary (parking/drop-off) through the "Principal Entrance" to all functional floor areas.
- **Sanitary Facilities (Chapter 6):** Precise coordination of lateral transfer spaces, grab bar heights (840mm-915mm), and turning circles ($1525mm$ diameter) for SBC-compliant WCs.
- **Vertical Circulation:** Ensuring lift car dimensions and call button placements align with both SBC 1001 and Saudi Civil Defence evacuation requirements.

## 2. Critical Saudi-Specific Challenges
- **Threshold Management:** Avoiding the "20mm trap" where floor finish transitions at balconies or entrances exceed SBC limits, often requiring recessed sub-slabs.
- **Extreme Climate Buffers:** Ensuring that sand-mitigation measures (vestibules/mats) do not impede the 450mm-600mm "pull-side" latch clearance for wheelchair users.
- **Wayfinding & Signage:** Integration of Arabic/English tactile and Braille signage as mandated by the Ministry of Municipalities and Rural Affairs and Housing (MoMRAH).
- **Public Realm Integration:** Coordinating R.O.W. (Right of Way) levels between the private plot and municipal sidewalks to avoid steep "boundary ramps."

## 3. Common Failure Modes in KSA Submissions
- **Value Engineering (VE):** Late-stage removal of automated door operators or high-spec tactile paving, leading to rejection during the Final Occupancy Certificate inspection.
- **M&E Conflicts:** Placing fire hose cabinets (FHC) or thermostats in locations that protrude into the 2050mm clear headroom or obstruct the accessible path.
- **Ramp Over-reliance:** Designing long ramps that meet the 1:12 gradient but lack the required 1525mm level landings every 9 meters of horizontal run.

## 4. Required Inputs for Review
- **Building Typology:** (e.g., Assembly, Business, Mercantile) to determine the "Scoping" requirements under SBC 1001 Chapter 2.
- **GA Plans & Site Plan:** Showing the path from the disabled parking stalls to the primary entrance.
- **Vertical Transport Strategy:** Lift schedules and stair nosing details.
- **Current Project Stage:** (Concept, Schematic, or Detailed Design for SBPS submission).
