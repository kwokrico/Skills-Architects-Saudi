# SBPS submission packaging (reference)

Use with `sa-op-submission-strategy`.

## Packaging principles

- One **Golden Thread** narrative across architecture, structure, fire, energy
- Discipline drawings share revision and sheet index
- Portal metadata matches physical drawing titles

## Phased permits

| Phase | Typical scope | Risk if skipped |
|-------|---------------|-----------------|
| Excavation / shoring | Early site works | Stop-work if full permit required |
| Preliminary / schematic | Massing freeze | Late redesign |
| Full building permit | IFC-level coordination | Occupancy delay |

## Special authorities

NEOM / RSG / DGDA may require supplemental design guidelines beyond SBC — confirm supplement list at kickoff.
