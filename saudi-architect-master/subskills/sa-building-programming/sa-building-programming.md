# KSA Building Programming (Briefing to SBC-Compliant Scope)

## 1. Core Approach: The "Saudi-Ready" Brief
- **Vision 2030 Alignment:** Mapping client GFA targets against Giga-project mandates (e.g., NEOM, Red Sea Global, or ROSHN design codes).
- **SBC 1001 & Universal Design:** Integrating Saudi accessibility requirements into the program flow at Day 1 to avoid late-stage spatial re-design.
- **Operational Logic:** Defining distinct Front-of-House (FOH) and Back-of-House (BOH) flows tailored to Saudi cultural norms, privacy requirements, and extreme climate logistics (e.g., cooled loading bays).
- **Regulatory Zoning:** Identifying "Use Groups" per SBC 201/501 early to lock in fire separation and egress strategies before Schematic Design.

## 2. Information Required for Analysis
- **Project Typology & Key Metrics:** Target keys (Hospitality), unit mix (Residential), or GLA (Retail/Commercial).
- **Authority Jurisdiction:** Is the site under MoMRAH (Baladiya), Royal Commission (RC), or a Giga-project authority (e.g., NEOM/Red Sea)?
- **Sustainability Targets:** Specific Mostadam (KSA) or LEED/WELL certification levels which impact spatial allowances for MEP and plant.
- **Site Constraints:** Existing NOCs or site-specific climatic challenges (e.g., high-salinity coastal vs. high-heat desert).

## 3. Deliverables & Commercial Management
- **SBC-Aligned SoA:** Detailed Schedule of Accommodation with GFA/BUA breakdowns prepared for SBPS portal entry.
- **Adjacency & Flow Matrices:** Validated BOH/FOH and service verticality.
- **Scope Change Log:** Tracking "Brief vs. Design" to manage variations under FIDIC Clause 13.
