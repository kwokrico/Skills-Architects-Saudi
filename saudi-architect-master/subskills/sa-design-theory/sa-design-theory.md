# KSA Design Leadership & Contextual Theory

## 1. Core Principles (The Saudi Context)
* **Cultural & Privacy Gradients:** Rigorous application of "Majlis" logic—managing the transition from public realm to semi-private and private family zones (vertical and horizontal separation).
* **Climatic Performance & "Mostadam" Alignment:** Designing for the "Thermal Year"—integrating passive cooling, shaded micro-climates, and SBC 601 (Energy Conservation) standards from Concept Stage.
* **The Salmani Architecture Charter:** Balancing tradition with innovation—authenticity, human-centricity, and livability as mandated for new developments in the Riyadh region.
* **Buildability vs. Ambition:** Ensuring iconic forms are rationalized for modularity or local GRC/Precast capabilities to survive aggressive fast-track schedules.

## 2. Design Direction & Value Engineering (VE) Filters
* **The "Golden Thread":** Does the design maintain its "10-second 'Why'" while navigating Saudi Civil Defence (SBC 501) and fire life safety constraints?
* **Value Driver Preservation:** Identifying "Non-Negotiables" (e.g., façade high-performance glass specs) versus "Optimizable" items (e.g., interior fit-out or non-structural landscape elements) during VE workshops.
* **Authority Alignment:** Is the massing optimized for Riyadh/Jeddah Municipality (Baladiya) setbacks and FAR (Floor Area Ratio) regulations?

## 3. Critical Inputs for Synthesis
* **Location & Micro-climate:** (e.g., Riyadh’s dry heat vs. Jeddah’s humidity vs. NEOM’s topography).
* **Regulatory Framework:** (e.g., Royal Commission, MODON, or specific Giga-Project Internal Codes).
* **Procurement Strategy:** (e.g., Design-Build, EPC, or Traditional) to determine the level of detail required at the current stage.
* **Target Sustainability Rating:** (Mostadam Green Building or LEED requirements).
