# KSA Plan of Work — Stages 0–7 Checklists

Use with `sa-plan-of-work`. Confirm AHJ, occupancy, and code edition before gate sign-off.

---

## Stage 0 — Strategic Definition

**KSA focus:** Confirm primary AHJ (Baladiya/SBPS vs NEOM/RSG/DGDA/RC/MODON).

| Input | Output | Owner |
|-------|--------|-------|
| Client brief, plot ref (Krooki) | Strategic brief | Client / PM |
| Land grant / lease conditions | AHJ & land constraint memo | `sa-lease-compliance` |
| Feasibility massing | Options appraisal | Architect |
| PI / appointment scope | Stage 0 gate minutes | Lead consultant |

**Gate:** Proceed when AHJ, occupancy hypothesis, and procurement route are documented.

---

## Stage 1 — Preparation & Brief

| Input | Output | Owner |
|-------|--------|-------|
| SOA / area schedule | `sa-building-programming` baseline | Architect |
| Code basis (SBC parts) | Compliance strategy outline | `sa-building-codes` |
| Procurement route | Route memo | `sa-procurement-strategy` |
| Consultant RACI | Appointment schedule | `sa-project-management` |

**Gate:** Brief frozen; exclusions and additional services listed (`sa-fee-proposal-strategy`).

## Stage 2 — Concept Design

| Input | Output | Owner |
|-------|--------|-------|
| Masterplan / FAR envelope | Massing options | `sa-concept-design` |

**Gate:** Preliminary design approval or equivalent client gateway; massing locked unless DCN.

## Stage 3 — Spatial Coordination (Schematic)

**Gate:** Schematic issue pack (`sa-deliverables-workstages`); SCD in-principle meeting scheduled.

## Stage 6 — Handover & Close Out (Construction End)

| Input | Output | Owner |
|-------|--------|-------|
| Site inspection records | Supervision reports | `sa-site-supervision` |
| Snag lists | Zone punch lists | `sa-practical-completion-snagging` |
| IST / commissioning records | SCD test index | `sa-scd-licensing-compliance` |
| As-built vs approved | Deviation log | `sa-alterations-additions` |

**Gate:** Contractual PC; authority inspections scheduled; utilities energization plan.

---

## Stage 7 — In Use (Defects & Occupancy)

| Input | Output | Owner |
|-------|--------|-------|
| DLP defect log | Making-good schedule | `sa-practical-completion-snagging` |
| BCC / Is'har prerequisites | Closeout index | `sa-certificate-of-compliance` |
| O&M manuals | Handover binders | `sa-project-management` |
| Final account | QS closeout | `sa-cost-consultancy` |

**Gate:** Safety License issued; occupancy certificate pathway complete; final certificate chain documented.

---

## Fast-track overlap note

On giga-projects, Stages 3–5 often run concurrently. Document **hold points** where SBC 501 or SCD NOC must not be bypassed.
