# ICC A117.1 Chapter 07 Communication Elements and Features (2017)
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
2017 | ICC

> Scope note: Chapter 7 is technical criteria for alarms, signs, telephones, detectable warnings, assistive listening, ATMs/fare machines, and two-way communication **where the administrative authority requires them** (701.1). What to sign and how many ALS receivers are **SBC 201 Chapter 11 / 1112**. SBC commentary often leaves A117.1 §704 telephones and §707 ATMs to **SBC 201 Appendix E** — do not invent those exceptions into the tables. SBC 201 Chapter 35 lists **ICC A117.1—09**.

## Regulatory Overview
This chapter does not classify occupancy or construction type. Fire-alarm audible/visible appliances follow **NFPA 72-2016** (702.1); dwelling/sleeping-unit devices follow 1106.2–1106.4.4 instead.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Alarms (702)
Audible and visible appliances that are part of a building fire alarm system: NFPA 72-2016; commercial power; permanently connected and installed (702.1).

**SD takeaway:** Coordinate fire-alarm strobes/speakers with the fire consultant to NFPA 72-2016; do not use Chapter 7 as the dwelling-unit notification spec — that is 1106.

### 2. Signs — visual, tactile, location (703)
Tactile signs: raised characters **and** braille (703.1). Permanent room/space IDs: 703.1 + 703.2 + 703.3 (exterior signs not at the door skip 703.3). Directional/informational: visual 703.2 only. Pictogram designations of permanent rooms: 703.5 plus text descriptors below (ISA and similar excepted).

| Item | Rule | Section |
|------|------|---------|
| Visual character min height | Per Table 703.2.4 vs height AFF and viewing distance (e.g. 40–70 in AFF, < 6 ft: ⅝ in (16 mm)) | 703.2.4 |
| Visual min AFF | 40 in (1015 mm) to baseline (elevator car controls excepted) | 703.2.9 |
| Finish | Nonglare; ≤ 19 gloss units (60° meter); light-on-dark or dark-on-light | 703.2.10 |
| Raised height | ⅝–2 in (16–51 mm); ½ in (13 mm) min if separate visual sign | 703.3.5 |
| Raised / braille AFF | Lowest raised baseline 48 in (1220 mm) min; highest 60 in (1525 mm) max; braille baseline 48–60 in (1220–1525 mm) | 703.3.10 / 703.4.5 |
| Tactile at door | Latch side; inactive leaf if one active; right of right-hand leaf if two active; 18×18 in (455×455 mm) CFS centered on raised characters, beyond swing to 45° | 703.3.11 |
| Braille | Grade 2 (contracted) | 703.4.1 |
| ISA | International Symbol of Accessibility where required by 703.6 | 703.6.3.1 |

**SD takeaway:** Reserve latch-side wall for a 455×455 mm tactile-sign CFS at 1220–1525 mm AFF on every permanent room; do not put the only ID in the door swing.

### 3. Detectable warnings (705)
Standardized within the site (705.2); visual contrast (705.3); interior surfaces differ in resiliency or cane sound (705.4).

| Truncated dome | Dimension | Section |
|----------------|-----------|---------|
| Base diameter | 0.9–1.4 in (23–36 mm) | 705.5.1 |
| Top diameter | 50–65% of base | 705.5.1 |
| Height | 0.2 in (5.1 mm) | 705.5.2 |
| Center-to-center | 1.6–2.4 in (41–61 mm) | 705.5.3 |
| Base-to-base | 0.65 in (16.5 mm) min | 705.5.3 |
| Depth in travel | 24 in (610 mm) min | 705.6 |
| Width | Full curb-ramp run (no flares); full rail crossing / platform / stop length as listed | 705.6 |

Locations that require them when provided on pedestrian access routes are in 406.6.2 (curb ramps, islands, rail crossings, unprotected transit platforms).

**SD takeaway:** Specify 610 mm-deep truncated-dome bands at every scoped curb ramp and unprotected platform edge — do not substitute scored concrete.

### 4. Telephones, ALS, ATMs, two-way comms (704, 706–708)
| Element | SD lock | Section |
|---------|---------|---------|
| Wheelchair telephone | CFS 704.2; operable parts 309; cord length specified in 704.2.4 | 704.2 |
| Volume-control / TTY | 704.3–704.6 | 704 |
| ALS | ⅛ in (3.2 mm) mono jack; induction loops IEC 60118.4-2014; hearing-aid compatibility via neck loops; 110–118 dB SPL, 50 dB volume range, 18 dB min S/N | 706 |
| ATM / fare | CFS in front (not drive-up-only); 309; 12-key layout with raised dot on 5; speech output + braille start instructions | 707 |
| Two-way comms | Visual **and** audible signals; handset cord 29 in (735 mm) min; telephone entry ANSI/DASMA 303-2006 | 708 |

**SD takeaway:** If the AHJ scopes ALS or ATMs, lock a 305 CFS at the device and an IEC 60118-4 loop or neck-loop receivers — quantities still come from SBC 201 Ch 11 / Appendix E.
