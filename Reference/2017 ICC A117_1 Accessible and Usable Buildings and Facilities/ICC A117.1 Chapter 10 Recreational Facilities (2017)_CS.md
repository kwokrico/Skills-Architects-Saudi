# ICC A117.1 Chapter 10 Recreational Facilities (2017)
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
2017 | ICC

> Scope note: Chapter 10 is technical criteria for recreation **the administrative authority requires to be accessible** (1001.1). Which pools, play areas, and rides are required is **SBC 201 Chapter 11 / 1111**. The extract’s user note: Chapters 10 and 11 swapped placement versus older editions — cite **2017** section numbers as in these files (1009 pools, not a Chapter 11 pool section). SBC 201 Chapter 35 lists **ICC A117.1—09**.

## Regulatory Overview
This chapter does not classify occupancy or construction type. Areas of sport activity must be served by an accessible route but need not be accessible except as this chapter states; 302/303 do not apply inside those exempted sport areas (1001.2.2).

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. What is not on an accessible route (1001.2.1)
The following shall not be required to comply with this standard or to be on an accessible route: raised referee/judge/scoring structures; water slides; non-public animal containment; raised boxing or wrestling rings; raised diving boards and platforms; bowling lanes not required to provide wheelchair spaces; mobile/portable amusement rides; rider-controlled rides; children’s rides where an adult assists on and off; rides without seats; shooting positions on free-standing platforms ≥ 12 ft (3660 mm) above grade if aggregate elevated firing area ≤ 500 ft² (46 m²).

Protruding-object 307 applies on circulation paths except inside sport-activity areas, and inside play areas if ground-level ARs meet 1008.2 vertical clearance (1001.3).

**SD takeaway:** Run an AR to the sport venue and pool deck; do not try to make the slide tower, diving board, or boxing ring accessible under this chapter.

### 2. Play areas (1008)
At least one AR in the play area connecting required ground-level and elevated components (1008.2.1). Soft contained play: 1 of ≤3 entries, or 2 of ≥4 entries, on an AR (1008.2.2). Each age-separated play area complies (1008.3). At least one of each ground-level type on an AR (1008.3.2.1.1); additional ground-level types per Table 1008.3.2.1.2 when elevated components exist (unless ≥50% of elevated are ramp-connected and ≥3 types). **≥50% of elevated components** on an AR (1008.3.2.2).

| Play AR | Clear width | Section |
|---------|-------------|---------|
| Ground level | 60 in (1525 mm) min; 44 in (1120 mm) if play area < 1000 ft² (93 m²) with a 304.3 turn if the restricted route > 30 ft (9145 mm); 36 in (915 mm) for 60 in (1525 mm) max if 60×60 in separators | 1008.4.1.1.1 |
| Elevated | 36 in (915 mm) min; 32 in (815 mm) for 24 in (610 mm) max with 48 in (1220 mm) separators; transfer systems 24 in (610 mm) min | 1008.4.1.1.2 |
| Headroom (ground) | 80 in (2030 mm) min | 1008.4.1 |

Use zones / surfacing: ASTM F 1487-01 and ASTM F 1292-13 as referenced in Ch 1. Transfer to play: entry 11–24 in (280–610 mm) from CFS (slides excepted) plus a transfer support (1008.4.3.4–1008.4.3.5).

**SD takeaway:** Hold the ground play loop at **1525 mm** (or 1120 mm only under 93 m²) and put **half of elevated components** on a ramp or transfer system — do not specify a single token accessible swing.

### 3. Pools, spas, wading (1009)
| Facility | Means of entry | Section |
|----------|----------------|---------|
| Swimming pool | **Two** accessible means; at least one a **lift (1009.2) or sloped entry (1009.3)**; others may be transfer wall, transfer system, or pool stairs | 1009.1.1 |
| Pool wall < 300 lf (91 m) | One means permitted | 1009.1.1 Exc. 1 |
| Wave / leisure river / sand-bottom / single-access | One means: lift, sloped entry, or transfer system | 1009.1.1 Exc. 2 |
| Catch pool | No entry if the edge is on an AR | 1009.1.1 Exc. 3 |
| Wading pool | At least one sloped entry | 1009.1.2 |
| Hot tub / spa | One means: lift, transfer wall, or transfer system; clusters: 5% but not less than one | 1009.1.3 |

**Pool lift:** water ≤ 48 in (1220 mm) unless entire pool is deeper; seat centerline over deck ≥ 16 in (405 mm) from pool edge; seat 16–19 in (405–485 mm) AFF in raised position, 16 in (405 mm) min wide, backrest; **new** clear deck 36×52 in (915×1320 mm), **existing** 36×48 in (915×1220 mm); unassisted from deck and water; submerge 18 in (455 mm) min; capacity 300 lb (136 kg) min (1009.2).

**Sloped entry:** 402/403/405 (surface not required to be slip resistant); swimming-pool run to 24–30 in (610–760 mm) below water, with a 405.7 landing in that band; two 505 handrails **33–38 in (840–965 mm)** apart (wading: handrails not required) (1009.3).

**Transfer wall:** 60×60 in (1525×1525 mm) clear deck at the base, slope ≤ 1:48 (1009.4.1).

**SD takeaway:** A new swimming pool needs **two** entries with at least one lift or sloped entry; budget **915×1320 mm** lift deck or a 1:12 ramp to 610–760 mm water depth with 840–965 mm between handrails.
