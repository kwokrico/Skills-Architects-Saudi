# ICC A117.1 Chapter 06 Plumbing Elements and Facilities (2017)
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
2017 | ICC

> Scope note: Chapter 6 is technical criteria for plumbing elements the administrative authority requires to be accessible (601.1). Counts of accessible WCs, stalls, showers, and fountains are **SBC 201 Chapter 11**. New-building transfer-shower clearance in this 2017 chapter uses the 52 in (1320 mm) CFS module; SBC 201 Chapter 35 lists **ICC A117.1—09** — do not treat 2017 sizes as automatically the legal SBC minimum.

## Regulatory Overview
This chapter does not classify occupancy or construction type; it sizes toilet rooms, fixtures, showers, and grab bars once they are scoped. Children’s-use rows apply only where the space is primarily for people 12 and younger (604.11); otherwise use adult dimensions.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Toilet and bathing rooms (603)
| Item | Rule | Section |
|------|------|---------|
| Turning space | Required **in the room**, not inside a toilet compartment | 603.2.1 |

| Door swing exceptions | Private-office single-occupant if swing reversible; or individual-use room with 305.3 CFS beyond the door arc | 603.2.2 |
| Mirror over 606 lavatory or non-lav counter | Bottom of reflecting surface 40 in (1015 mm) max AFF | 603.3 |
| Alternate mirror (not in Accessible units) | Bottom 35 in (890 mm) max AFF if provided elsewhere in the same room | 603.3 Exc. |
| Coat hooks | Within 308 reach | 603.4 |
| Shelves | 40–48 in (1015–1220 mm) AFF | 603.4 |
| Diaper changing | 309 + 902 | 603.5 |
| Towel/dryer reach vs depth | 0.5 in → 48 in (1220 mm); 2 in → 46 in (1170 mm); 5 in → 42 in (1065 mm); 6 in → 40 in (1015 mm); 9 in → 36 in (915 mm); 11 in → 34 in (865 mm) | Table 603.6 |

**SD takeaway:** Size the accessible toilet room for a **Ch 3 turning space outside the stall** and keep the entry door off fixture clearances (or provide a 305.3 CFS beyond the swing).

**Wheelchair stall (604.9):** 60 in (1525 mm) min width × 56 in (1420 mm) min depth (wall-hung WC) or 59 in (1500 mm) min depth (floor-mounted); children’s stall 60×59 in (1525×1500 mm); **alternate** 60×84 in (1525×2135 mm). Door 404, self-closing, pulls both sides; door shall not swing into the minimum stall area (exceptions for latch-side 42 in (1065 mm) outside, and alternate stall with 305.3 CFS beyond swing). Toe clearance 12 in (305 mm) AFF extending 8 in (205 mm) beyond partition (waived if stall deeper/wider than 64/67/68 in as listed).

**Ambulatory stall (604.10):** 60 in (1525 mm) min depth × **35–37 in (890–940 mm)** width; grab bars both sides.

**SD takeaway:** Plan the accessible stall as **1525 × 1420/1500 mm** plus 404 door clearances; do not swing the door into that rectangle unless using the 2135 mm alternate.

### 3. Urinals, lavatories, drinking fountains (605–606, 602)
| Element | Hard rule | Section |
|---------|-----------|---------|
| Urinal | Stall type **or** wall-hung rim ≤ 17 in (430 mm) AFF; depth 13½ in (345 mm) min; forward CFS; flush 309 | 605 |
| Lavatory/sink | Forward CFS 305.3 + knee/toe 306; rim/counter 34 in (865 mm) max AFF; faucets 309 (metering 10 s min); pipes insulated | 606.2–606.6 |
| Enhanced-reach lav | Faucet/soap reach depth 11 in (280 mm) max | 606.5 |
| Wheelchair fountain | Forward CFS centered + 306; spout ≤ 36 in (915 mm) AFF (children ≤ 30 in (760 mm)); spout 15 in (380 mm) min from support, 5 in (125 mm) max from front | 602.2 |
| Standing fountain | Spout 38–43 in (965–1090 mm) AFF | 602.3 |
| Bottle filler | Forward or side CFS; controls hand-operated 309 or automatic | 602.4 |

Parallel approach allowed at kitchen sink without cooktop/range, wet bars, and some children’s lavs (606.2 Exc.).

**SD takeaway:** Pair wheelchair and standing fountains; hold accessible lavatories at **865 mm** rim with forward 760×1320 mm CFS and knee space.

**SD takeaway:** Coordinate blocking for 1112 N grab bars at every scoped WC/tub/shower; laundry doors in the 380–915 mm band with 305.3 CFS.
