# ICC A117.1 Chapter 03 Building Blocks (2017)
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
2017 | ICC

> Scope note: Chapter 3 is the dimensional module for turning space, clear floor space, level change, protrusions, reach, and operable parts. It applies where the administrative authority requires accessibility or where Chapters 4–11 invoke it (301.1). It does **not** say how many elements are required — that is **SBC 201 Chapter 11**. New-building sizes in this 2017 chapter are larger than the 2009 edition listed as **ICC A117.1—09** in SBC 201 Chapter 35; do not treat 2017 numbers as automatically the legal SBC minimum.

## Regulatory Overview
This chapter does not classify occupancy or construction type; it supplies adult anthropometric criteria (children only where a later section so notes). Use new-building dimensions for new work and existing-building dimensions only where the AHJ treats the facility as existing (304.3, 305.3).

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Surfaces and level change (302–303)
| Condition | Limit | Section |
|-----------|-------|---------|
| Floor | Stable, firm, slip resistant | 302.1 |
| Carpet pile | ½ in (13 mm) max; edges trimmed; comply with 303 | 302.2 |
| Openings | Sphere ½ in (13 mm) shall not pass; elongated openings perpendicular to travel | 302.3 |
| Vertical change | ¼ in (6.4 mm) max | 303.2 |
| Beveled | > ¼ in (6.4 mm) and ≤ ½ in (13 mm); slope ≤ 1:2 | 303.3 |
| Greater than ½ in (13 mm) | Ramp 405 or curb ramp 406 | 303.4 |
| Overlap | Clear floor spaces, fixture clearances, door maneuvering, and turning spaces may overlap unless otherwise specified | 301.2 |

**SD takeaway:** Any finish step over ½ in (13 mm) is a ramp; hold interior thresholds and paving joints to ¼ in / ½ in before schematic grading.

### 2. Turning space — new vs existing (304)
Turning space floor: 302; no level change except slope ≤ 1:48 (304.2). Doors may swing into turning spaces unless otherwise specified (304.4).

| Type | New buildings | Existing buildings |
|------|---------------|-------------------|
| Circular | 67 in (1700 mm) min diameter | 60 in (1525 mm) min diameter |
| Circular overlap under object | Knee/toe 306; overlap depth ≤ 10 in (255 mm) and not more than the clearance provided; only in the shaded sector of Fig. 304.3.1.1 | Knee/toe 306 permitted (no 10 in cap in 304.3.1.2.1) |
| T-shaped | One of three unobstructed options: (1) 68×60 in (1725×1525 mm) with 36 in (915 mm) arms/base and 8 in (205 mm) chamfers; (2) 64×60 in (1625×1525 mm) with 38 in (965 mm) arms and 42 in (1065 mm) base; (3) 64×60 in (1625×1525 mm) with 40 in (1015 mm) arms and base | 60 in (1525 mm) min square; arms and base 36 in (915 mm) min |

**SD takeaway:** Plan new rooms on a **1700 mm** turning circle (or the matching T); 1525 mm is existing-only.

### 3. Clear floor space and alcoves (305–306)
| Item | New | Existing | Section |
|------|-----|----------|---------|
| Clear floor space | 52 in (1320 mm) length × 30 in (760 mm) width | 48 in (1220 mm) × 30 in (760 mm) | 305.3 |
| Floor in CFS | 302; no level change except slope ≤ 1:48 | same | 305.2 |
| Approach | One full unobstructed side adjoins AR or another CFS | same | 305.6 |
| Alcove, parallel, depth > 15 in (380 mm) | 60 in (1525 mm) min width | same | 305.7.1 |
| Alcove, forward, depth > 24 in (610 mm) | 36 in (915 mm) min width | same | 305.7.2 |

Knee and toe (306), if counted as CFS or turning space:

| Zone | Height | Depth | Width |
|------|--------|-------|-------|
| Toe | Floor to 9 in (230 mm) | 17 in (430 mm) min when required; 25 in (635 mm) max | 30 in (760 mm) min |
| Knee | 9 in (230 mm) to 27 in (685 mm) | 11 in (280 mm) min at 9 in; 8 in (205 mm) min at 27 in; 25 in (635 mm) max at 9 in | 30 in (760 mm) min |

**SD takeaway:** New fixture, appliance, and control positions need a **760 × 1320 mm** CFS, not the older 760 × 1220 mm.

### 4. Protruding objects and headroom (307)
| Rule | Dimension | Section |
|------|-----------|---------|
| Leading edge 27–80 in (685–2030 mm) AFF | 4 in (100 mm) max horizontal into circulation path | 307.2 |
| Handrails | 4½ in (115 mm) max | 307.2 Exc. |
| Post-mounted object | 4 in (100 mm) max overhang in that height band; if posts > 12 in (305 mm) apart, lowest edge ≤ 27 in (685 mm) or ≥ 80 in (2030 mm) | 307.3 |
| Vertical clearance | 80 in (2030 mm) min; if less, barrier with leading edge ≤ 27 in (685 mm) AFF | 307.4 |
| Door closers/stops | 78 in (1980 mm) min AFF | 307.4 Exc. |
| Clear width | Protrusions shall not reduce required AR width | 307.5 |

**SD takeaway:** Keep wall-mounted objects either below 685 mm, above 2030 mm, or ≤ 100 mm off the wall; hold soffits at 2030 mm.

### 5. Reach ranges and operable parts (308–309)
| Reach | High | Low | Notes |
|-------|------|-----|-------|
| Unobstructed forward | 48 in (1220 mm) max | 15 in (380 mm) min | 308.2.1 |
| Obstructed forward | 48 in (1220 mm) if reach depth ≤ 20 in (510 mm); 44 in (1120 mm) if depth > 20 in and ≤ 25 in (635 mm) | — | CFS + knee/toe under element |
| Unobstructed side (CFS edge ≤ 10 in (255 mm) from element) | 48 in (1220 mm) max | 15 in (380 mm) min | Existing unaltered elements 54 in (1370 mm) max; existing-curb fuel dispensers 54 in (1370 mm) max |
| Obstructed side | Obstruction ≤ 34 in (865 mm) high and ≤ 24 in (610 mm) deep; high reach 48 in (1220 mm) at ≤ 10 in (255 mm) depth, 46 in (1170 mm) at ≤ 24 in (610 mm) depth | — | 308.3.2 |

Operable parts (309): CFS 305; height in 308; one hand, no tight grasp/pinch/wrist twist; **5.0 lb (22.2 N) max** (gas pump nozzles excepted from the force limit). Dedicated receptacles, floor outlets, HVAC diffusers, ceiling-fan controls, some kitchen receptacles, panelboards (force), and emergency-personnel devices are excepted as listed in 309.1.

**SD takeaway:** Put switches, card readers, and hardware in the **380–1220 mm** band with a 760 × 1320 mm CFS in front.
