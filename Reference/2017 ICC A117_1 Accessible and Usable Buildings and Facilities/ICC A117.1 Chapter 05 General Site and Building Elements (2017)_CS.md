# ICC A117.1 Chapter 05 General Site and Building Elements (2017)
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
2017 | ICC

> Scope note: Chapter 5 is technical criteria for parking, passenger loading, stairs, handrails, operable windows, and accessible routes through parking **where the administrative authority requires them** (501.1). Counts of accessible spaces are **SBC 201 Chapter 11** (and 1106 van ratio). SBC commentary often leaves A117.1 §504 stairways and §507 parking routes unscoped — do not invent those exceptions into the tables below. SBC 201 Chapter 35 lists **ICC A117.1—09**.

## Regulatory Overview
This chapter does not classify occupancy or construction type and does not require parking or loading zones to be provided. Where those elements (or accessible stairs/handrails) are scoped, the dimensions in this chapter apply, including new-vs-existing loading-aisle width (503.3.2).

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Parking spaces (502)
Applies to lot spaces (502.2–502.8); on-street (502.9–502.10); EV charging at a parking space (502.11).

| Item | Car | Van | Section |
|------|-----|-----|---------|
| Space width | 96 in (2440 mm) min | 132 in (3355 mm) min; **or** 96 in (2440 mm) if aisle is 96 in (2440 mm) min | 502.2 |
| Access aisle width | 60 in (1525 mm) min; full length of space; may be shared by two spaces; not in the vehicular way | same | 502.4 |
| Angled van aisle | Passenger side of the space | 502.4.1 |
| Slope | ≤ 1:48; aisle same level as space | 502.5 |
| Vertical clearance | — | 98 in (2490 mm) at van spaces, their aisles, and the vehicular route to them | 502.6 |
| Sign height | ISA; van signs say “van accessible”; 60 in (1525 mm) min AFF to bottom of sign | 502.7 |
| Parked vehicles | Shall not obstruct required AR width | 502.8 |

On-street parallel: if sidewalk/ROW > 14 ft (4265 mm), 60 in (1525 mm) street-level aisle; if ≤ 14 ft (4265 mm), no aisle but space at end of block face (502.9.1). Perpendicular/angled on-street: 96 in (2440 mm) aisle (502.9.2). Meters/pay stations: operable parts 309; parallel meters at head or foot of space (502.10).

**EV charging (502.11):** user operable parts (including card readers) 309; AR from the parking access aisle to the CFS at the charger; charging cable shall not obstruct the AR; bollards/curbs/wheel stops shall not eat the CFS or AR.

**SD takeaway:** Module each accessible stall as **2440 mm + 1525 mm aisle** (shared aisle OK); van as **3355 mm + 1525 mm** or **2440 + 2440**; keep **2490 mm** van headroom on one route through the garage.

### 2. Passenger loading zones (503)
| Item | New | Existing | Section |
|------|-----|----------|---------|
| Pull-up space | 96 in (2440 mm) wide × 20 ft (6095 mm) long | same | 503.2 |
| Access aisle width | 67 in (1700 mm) min | 60 in (1525 mm) min | 503.3.2 |
| Aisle length / marking | Full length of pull-up; marked to discourage parking | same | 503.3.3–503.3.4 |
| Slope | ≤ 1:48; aisle same level | same | 503.4 |
| Vertical clearance | 114 in (2895 mm) at space, aisle, inbound and outbound vehicular routes | same | 503.5 |

**SD takeaway:** A new drop-off needs **1700 mm** aisle and **2895 mm** clear height from porte-cochère to exit — larger than the parking van envelope.

### 3. Stairways (504)
| Item | Rule | Section |
|------|------|---------|
| Risers | Uniform; 4–7 in (100–180 mm) | 504.2 |
| Treads | Uniform; 11 in (280 mm) min depth; slope ≤ 1:48 | 504.2 / 504.4 |
| Open risers | Not permitted | 504.3 |
| Nosing projection | 1½ in (38 mm) max; radius or 45° bevel ≤ ½ in (13 mm) | 504.5 |
| Contrast | Leading 1–2 in (25–51 mm) of every tread/landing, or adopted-code warning marking | 504.6 |
| Handrails | 505 | 504.7 |
| Interior lighting | 1 fc (10.8 lux) unused; 10 fc (108 lux) in use; motion sensors fail-safe, 15 min min | 504.9.1 |
| Tactile in enclosure | Floor ID at each landing adjacent to corridor door; discharge door “EXIT” | 504.10 |
| Tactile at exits | “EXIT” at doors to area of refuge, exterior assisted rescue, exit stair/ramp/passage, exit discharge | 504.11 |

**SD takeaway:** If this chapter is scoped, lock **180 mm max riser / 280 mm min tread**, closed risers, and stair-width for 505 handrails on both sides.

### 4. Handrails (505)
Required by 405.8 (ramps), 504.6 (stairs), 1009.3.3 (pool sloped entries), 1009.6.2 (pool stairs).

| Item | Rule | Section |
|------|------|---------|
| Sides | Both sides of stairs and ramps (assembly aisle exceptions) | 505.2 |
| Height | 34–38 in (865–965 mm) above nosing/ramp/walk; consistent | 505.4 |
| Clearance to wall | 1½ in (38 mm) min | 505.5 |
| Circular OD | 1¼–2 in (32–51 mm) | 505.7.1 |
| Noncircular | Perimeter 4–6¼ in (100–160 mm); cross-section ≤ 2¼ in (57 mm) | 505.7.2 |
| Ramp extensions | 12 in (305 mm) horizontal beyond top and bottom of run | 505.10.1 |
| Stair top extension | 12 in (305 mm) horizontal above landing | 505.10.2 |
| Stair bottom extension | Horizontal distance = one tread depth beyond bottom nosing, at the stair slope | 505.10.3 |

**SD takeaway:** Add **305 mm** handrail extensions to ramp and stair landings in schematic; keep 38 mm clear to finishes.

### 5. Windows and routes through parking (506–507)
Operable windows in an accessible room: at least one complies with 506; if a window is required for ventilation or emergency escape, **that** window is the accessible one (506.1). Opening/closing force: 8.5 lb (37.7 N) max sliding; 5 lb (22.2 N) max other types; lock/latch parts 309; test AAMA 513-14 (506.2). Employee-only, Type A unit windows under 1103.13, and operable skylights excepted.

Accessible routes through parking facilities shall be physically separated from vehicular traffic, except when crossing drive aisles, or the short route from 502/503 spaces to accessible entrances (507.1).

**SD takeaway:** Do not run the site AR down a drive aisle as the primary path; separate it, and put reachable hardware on the required escape/ventilation window.
