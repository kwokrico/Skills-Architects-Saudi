# ICC A117.1 Chapter 09 Furnishings and Equipment (2017)
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
2017 | ICC

> Scope note: Chapter 9 is technical criteria for dining/work surfaces, benches, sales/service counters, storage, wheelchair charging outlets, and gaming machines **where the administrative authority requires them** (901.1). How many accessible counters or tables is **SBC 201 Chapter 11**. Drive-up-only counters are outside this chapter (904.1 Exc.).

## Regulatory Overview
This chapter does not classify occupancy or construction type. It sizes the accessible portion of furniture and counters once they are scoped; kitchen cabinets are not storage under 905 (905.1 Exc.).

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Dining and work surfaces (902)
| Item | Adult | Children’s use | Section |
|------|-------|----------------|---------|
| Approach | Forward CFS + knee/toe 306 | Forward CFS + 306; knee 24 in (610 mm) min AFF permitted | 902.2 / 902.5.1 |
| Height | 28–34 in (710–865 mm) AFF to top | 26–30 in (660–760 mm) AFF | 902.4 / 902.5.2 |
| Parallel allowed | Drink surfaces ≤ 12 in (305 mm) deep (no 306 beyond that depth); dining 15–24 in (380–610 mm) high | Ages 5 and younger: parallel CFS only, no 902.5 | 902.2 Exc. / 902.5 Exc. |

No sharp/abrasive surfaces under exposed tops (902.3).

**SD takeaway:** Hold accessible tables and work counters at **710–865 mm** with a forward 760×1320 mm CFS and 306 knee space.

### 2. Benches (903)
| Item | Rule | Section |
|------|------|---------|
| CFS | Parallel to the short axis, at the end of the seat | 903.2 |
| Seat | 42 in (1065 mm) min length; 20–24 in (510–610 mm) depth | 903.3 |
| Height | 17–19 in (430–485 mm) AFF; children’s use 11–17 in (280–430 mm) | 903.5 |
| Back | Wall-affixed or back support 42 in (1065 mm) min long, from ≤ 2 in (51 mm) above seat to ≥ 18 in (455 mm) above seat; ≤ 2½ in (64 mm) from rear edge | 903.4 |
| Strength | 250 lb (1112 N) | 903.6 |
| Wet | Slip resistant; shall not accumulate water | 903.7 |

**SD takeaway:** A 903 bench is a **1065 mm**-long seat at 430–485 mm AFF with an end CFS — locker-room “benches” that are only 300 mm deep fail.

### 3. Sales and service counters (904)
Accessible portion adjacent to a 403 walking surface (904.2). Accessible countertop same depth as the standing public top (904.3). Vertical barrier at windows/counters ≤ 43 in (1090 mm) AFF (transparent security glazing may go higher) (904.3.1).

| Approach | Length | Height | Clearance above | Section |
|----------|--------|--------|-----------------|---------|
| Parallel | 36 in (915 mm) min (or full top if shorter) | 26–36 in (660–915 mm) | 12 in (305 mm) min to objects above | 904.3.2 |
| Forward | 30 in (760 mm) min | 36 in (915 mm) max | 12 in (305 mm) min; knee/toe 306 | 904.3.3 |

Checkout: aisle 403; counter 38 in (965 mm) max AFF; edge protection 2 in (51 mm) max above the top on the aisle side (904.4). Food-service tray slides 28–34 in (710–865 mm); self-service shelves 308 (904.5). Security glazing: a voice method; handsets if provided 704.3 (904.6). Alteration exception: 24 in (610 mm) min length at 904.3.2 if a full counter would cut workstation or mailbox count (904.3 Exc.).

**SD takeaway:** Provide either a **915 mm**-long 660–915 mm parallel service top or a **760 mm** forward 306 knee-space top at every scoped counter; keep glass/talk-through at **1090 mm**.

### 4. Storage, charging, gaming (905–907)
| Element | Rule | Section |
|---------|------|---------|
| Built-in storage | CFS; at least one 308 reach range; operable parts 309 | 905 |
| Charging station | Grounded duplex; CFS; 308 reach | 906 |
| Gaming machines/tables | CFS for transfer or seated wheelchair use; CFS may overlap | 907.1 |

**SD takeaway:** Put at least one locker/shelf of each type in the 380–1220 mm reach band with a 305 CFS; wheelchair charging is a duplex in that same band (used again at assembly spaces and Accessible-unit beds).
