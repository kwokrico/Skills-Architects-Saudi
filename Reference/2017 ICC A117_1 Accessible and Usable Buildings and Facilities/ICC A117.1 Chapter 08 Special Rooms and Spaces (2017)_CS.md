# ICC A117.1 Chapter 08 Special Rooms and Spaces (2017)
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
2017 | ICC

> Scope note: Chapter 8 is technical criteria for assembly seating, dressing/locker rooms, kitchens, transportation facilities, cells, courtrooms, and classroom acoustics **where the administrative authority requires them** (801.1). How many wheelchair spaces and which kitchens are **SBC 201 Chapter 11**. SBC commentary often leaves A117.1 §802.11 interpreter stations unscoped. SBC 201 Chapter 35 lists **ICC A117.1—09**.

## Regulatory Overview
This chapter does not classify occupancy or construction type. New-building wheelchair-space depth uses the 52 in (1320 mm) module; existing buildings may use 48 in (1220 mm) for front/rear entry (802.4). Quantity of locations is Table 802.10 once the AHJ scopes assembly seating.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Assembly wheelchair spaces (802)
| Item | New | Existing | Section |
|------|-----|----------|---------|
| Single space width | 36 in (915 mm) min | same | 802.3 |
| Adjacent pair, each | 33 in (840 mm) min | same | 802.3 |
| Depth, front or rear entry | 52 in (1320 mm) min | 48 in (1220 mm) min | 802.4 |
| Depth, side entry only | 60 in (1525 mm) min | 60 in (1525 mm) min | 802.4 |
| Floor slope | ≤ 1:48 | same | 802.2 |
| Aisle overlap | Depth may overlap required aisle 4 in (100 mm) max | No 4 in overlap exception | 802.5.1 |
| Companion seat | One beside each wheelchair space; shoulder alignment (36 in (915 mm) from front, 12 in (305 mm) from rear of space); same floor elevation | same | 802.7 |
| Designated aisle seats | Folding/retractable aisle-side armrest; identified | same | 802.8 |

Line of sight: comparable to nearby seated spectators (802.9.1); over standing spectators per Table 802.9.2.2 with the space front 12 in (305 mm) max from the back of the row in front (802.9.2.1).

**Location count (Table 802.10)** — technical dispersion once spaces are scoped:

| Total assembly seats | Min wheelchair space **locations** |
|----------------------|-----------------------------------|
| Up to 150 | 1 |
| 151–500 | 2 |
| 501–1000 | 3 |
| 1001–5000 | 3 + 1 per 1000 (or portion) above 1000 |
| 5001+ | 7 + 1 per 2000 (or portion) above 5000 |

Horizontal / distance / amenity-type dispersion 802.10.1–802.10.3; cinema 802.10.4. Charging at wheelchair spaces: 906 (802.10.3.1).

**Interpreter station (802.11):** 24×36 in (610×915 mm) level clear floor; sightlines in a 60° arc to 65 ft (19.8 m); 10 fc (108 lux) at 48 in (1220 mm) AFF; backdrop if a wall is < 10 ft (3050 mm) behind.

**SD takeaway:** Module each new wheelchair space as **915 × 1320 mm** (or 1525 mm if side-entry) plus a companion seat, and disperse locations per Table 802.10 — do not stack them all at the rear.

### 2. Dressing, fitting, locker rooms (803)
Turning space in the room (803.2). Door shall not swing in unless a 305.3 CFS is beyond the arc (803.3). One 903 bench (803.4). Coat hooks in 308 reach; shelves 40–48 in (1015–1220 mm) AFF (803.5).

**SD takeaway:** A locker/fitting room is not accessible unless it contains a Ch 3 turning space and a 903 bench — stall rows alone are not enough.

### 3. Kitchens (804)
| Layout | Clearance between opposing bases/counters/appliances/walls | Section |
|--------|--------------------------------------------------------------|---------|
| Pass-through (two entries) | 40 in (1015 mm) min | 804.2.1 |
| U-shaped (three sides) | 60 in (1525 mm) min; island exception uses 804.2.1 | 804.2.2 |
| No cooktop/range | 40 in (1015 mm) min | 804.2.3 |

At least one 30 in min 902 work surface (not required if no cooktop/range) (804.3). Sink 606. CFS at each appliance; controls 309 (door latches excepted). Dishwasher door shall not block its CFS or the adjacent sink CFS. Cooktop: parallel (centered) or forward + 306, insulated underside; controls not across burners. Side-hinged oven: work surface at latch side; bottom-hinged: work surface at one side. Combination fridge: ≥ 50% of freezer shelves including bottom ≤ 54 in (1370 mm) AFF; parallel CFS offset ≤ 24 in (610 mm) from appliance centerline.

**SD takeaway:** Lock U-kitchens at **1525 mm** between faces; pass-throughs at **1015 mm** with two entries; put a 760 mm 902 work surface next to the oven.

### 4. Classrooms — enhanced acoustics (808)
Applies to classrooms ≤ 20,000 ft³ (565 m³) **required** to provide enhanced acoustics (808.1).

| Method | Limit | Section |
|--------|-------|---------|
| Performance T60 | ≤ 0.6 s if volume ≤ 10,000 ft³ (285 m³); ≤ 0.7 s if 10,000–20,000 ft³ (285–566 m³); 500/1000/2000 Hz; furnished unoccupied; ASTM E2235 | 808.2.1 |
| Prescriptive NRC | Σ(NRC×area) ≥ Volume/12 (≤ 10,000 ft³) or Volume/14 (10,000–20,000 ft³) | 808.2.2 |
| Ambient | ≤ 35 dBA and 55 dBc from outside **and** inside sources; loudest usable point 36–42 in (915–1065 mm) AFF, ≥ 36 in (915 mm) from walls | 808.3 |

**SD takeaway:** If SBC/AHJ scopes 808, treat classroom volume, NRC finishes, and 35 dBA HVAC as schematic locks — not a fit-out afterthought.
