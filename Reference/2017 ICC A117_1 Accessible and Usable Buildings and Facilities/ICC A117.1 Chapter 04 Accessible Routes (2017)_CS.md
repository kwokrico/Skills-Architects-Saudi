# ICC A117.1 Chapter 04 Accessible Routes (2017)
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
2017 | ICC

> Scope note: Chapter 4 is the technical how-to for accessible routes required by the administrative authority (401.1). It does **not** say which routes or how many — that is **SBC 201 Chapter 11**. Accessible means of egress remains **SBC 201 Chapter 10**. New-building widths, 90° turns, door maneuvering, and lift/car sizes in this 2017 chapter are larger than **ICC A117.1—09** listed in SBC 201 Chapter 35; do not treat 2017 numbers as automatically the legal SBC minimum.

## Regulatory Overview
This chapter does not classify occupancy or construction type; it applies to walking surfaces, doors, ramps, curb ramps, elevators, and platform lifts that form a scoped accessible route. Revolving doors, revolving gates, and turnstiles shall not be part of an accessible route (402.3).

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Route components, slope, and clear width (402–403)
Accessible route components: walking surfaces with running slope ≤ 1:20, doors/doorways, gates, ramps, curb ramps (not flared sides), blended transitions, elevators, platform lifts (402.2).

| Item | Rule | Section |
|------|------|---------|
| Running slope (walk) | ≤ 1:20 | 403.3 |
| Cross slope (walk) | ≤ 1:48 | 403.3 |
| Level change | 303 | 403.4 |
| Interior clear width | 36 in (915 mm) min | 403.5.1 |
| Exterior clear width | 48 in (1220 mm) min | 403.5.1 |
| Exterior in seating | 36 in (915 mm) min | 403.5.1 Exc. 3 |
| Exterior ramp | 405.5 | 403.5.1 Exc. 4 |

Pinch to 32 in (815 mm) for 24 in (610 mm) max length, separated by 36 in (915 mm) wide segments of **52 in (1320 mm)** (new) or **48 in (1220 mm)** (existing) (403.5.1 Exc. 1–2).

**90° turn — new (403.5.3.1)** — one of: both legs 40 in (1015 mm) for 28 in (710 mm) from inner corner; both 36 in (915 mm) with 8 in (205 mm) chamfers; 42 in (1065 mm) + 38 in (965 mm); or 44 in (1120 mm) + 36 in (915 mm). Not required at 404.2.3 doors or 407–410 lifts. **Existing:** 36 in (915 mm) both legs (403.5.3.2).

**180° turn around object < 52 in (1320 mm) — new:** 36/60/36 in (915/1525/915 mm), or 42/48/42 in (1065/1220/1065 mm), or 43 in (1090 mm) all around (403.5.2.1). **Existing** around object < 48 in (1220 mm): 42/48/42 in (1065/1220/1065 mm), or 60 in (1525 mm) in the turn (403.5.2.2).

**Passing space** where width < 60 in (1525 mm): every 200 ft (61 m); 60×60 in (1525×1525 mm) or T-turn with arms/base extending **52 in (1320 mm)** (new) or **48 in (1220 mm)** (existing) (403.5.4).

**SD takeaway:** Hold new interior corridors at **915 mm** clear and size 90° corners to a 2017 option (40/40 or chamfered 36/36); exterior walks **1220 mm**.

### 2. Doors, doorways, and gates (404)
| Item | Rule | Section |
|------|------|---------|
| Clear opening | 32 in (815 mm) min at 90°; 36 in (915 mm) if opening depth > 24 in (610 mm) without a door | 404.2.2 |
| Projections in opening | None below 34 in (865 mm); ≤ 4 in (100 mm) from 34–80 in (865–2030 mm) | 404.2.2 |
| Threshold | ½ in (13 mm) max; existing/altered ¾ in (19 mm) max if beveled 1:2 above ¼ in (6.4 mm) | 404.2.4 |

| Hardware height | 34–48 in (865–1220 mm) | 404.2.6.1 |
| Hardware force | Push/pull 15 lb (66.7 N) max; rotation 28 in-lb (315 N·cm) max | 404.2.6 |
| Interior hinged / sliding open force | 5.0 lb (22.2 N) max (fire/panic per AHJ) | 404.2.8 |
| Automatic public entrance | Full-power or low-energy automatic (not power-assist only) | 404.3.1 |
| Vestibule automatics | Same opener type at one exterior and one interior leaf | 404.3.2 |
| Automatic clear width | 32 in (815 mm) power-on and power-off | 404.3.3 |

Manual swinging maneuvering (Table 404.2.3.2), selected SD rows:

| Approach | Side | Perpendicular | Parallel beyond latch |
|---------|------|---------------|------------------------|
| Front | Pull | 60 in (1525 mm) | 18 in (455 mm) |
| Front | Push | 52 in (1320 mm); **existing 48 in (1220 mm)** | 0; +12 in (305 mm) if closer and latch |
| Hinge | Pull | 60 in (1525 mm) or 54 in (1370 mm) | 36 in (915 mm) or 42 in (1065 mm) |
| Latch | Pull | 48 in (1220 mm); +6 in (150 mm) if closer | 24 in (610 mm) |

Double-leaf: at least one active leaf meets 404.2.2 and 404.2.3 (404.2.1). Full-power doors: BHMA A156.10-2011; power-assist/low-energy: BHMA A156.19-2013 (404.3).

**SD takeaway:** Budget **1525 mm** pull-side depth and **455 mm** latch-side at every manual AR door; vestibules need turning space between leaves unless both are automatic (404.3.6 Exc.).

### 3. Ramps (405)
| Item | Rule | Section |
|------|------|---------|
| Running slope | > 1:20 and ≤ 1:12 | 405.2 |
| Existing steeper (space-limited) | 1:10–1:8 → 3 in (75 mm) max rise; 1:12–1:10 → 6 in (150 mm) max rise; never steeper than 1:8 | Table 405.2 |
| Cross slope | ≤ 1:48 | 405.3 |
| Clear width | 36 in (915 mm) min; handrails shall not project into it | 405.5 |
| Rise per run | 30 in (760 mm) max | 405.6 |
| Landings | Top and bottom; slope ≤ 1:48; length 60 in (1525 mm) min; width ≥ widest run | 405.7 |
| Change of direction | 60×60 in (1525×1525 mm) landing | 405.7.4 |
| Handrails | Required if rise > 6 in (150 mm) | 405.8 |
| Edge protection | Both sides of runs and landings (curb ≥ 4 in (100 mm) or 4 in sphere barrier) | 405.9 |

Assembly aisle ramps not serving required AR elements are excepted (405.1).

**SD takeaway:** 1:12 max and **760 mm** rise per run; land **1525 mm** long landings (and 1525×1525 mm at turns) before setting floor-to-floor.
