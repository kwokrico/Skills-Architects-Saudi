# SBC 201 Chapter 7 Fire and Smoke Protection Features
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
2024 | SBCNC

> Scope note: This chapter is **how** rated walls, floors, shafts, openings, ducts and concealed spaces are built and protected. It is **not** when those ratings are required (Ch. 4, 5, 6, 10) and **not** mechanical, plumbing or fire-code operations. Companions named in this extract: **SBC 501** (ducts, plenums, grease/hazardous exhaust), **SBC 701** (combustible piping in Type I/II concealed ceilings), **SBC 801** (fire lanes, energy-storage enclosures, fire-barrier trigger), **NFPA 80** (opening-protective installation).

## Regulatory Overview
Chapter 7 supplies the construction, continuity, opening and penetration rules for the passive fire-resistance and smoke-control assemblies that other chapters require. Fire walls create separate buildings; fire barriers, partitions, smoke barriers/partitions and horizontal assemblies only subdivide one building; shafts and opening protectives keep those assemblies intact.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Ratings and testing overview (701–704, 703)
| Rule | Extract |
|------|---------|
| What this chapter governs | Materials, systems and assemblies for structural fire resistance and rated separation against fire/smoke spread **within** a building and **to/from** buildings (701.1) |
| Multi-use wall | One assembly serving two roles (e.g. load-bearing + incidental-use fire barrier + corridor fire partition) must meet **all** applicable sections (702.1) |
| How ratings are obtained | ASTM E119 or UL 263 **without** sprinklers in the test; or analytical methods (703.2.2: approved sources, §721, §722, engineering comparison, approved agency); or §104.11 alternative (703.2.1–703.2.3) |
| Interior nonsymmetrical walls | Test **both** faces; assigned rating = shorter duration. Exterior walls with FSD **> 3 m** may be rated from the **inside only** (703.2.1.1 / 705.5) |
| Noncombustible | ASTM E136 (or E2652 with E136 acceptance). Thin surfacing **≤ 3.18 mm**, flame spread **≤ 50** (E84 / UL 723) on a noncombustible base is still “noncombustible” (703.3.1 exception) |
| Rated glazing as a wall | ASTM E119 / UL 263 glazing with **W** label is a wall, not a window (703.4; Table 716.1(1)) |
| Identification in concealed space | Fire walls, fire barriers, fire partitions, smoke barriers, smoke partitions: stencil within **4.5 m** of each end and at **≤ 9 m** intervals; lettering **≥ 75 mm** high, **10 mm** stroke (703.5) |
| Columns / primary frame | Individual encasement for the full height/length including connections; pipes/wires/ducts **not** embedded in required individual encasement (704.2, 704.8) |
| Impact / attachments | Guard fireproofing to **≥ 1.5 m** above floor where vehicle/merchandise impact is expected; secondary steel attachments protected **≥ 300 mm** out (704.9, 704.6.1) |

**SD takeaway:** Ratings are tested assemblies, not “sprinkler credit in the furnace”; pick listed walls/floors at SD and assume interior walls must work from **either** side.

### 2. Exterior walls — FSD, ratings, openings, projections (705)
FSD is measured to the **wall**, not the overhang. Exterior bearing walls take the **higher** of Table 601 and Table 705.5; nonbearing walls use Table 705.5 only. **FSD ≤ 3 m:** rate the wall from **both** sides. **FSD > 3 m:** inside only (705.5).

**Table 705.2 — minimum distance of projection from the FSD line**

| FSD | Closest projection may come to the FSD line |
|-----|---------------------------------------------|
| 0 to < 600 mm | Projections **not permitted** |
| 600 to < 900 mm | **600 mm** |
| 900 to < 1500 mm | **Two-thirds of FSD** |
| ≥ 1500 mm | **1000 mm** |

Projections within **1500 mm** of the FSD line: noncombustible, 1-hour, heavy timber (§2304.11), FRTW, or balcony rules in 705.2.3.1. Type I/II projections: noncombustible (balcony/FRTW exceptions). Combustible balconies: aggregate length **≤ 50%** of perimeter per floor unless sprinklers are extended onto them (705.2.3.1). Type I/II buildings **≤ 3 stories**: FRTW balconies allowed.

**Table 705.5 — exterior-wall fire-resistance (hours) by FSD** *(parsed from extract HTML; occupancy columns as published)*

| FSD *X* (m) | Type | Group H | F-1, M, S-1 | A, B, E, F-2, I, R, S-2, U |
|-------------|------|---------|-------------|----------------------------|
| *X* < 1.5 | All | 3 | 2 | 1 |
| 1.5 ≤ *X* < 3 | IA, IVA | 3 | 2 | 1 |
| 1.5 ≤ *X* < 3 | Others | 2 | 1 | 1 |
| 3 ≤ *X* < 9 | IA, IB, IVA, IVB | 2 | 1 | 1 |
| 3 ≤ *X* < 9 | IIB, VB | 1 | 0 | 0 |
| 3 ≤ *X* < 9 | Others | 1 | 1 | 1 |
| *X* ≥ 9 | All | 0 | 0 | 0 |

Notes that lock SD: **a** bearing walls also Table 601; **b** zero-FSD party walls = fire walls (Table 706.4); **c** open parking garage **≥ 3 m** FSD: **no** wall rating; **d** each story’s FSD is independent; **e/f** Group H / hangars override via 415.6 / 412.3.1; **g** where Table 705.8 allows **unlimited unprotected openings**, nonbearing exterior-wall rating = **0 h** (does **not** waive Table 601 for bearing walls); **h** Group U private garage/carport: no rating if FSD **≥ 1.5 m**; **i** Group R-3 Type IIB/VB: no rating if FSD **≥ 1.5 m**.

**Table 705.8 — max opening area of the wall, per story** *(UP,S = NFPA 13 / 903.3.1.1 only; 13R counts as nonsprinklered)*

| FSD (m) | UP, NS | UP, S (NFPA 13) | Protected |
|---------|--------|-----------------|-----------|
| 0 to < 0.9 | Not permitted | Not permitted | Not permitted |
| 0.9 to < 1.5 | Not permitted | 15% | 15% |
| 1.5 to < 3 | 10% | 25% | 25% |
| 3 to < 4.5 | 15% | 45% | 45% |
| 4.5 to < 6 | 25% | 75% | 75% |
| 6 to < 7.5 | 45% | **No limit** | **No limit** |
| 7.5 to < 9 | 70% | No limit | No limit |
| ≥ 9 | No limit | No limit | No limit |

Commentary example at **3.6 m** NS: **15%** unprotected or **45%** protected (or mix via Eq. 7-2). Note **g**: open parking structure FSD **≥ 3 m** → openings **unlimited**. Note **f**: Group R-3 FSD **≥ 1.5 m** → unlimited. First-story unlimited unprotected (not Group H): wall faces a street with FSD **> 4.5 m**, or a **≥ 9 m** unoccupied space with an SBC 801 fire lane (705.8.1 Exc. 1). Type IIB/VB with **no** required exterior-wall or primary-frame rating (typically FSD **≥ 3 m**) → unlimited unprotected (705.8.1 Exc. 2). Vertical opening separation (spandrel **≥ 900 mm** 1-hour both sides, or flame barrier **≥ 750 mm**) does **not** apply to ≤ 3 stories, NFPA 13/13R buildings, or open parking (705.8.5).

Same-lot imaginary line unless treated as one building under Ch. 5. S-2 Type I/IIA garage against R-2 at **0 m** FSD: occupant openings allowed; protectives **≥ 1½ h** in the **garage** wall only (705.3 Exc. 2).

**SD takeaway:** Set lot-line FSD at SD against Tables 705.5 and 705.8 together — **≤ 3 m** means both-side rating and tight opening %, NFPA 13 at **≥ 6 m** often unlocks unlimited openings and (Note g) 0-hour nonbearing walls, and open parking is a **3 m** special case.

### 3. Fire walls (706)
Fire walls create **separate buildings** for height, area and construction type. They do **not** increase allowable height. Collapse of structure on either side must not collapse the wall (NFPA 221 deemed to comply). Materials: noncombustible except Type V (706.2–706.3). Party walls on a lot line: **no openings** (706.1.1). Ducts/air transfers: **not** through a lot-line fire wall; elsewhere only if §717 + §706.8 size limits (706.11).

**Table 706.4 — fire-wall ratings (hours)** *(occupancy groups from extract; hours as published with notes)*

| Occupancy groups | Rating |
|------------------|--------|
| A, B, E, H-4, I, R-1, R-2, U | **3** (Note a: Type II or V → **2**) |
| F-1, H-3, H-5, M, S-1 | **3** |
| H-1, H-2 | **4** (Note b: also 415.7 / 415.8) |
| F-2, S-2, R-3, R-4 | **2** |

Use the more restrictive occupancy of the two buildings. Horizontal continuity: **≥ 450 mm** beyond the exterior wall (exceptions: 1-hour / noncombustible / sprinklered 1.2 m each side). Vertical continuity: **≥ 750 mm** above both roofs (exceptions for 2-hour walls, noncombustible roofs, FRTW/Type X 1.2 m, podium §510.2). Openings: each **≤ 15 m²**, aggregate width **≤ 25%** of wall length; 15 m² cap drops if **both** sides are NFPA 13 (706.8).

**SD takeaway:** A fire wall is the only Chapter 7 device that splits one structure into two buildings — budget a 2–4 hour noncombustible wall from foundation through roof, almost no ducts, and openings as a last resort.

### 4. Fire barriers, fire partitions, smoke barriers, smoke partitions (707–710)
| Assembly | Typical rating | Continuity | Used for |
|----------|----------------|------------|----------|
| **Fire barrier** (707) | Per referenced section; fire-area split = Table 707.3.10 | Floor/foundation **to underside of deck**; through concealed space. Supporting construction rated like the barrier (Exc.: 1-hour incidental uses in IIB/IIIB/VB) | Shafts, exit stairs/passageways, horizontal exits, atriums, incidental uses, control areas, mixed occupancy (Table 508.4), fire areas |
| **Fire partition** (708) | **1 h** (corridor **½ h** where Table 1020.2 allows; dwelling/sleeping **½ h** in IIB/IIIB/VB + NFPA 13) | To deck **or** to a rated floor/roof assembly of equal rating; corridor “tunnel” and mall-tenant exceptions | I-1/R unit walls, mall tenants, corridors, elevator lobbies, egress balconies, ambulatory care, R-1/R-2 unit walls, vestibules |
| **Smoke barrier** (709) | **1 h** (I-3: **2.5 mm** steel exception) | Through concealed space; compartment walls **outside-wall to outside-wall**. Supporting construction not required in IIB/IIIB/VB | I-2 / I-3 compartments, smoke-control, areas of refuge, some elevator lobbies |
| **Smoke partition** (710) | **None** unless another section adds it | To deck **or** to a ceiling that limits smoke (taped drywall yes; typical lay-in tile no) | I-2 corridors (407.3); sprinkled elevator lobby option (3006.2). “Resist the passage of smoke” incidental-use walls (509.4.2) are **not** automatically §710 |

**Table 707.3.10 — fire-area separations (hours)**

| Occupancy | Rating |
|-----------|--------|
| H-1, H-2 | 4 |
| F-1, H-3, S-1 | 3 |
| A, B, E, F-2, H-4, H-5, I, M, R, S-2 | 2 |
| U | 1 |

Fire-area walls do **not** create separate buildings. Fire-barrier openings: **≤ 15 m²** each and **≤ 25%** of wall length (exceptions: both sides NFPA 13; exit-stair fire doors; ASTM E119/UL 263 opening protective equal to the wall). Exterior wall used as part of a shaft/stair/exit enclosure follows **§705**, except egress-specific ratings and **SBC 801** §1207 energy-storage rooms.

**SD takeaway:** At SD, name the wall type — fire wall vs fire barrier vs fire partition vs smoke barrier — because continuity through the ceiling plenum and whether the floor below must be rated all change with that name.

### 5. Horizontal assemblies (711)
A horizontal assembly is a **rated** floor or roof. Rating **not less than Table 601**, and not less than mixed-occupancy (508.4), fire-area (707.3.10), dwelling/sleeping (420.3 → **1 h**, or **½ h** in IIB/IIIB/VB with NFPA 13), smoke-barrier (709), or incidental-use (509) rules. Continuity: no vertical openings except §712. Supporting construction rated like the assembly (exceptions in IIB/IIIB/VB for 1-hour incidental uses, dwelling/sleeping separations, smoke barriers). Lay-in ceiling panels in a rated assembly: resist **48 Pa** uplift or use hold-downs. Unusable crawl/attic: 1-hour membrane on the unused side may be omitted (711.2.6). Nonrated floors still must be continuous except §712 openings (711.3).

**SD takeaway:** Dwelling-over-dwelling and mixed-use floor sandwiches are horizontal assemblies — a ½-hour sprinklered IIB/IIIB/VB unit floor is the only SD relief; mixed-occupancy Table 508.4 can still demand 2 hours.

### 6. Vertical openings and shafts (712–713)
Every floor opening uses **one** of 712.1.1–712.1.16. Default is a **shaft** (713). Architect-relevant allowances:

| Method | SD lock |
|--------|---------|
| Shaft (712.1.1 / 713) | Fire barrier +/or horizontal assembly. **2 h** if connecting **≥ 4 stories** (count basements, not mezzanines); **1 h** if < 4 stories; never less than the floor penetrated, **cap 2 h**. Rated **both** sides. Openings only those needed for the shaft; doors self- or smoke-automatic closing |
| Two-story opening (712.1.9) | Not I-2/I-3; not through fire-area or smoke-compartment separations; not concealed; not open to I/R corridors or to a corridor on a nonsprinklered floor |
| Atrium (712.1.7 / 404) | ≥ 2 stories in I-2/I-3 or ≥ 3 stories elsewhere; **not** Group H |
| Parking garage (712.1.10) | Vehicle ramps, garage-only elevators and garage-only ducts may be unenclosed per 406.5 / 406.6 |
| Escalator (712.1.3) | NFPA 13 building; draft curtain + closely spaced sprinklers (opening ≤ 2× escalator plan; 4-story cap except B and M) **or** 1.5-hour shutters |
| Dwelling unit (712.1.2) | Unconcealed openings within one dwelling, **≤ 4 stories** |
| Grease ducts (712.1.6) | **SBC 501**, not only §717 |

Shafts that stop above the lowest floor: enclose the bottom like the shaft, terminate in a related-use room of equal rating, or fire-damper the lowest floor (713.11). Interior **exit** stairs follow **§1023**, not this shaft section.

**SD takeaway:** If a void connects three or more stories, assume a 2-hour shaft from SD unless a named 712 allowance (atrium, garage ramp, two-story hole) is already in the plan.

### 7. Opening protectives (716)
Install to **NFPA 80**. Fire-**protection** rating (door/window tests) is not the same as wall fire-**resistance** (E119 / UL 263). **W**-labeled glazing is a wall. Corridor fire doors also need smoke-and-draft control. Exit-enclosure doors: unexposed-face rise **≤ 230 °C** at 30 min (commentary to Table 716.1(2)).

| FC | Meets floor/ceiling criteria |
| OH | Fire-window assembly including hose stream (NFPA 257 / UL 9) |
| D / H / T | Door / hose stream / 232 °C temperature-rise for 30 min |

### 8. Ducts and dampers (717) — SBC 501 interface
§717 is duplicated in **SBC 501** §607. Dampers: fire UL 555, smoke UL 555S, combination both, ceiling radiation UL 555C (or tested in the E119/UL 263 ceiling). **Table 717.3.2.1:** assemblies **< 3 h** → damper **1.5 h**; **≥ 3 h** → damper **3 h**.

| Penetration | Default damper |
|-------------|----------------|
| Fire wall (not on a lot line) | Fire damper; **plus smoke** if the wall is a horizontal exit (717.5.1 / 717.5.1.1) |
| Fire barrier | Fire damper. **No** ducts through interior exit-stair or exit-passageway enclosures except 1023.5 / 1024.6. Horizontal-exit fire barriers also get a **smoke** damper |
| Shaft | **Fire and smoke** (717.5.3). Steel **550 mm** subduct + continuous upward airflow can omit fire damper; garage-only shafts have a listed exception |
| Fire partition | Fire damper; corridor walls also **smoke** unless 717.5.4.1 exceptions |
| Smoke barrier | Smoke damper (steel duct serving only one compartment: exception) |
| Exterior wall | Fire damper only where Table 705.8 requires **protected** openings (typically NS FSD 900 mm–1.5 m) |
| Hazardous / grease / dryer exhaust | **SBC 501** — dampers often **prohibited**; use rated enclosure / subducts instead (717.2.2, 712.1.6) |

No damper still means a §714 through-penetration firestop. Flexible air ducts shall not penetrate rated assemblies; flexible connectors shall not pass through any wall/floor/ceiling (717.7). Access for inspection: NFPA 80 / NFPA 105.

**SD takeaway:** Coordinate shafts and rated walls with MEP at SD — shafts want combination dampers, exit enclosures want almost **no** ducts, and kitchen/dryer/hazardous exhaust follow **SBC 501**, not a damper schedule.

### 9. Concealed spaces (718)
Applies to **combustible** concealed voids. Fireblocking = small cavities; draftstopping = large floor/attic volumes. Type I/II buildings are outside 718.2–718.4 except the combustible-in-void limits of 718.5.

| Location | Lock |
|----------|------|
| Stud walls / furred / double studs | Fireblock **vertically** at floor and ceiling; **horizontally ≤ 3000 mm** |
| Wall-to-floor/soffit junctions | Fireblock the interconnection |
| Stair stringers | Fireblock top and bottom of run; under-stair rooms §1011.7.3 |
| Combustible exterior cladding voids | Fireblock ≤ **6000 mm** either way so no void **> 9 m²** (exceptions: NFPA 285 covering; metal on noncombustible framing) |
| Combustible floor/ceiling, other than Group R | Draftstop so each pocket **≤ 100 m²** (718.3); NFPA 13 throughout omits |
| Combustible attic / roof void, other than Group R | Draftstop **≤ 280 m²** (718.4); keep Ch. 12 ventilation; NFPA 13 throughout omits |
| Group R attics via fire partitions | Also 708.4.2: R-2 ≤ 4 stories and ≤ **18 m** → **280 m²** or every **two** dwelling units, whichever is smaller |

718.5 Type I/II combustibles in concealed space: only §603, plenums per **SBC 501** §602, Class A finish, piping in partitions/shafts, piping in concealed ceilings per **SBC 501 and SBC 701**, or insulation per 720.7.

**SD takeaway:** Combustible floor and attic plenums are a compartmentation problem at SD — 100 m² floor pockets and 280 m² attics (or NFPA 13 throughout) — and Type I/II still cannot hide random wood in those voids.
