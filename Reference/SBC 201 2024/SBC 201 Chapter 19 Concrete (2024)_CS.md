# SBC 201 Chapter 19 Concrete
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
2024 | SBCNC

> Scope note: This chapter governs **materials, quality control, design, and construction of concrete** (1901.1). Structural concrete is this chapter **and SBC 304** (as amended in §1905). It is **not** fire-resistance ratings of concrete assemblies (those live in **Chapter 7**; this extract only points GFRC to Table **721.1(2)** Item 15). Composite steel-concrete: **§2206**. Special inspections: **Chapter 17**.

## Regulatory Overview
Structural concrete shall be designed and constructed per this chapter and **SBC 304**. Slabs on grade are **not** governed here except **§1904** (durability) and **§1907** (thickness/vapor retarder), unless they transmit vertical loads or lateral forces from the structure to the soil (1901.2).

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Companion — SBC 304 (1901.2, 1905)
Plain and reinforced structural concrete: this chapter + **SBC 304**. Anchoring to concrete: **SBC 304** as amended in §1905. Materials and tests: standards listed in **SBC 304** (1903.1). Durability of structural concrete: **SBC 304** (1904.1). §1905: modifications are already reflected in **SBC 304-24**. Construction documents must include strengths, reinforcement, prestress, joints, and, for Seismic Design Category **D, E or F**, whether the slab on grade is a structural diaphragm (1901.5).

**SD takeaway:** Concrete design is SBC 304, not a mix-spec in this chapter — flag SDC D–F slabs that act as diaphragms.

### 2. Ground-floor / light-frame locks (1906, 1907)
| Item | Lock |
|------|------|
| Plain concrete footings (1906.1) | Group **R-3**, and other occupancies **less than two stories** above grade plane of light-frame construction: thickness permitted **150 mm**, provided the footing does not extend more than **100 mm** on either side of the supported wall |
| Slab on ground (1907.1) | Thickness **not less than 90 mm**. **150 microns (0.15 mm)** polyethylene vapor retarder, joints lapped **not less than 150 mm**, between base/subgrade and slab (or approved equivalent) |
| Vapor-retarder exceptions | Driveways, walks, patios and other flatwork that will not be enclosed later; or where approved based on local site conditions |

**SD takeaway:** Enclosed ground-floor / basement slabs are ≥ 90 mm with a 150-micron retarder; do not omit the retarder under interior floor finishes.

### 3. Fire-resistance is not this chapter (1903.3 commentary)
This chapter does not assign hourly ratings to concrete walls, floors, or columns. Commentary: glass fiber-reinforced concrete is used in the **prescriptive fire-resistance ratings** for wall assemblies — **Table 721.1(2), Item 15** (Chapter 7).

**SD takeaway:** Concrete fire ratings are Chapter 7 (and listings), not Chapter 19.
