# SBC 201 Chapter 15 Roof Assembly
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
2024 | SBCNC

> Scope note: This chapter is the **roof covering and rooftop structures** — weather protection, fire classification, drainage, aggregate parapets. It is not structural live/wind load (Ch. 16) except by pointer, and not exterior-wall parapet **fire** height (that is **705.11**). Primary/secondary drain sizing: **SBC 701** Chapter 11 and §1611. Membrane structures as roofs: **§3102**. Light-transmitting plastic roofs: **§2606**.

## Regulatory Overview
Minimum roof-covering class is Table 1505.1 by construction type (Class A anywhere; Class C only on IIB / IIIB / VB in this extract’s commentary). Parapets that trap water need secondary drains or scuppers (**≥ 100 mm** opening); aggregate roofs need Table 1504.9 parapet height.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Roof covering class (1505, Table 1505.1)
Test Class A/B/C to ASTM E108 or UL 790. Table 1505.1 in the extract is concatenated as **B B B C° B C° B B C°** under IA–VB. That matches commentary: **Class C listed only for Types IIB, IIIB and VB**; Class A or B allowed on any type. Notes as printed:

| Note | Effect |
|------|--------|
| (unlettered first note) | Otherwise as required for Urban Wildland Interface or fire district **Appendix D** |
| Nonclassified | Group **R-3** and **U** with FSD ≥ **1.8 m** from the leading edge of the roof |
| Note c (cedar/redwood) | ≤ **2 stories**, projected roof ≤ **560 m²**, FSD ≥ **3.0 m** to lot lines (except street/public way): No. 1 cedar or redwood shakes/shingles per 1505.7 |

Class A deemed-to-comply examples (1505.2): brick, masonry, exposed concrete; ferrous/copper/metal/clay/concrete/slate on noncombustible decks; copper ≥ **4.9 kg/m²** over combustible decks; slate over ASTM D226 Type II over combustible decks. BIPV covering: 1505.8 / E108–UL 790. Rooftop PV: UL 2703 class must meet Table 1505.1 (1505.9). Landscaped roofs: 1505.1, 1507.15, ANSI/SPRI VF-1.

**SD takeaway:** Type IIB/IIIB/VB can take Class C; everything else in the table is Class B minimum — do not put an unlisted torch-down on a Type IIA plate.

### 2. Parapets (1503.3, 705.11, 1504.9)
Cope/cover the parapet; top surface **positive drainage**. Width of weatherproof cap **not less than** parapet thickness. Fire-resistance-rated parapets required by **705.11**: covering shall not reduce the wall rating. Exception parapets under 705.11 still get a full-width coping.

**Aggregate-surfaced roofs:** parapets per **Table 1504.9** (extract table OCR-garbled — do not invent millimetre rows). Published notes: interpolate height and wind; where the table cell is **50 mm**, a gravel stop ≥ 50 mm and ≥ aggregate height is allowed; Exposure **D** = Exposure C height **+ 200 mm**, and **not less than 300 mm**.

**SD takeaway:** A 705.11 fire parapet is a rated wall with a full-width coping; a gravel roof adds Table 1504.9 height (Exposure D at least 300 mm) — do not treat them as the same detail.

### 3. Drainage and scuppers (1502)
Design/install per this section, **§1611**, and **SBC 701 Chapter 11**. Secondary (overflow) drains **or scuppers** required where the perimeter can **entrap** water if primary drains fail. Scuppers: sized so ponding does not exceed the §1611.1 design depth; opening dimension **≥ 100 mm**; **ignore** primary flow when locating/sizing. Gutters/leaders on the outside (other than R-3, private garages, Type V): noncombustible or min. Schedule 40 plastic. Flashing metal ≥ **0.48 mm** (No. 26 galvanized), corrosion-resistant. Crickets/saddles on the ridge side of chimneys/penetrations **> 760 mm** wide (unit-skylight exception). Attic vents: **1202.2**.

**SD takeaway:** Any roof with a parapet or upstand that can pond needs a second drain path; scuppers are at least 100 mm and are sized as if the primary drains are dead.
