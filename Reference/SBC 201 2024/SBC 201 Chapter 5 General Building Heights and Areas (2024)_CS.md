# SBC 201 Chapter 5 General Building Heights and Areas
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
2024 | SBCNC

> Scope note: This chapter sets allowable building height (m), stories above grade plane, and area from occupancy × construction type × sprinklers, with frontage, unlimited-area, mixed-use, incidental-use, and podium options. It is not the occupancy-classification chapter (Ch. 3) or the construction-type ratings chapter (Ch. 6 / Table 601). Open parking garages use Table 406.5.4 in Chapter 4, not Tables 504/506. Existing-building evaluation of NS rows for groups that must be sprinklered when new is via **SBC 901**.

## Regulatory Overview
Height in metres (Table 504.3), stories (Table 504.4), and area (Table 506.2) are applied independently; fire walls (Section 706) create separate buildings for these tests. Mixed occupancies pick accessory, nonseparated, or separated (Section 508); a 3-hour horizontal assembly over Type IA can split a podium into distinct buildings (Section 510.2).

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Independent H / S / A limits and fire walls (503.1)
Building height, number of stories, and building area shall not exceed Sections 504 and 506 except as modified in Chapter 4 or this chapter. Each portion separated by one or more fire walls complying with Section 706 is a separate building for area, height, and construction type. Address identification: characters **≥ 100 mm** high, stroke **≥ 12.5 mm** (502.1).

**SD takeaway:** If the massing exceeds one tabular building, either raise construction type / sprinkle throughout, or cut the plan with fire walls — do not average two occupancies across one unseparated volume.

### 2. Occupied roofs (503.1.4, 503.1.4.1)
An occupied roof is permitted if that occupancy is allowed by Table 504.4 on the story immediately below. Roof area is **not** building area (Section 506) and the roof is **not** a story or height increment if penthouses comply with Section 1511.

| Exception | Effect |
|-----------|--------|
| Building sprinkled 903.3.1.1 or 903.3.1.2 **and** occupant notification 907.5.2.1 / 907.5.2.3 (plus EVAC if required elsewhere) | Occupancy on the roof is **not** limited to the story below |
| Assembly on Type I or II open parking | Aligns with 903.2.1.6 exception |

Enclosures over occupied roof areas shall not extend more than **1200 mm** above the roof surface except penthouses/towers/domes/spires/cupolas per 1511. Shade structures, kiosks, storage, or bathrooms on the roof make it a story.

**SD takeaway:** A rooftop restaurant on unsprinklered Type VB office is limited to a one-story building; sprinkle + notify, or keep occupied-roof uses inside the Table 504.4 envelope of the floor below.

### 3. Height in metres and stories (504.3, 504.4)
Allowable height (m above grade plane) = Table 504.3; stories above grade plane = Table 504.4. Both vary by occupancy, construction type, and sprinkler row:

| Row tag | Meaning |
|---------|---------|
| NS | Not sprinkled throughout |
| S | NFPA 13 (903.3.1.1) throughout |
| S13R | NFPA 13R (903.3.1.2) — Group R / I-1 low-rise |
| S13D | NFPA 13D (903.3.1.3) — R-3, R-4 Condition 1, townhouses |

Worked examples in this chapter: Group B, Type VB, NS → **12 m** and **2 stories**. Type IA height in metres is **UL** for many groups; still check stories and area. Type IV-A commentary cap: **81 m** (less than Type IA unlimited) because of mass-timber combustibility.

Combustible rooftop towers/spires: not more than **6 m** above allowable building height; noncombustible such structures unlimited (504.3 exception). New Group H, I-1, I-2, I-3, and R are required to be sprinklered (notes to the tables); NS values for those groups are for **SBC 901** existing-building evaluation only.

**SD takeaway:** Pick occupancy, construction type, and sprinkler standard together — an NFPA 13R residential stack is a different height row than NFPA 13, and NS is not a legal new-build row for Group R.

### 4. Mezzanines (505.2)
A complying mezzanine is part of the story below: it does **not** add building area or a story, but **does** count in fire area. Clear height above and below **≥ 2.1 m**. Aggregate mezzanine area **≤ 1/3** of the room (enclosed portions of the room excluded from the denominator).

| Exception | Aggregate limit |
|-----------|-----------------|
| Type I or II special industrial (503.1.1) | **2/3** of room |
| Type I or II + NFPA 13 + EVAC | **1/2** of room |
| Dwelling-unit mezzanine in 13 or 13R building, open per listed conditions | **1/2** of room |

**SD takeaway:** Keep mezzanines ≤ one-third of the open room and 2.1 m clear both ways, or they become another story and eat Table 506.2 area.

### 5. Area, frontage, and unlimited-area paths (506, 507)
Tabular area \(A_T\) is Table 506.2 (NS / S1 one-story sprinkled / SM multi-story sprinkled / S13R / S13D). Frontage increase requires **≥ 25%** of the perimeter on a public way or open space **and** that open space **≥ 6 m** measured perpendicular to the face (506.3.1–506.3.2). Increase factor from Tables 506.3.3 / 506.3.3.1.

Section 507 unlimited-area highlights (yards typically **18 m**, reducible to **12 m** under 507.2.1):

| Section | Occupancy / type | Stories | Other lock |
|---------|------------------|---------|------------|
| 507.3 | F-2 or S-2 | 1, NS | 18 m yards |
| 507.4 | B, F, M, S any type; A-4 other than Type V | 1, NFPA 13 | 18 m yards |
| 507.5 | B, F, M, S | 2, NFPA 13 | 18 m yards |
| 507.6 / 507.7 | Specified A-3 halls | 1 | Type II vs III/IV extra conditions |
| 507.11 | E | 1 | Type II, IIIA or IV + listed conditions |
| 507.12 | Motion-picture theater | First story | Type II + NFPA 13 + 18 m |
| 507.13 | Covered/open mall + anchors | ≤ 3 | Section 402 |

**SD takeaway:** Big-box B/F/M/S can go unlimited on one or two stories if NFPA 13 plus a 18 m yard; otherwise buy area with construction type and ≥ 25% / ≥ 6 m frontage.

### 6. Mixed occupancy (508)
Each portion is classified per 302.1. Choose one (or a combination):

| Path | Height/area | Separation |
|------|-------------|------------|
| **Accessory 508.2** | Use **main** occupancy for H/S/A | None required; aggregate accessory **≤ 10%** of the story |
| **Nonseparated 508.3** | **Most restrictive** occupancy for H/S/A; most restrictive Ch. 9 (and §403 if high-rise) throughout the nonseparated area | None |
| **Separated 508.4** | Each occupancy vs its own Table 504/506; sum of area ratios ≤ 1 | Table 508.4 hours (S vs NS columns). N = no separation; NP = not permitted |

Table 508.4 is occupancy-pair × sprinklered/nonsprinklered. Occupancy separations that also define Chapter 9 fire areas must meet Table 707.3.10 (note f). Ambulatory care: see 422.2 (note e).

**SD takeaway:** Nonseparated mixed use is sized by the worst occupancy in the mix; if A-2 sits under B on Type VB NS, the A-2 one-story cap kills the second floor unless you separate, sprinkle, or raise type.

### 7. Incidental uses (509 / Table 509.1)
Incidental rooms (furnace > **117 kW**, boiler > **103.5 kPa** and **7.5 kW**, laundry **> 9 m²**, waste/linen rooms, labs in E/I-2/ambulatory care, incinerator rooms, etc.) need **1-hour** separation **or** a sprinkler in that room (some rows require both). They do not become a separate occupancy group. Electrical rooms: NFPA 70 / **SBC 401**.

**SD takeaway:** Plant, laundry, and waste rooms over 9 m² need a 1-hour envelope or a dedicated sprinkler — budget that room at SD, not in CD.

### 8. Podium / pedestal (510.2, 510.9)
A 3-hour horizontal assembly over **Type IA** construction may treat the volume above and below as **separate buildings** for area, stories, fire-wall continuity, and construction type. Commentary conditions include: no Group H below; NFPA 13 throughout the lower building; overall height in **metres** still limited by Table 504.3 for the **upper** type.

Commentary example: Type IA parking/retail below + Type VA R-2 above — **four** VA stories above the slab if 13R, provided total height ≤ **18 m**; NFPA 13 on the upper building raises the metre cap to **21 m**. Multiple buildings may sit on one pedestal (510.9). Shafts through the 3-hour slab connecting ≤ four stories may be 1-hour above the slab (exception to Item 3).

**SD takeaway:** Podium is the schematic move for mixed residential-over-retail: 3-hour Type IA plate, then count upper-building stories from that plate — but the metre height is still the upper type measured from grade plane.
