# SBC 201 Chapter 33 Safeguards During Construction
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
2024 | SBCNC

> Scope note: Construction- and demolition-phase protection of the **public and adjoining property** (3301.1). Schematic locks are **occupied-building phasing**, **site fences / pedestrian walkways / covered walkways**, and **hydrants–standpipes–fire watch** that eat the plot. It is not a substitute for Chapter 10 egress, Chapter 11 accessibility of temporary walkways, or **SBC 801 Chapter 33** fire operations. This CS skips waste-haul methods, portable-extinguisher counts, and formwork wood-removal unless they change the site diagram.

## Regulatory Overview
This chapter does not set occupancy or construction type; it governs how an occupied shell, a sidewalk, and a construction plot stay safe while work proceeds. Fire-safety operations during construction and demolition are correlated to **Chapter 33 of SBC 801** (3302.3, 3303.7).

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Occupied-building phasing (3302, 3303.3, 3310)
If the existing building stays occupied, required **exits, existing structural elements, fire-protection devices and sanitary safeguards** shall be maintained during alterations, repairs or additions (3302.1).

| Exception | When it applies |
|-----------|-----------------|
| 1 | The required element is itself being altered or repaired — **adequate substitute** provisions |
| 2 | The existing building is **not occupied** |

A **horizontal exit** shall not be destroyed until a substitute means of egress has been provided and approved (3303.3). Required means of egress shall be maintained at all times during construction, demolition, remodeling or alterations and additions (3310.2); exception: approved **temporary** means of egress.

Where building construction exceeds **12 m** in height above the lowest level of fire-department vehicle access, a temporary or permanent **stairway** shall be provided and extended to within one floor of the highest secured decking/flooring (3310.1). Commentary: stairways per **Section 1011**.

**SD takeaway:** Occupied phasing keeps existing exits and fire protection live (or an approved substitute); above 12 m of construction height, lock a stair that tracks one floor below the working deck.

### 2. Pedestrian protection, railings, barriers, covered walkways (3306, Table 3306.1)
Pedestrians shall be protected as required by this chapter and **Table 3306.1**; signs shall direct pedestrian traffic (3306.1). Demolition shall not start until pedestrian protection is in place (3303.2).

A walkway shall be provided in front of every construction and demolition site unless the applicable governing authority authorizes the sidewalk to be fenced or closed. A walkway shall lead from a building entrance or exit of an **occupied** structure to a public way.

| Walkway / railing / barrier | Lock |
|-----------------------------|------|
| Walkway width | Sufficient for traffic, **not less than 1.2 m** |
| Walking surface | Durable; **accessible per Chapter 11**; design live load **not less than 7.2 kN/m²** |
| Construction railings (3306.4) | **Not less than 1.05 m** high |
| Barriers (3306.5) | **Not less than 2.4 m** high, on the construction side of the walkway, full site length; openings by doors **normally kept closed** |
| Covered walkway (3306.7) | Clear height **not less than 2.4 m**; design live load **not less than 7.2 kN/m²**; lighting at all times |
| Covered-walkway exception | New **light-frame** not exceeding **two stories** above grade plane: live load **3.6 kPa** or imposed loads, whichever is greater (or the seven prescriptive timber criteria) |

**Table 3306.1** (HTML extract mashed). Printed bands: building height **2.4 m or less** vs **more than 2.4 m**; distance from construction to lot line **less than 1500 mm**, **1500 mm or more**, and fractions of construction height (**one-fourth** / **one-half**). Protection types named: **construction railings**, **barrier**, **barrier and covered walkway**, or **none**. Confirm the ¼- and ½-height rows on the published table before dropping a covered walkway.

Directional barricade where the walkway extends into the street (3306.3). Pedestrian protection stays until pedestrians are no longer endangered, then the owner shall immediately restore public property (3306.8).

**SD takeaway:** Budget a **≥ 1.2 m** accessible walkway (7.2 kN/m²) along the frontage; if Table 3306.1 calls a covered walkway, lock **2.4 m** clear height and a **2.4 m** site barrier on the construction side.

### 3. Excavation, adjoining property, streets (3304, 3306.9, 3307, 3308)
| Item | Lock | Section |
|------|------|---------|
| Permanent fill / cut slopes | Not steeper than **1 vertical : 2 horizontal (50-percent slope)** unless a soil report is accepted for cut slopes | 3304.1.1 |
| Surcharge / adjacent footings | No fill or surcharge unless the structure can take it; affected existing footings **underpinned or otherwise protected** | 3304.1.2 |
| Excavation vs street lot line | **≤ 1.5 m** from street lot line → barrier **not less than 1.8 m** high; **> 1.5 m** → barrier where the building official requires; wind per Chapter 16 | 3306.9 |
| Adjoining notice | Written notice to adjoining building owners **not less than 10 days** before scheduled excavation start | 3307.1 |
| Retention systems | Designed by a registered design professional; monitor horizontal and vertical movement; remove only with replacement support | 3307.2–3307.2.3 |
| Storage on streets | Comply with the applicable governing authority and this chapter | 3308.1 |
| Obstructions | Do not obstruct hydrants, standpipes, fire/police alarm boxes, catch basins or manholes; do not locate within **6.1 m** of a street intersection; do not block traffic-signal observation or public-transit loading platforms | 3308.1.1 |

Footings on adjacent slopes: **Chapter 18**. Fill supporting foundations: **SBC 303**; special inspection **1705.6**. Sanitary facilities during construction: **SBC 701** (3305.1). Vacant lot after demolition: fill to existing grade or AHJ ordinances; prevent water accumulation (3303.4–3303.5).

**SD takeaway:** A basement next to a street needs a **1.8 m** excavation barrier if within **1.5 m** of the street lot line; keep a **6.1 m** clear cone at intersections and 10-day adjoining-owner notice before digging.

### 4. Construction fire that locks the plot (3302.3, 3311, 3313, 3314)
Fire safety during construction and demolition shall comply with this code and **Chapter 33 of SBC 801** (3302.3, 3303.7).

| Trigger | Site lock | Section |
|---------|-----------|---------|
| Standpipe required by 905.3.1 | **≥ 1** standpipe **before** construction exceeds **12 m** above lowest FD vehicle access; hose connections adjacent to 3310.1 stairways; extend to within one floor of highest secured decking | 3311.1 |
| Combustible materials on site | Minimum fire flow **1900 L/m**; hydrant within **152 m** of those materials along an approved fire-apparatus lane | 3313.2 |
| Standpipe (any type / FSD) | Minimum flow **1900 L/m**; hydrant within **30 m** of the FDC supplying the standpipe | 3313.5 |
| Type III, IV or V vertical construction | Fire-flow vs lot-line separation: **< 9 m** → 1900 L/m **or** full completed-building fire flow, whichever greater; **9–18 m** → 1900 L/m **or 50%** of completed fire flow; **≥ 18 m** → **1900 L/m** | 3313.3.1–3313.3.3 |
| Type I and II | 3313.2 if combustibles are delivered; **no extra** fire flow solely to start vertical construction | 3313.4 |
| Fire watch (nonworking hours) | Construction **> 12 m** above lowest adjacent grade at any perimeter point, **or** new multistory with aggregate area **> 4645 m²** per story, **or** as required by the fire code official | 3314.1 |

Sprinklered buildings: no occupancy of any portion until the system is tested and approved, except **Section 111.3** (3312.1). Isolated rural reduction of fire-flow: fire code official (exception to 3313.1). Commentary: Appendix B of SBC 801 is not mandatory unless adopted.

**SD takeaway:** On a combustible or close-to-lot-line plot, lock hydrants at **152 m** of stored combustibles and **30 m** of the standpipe FDC before the building passes **12 m** — that is a site-plan item, not a late shop drawing.
