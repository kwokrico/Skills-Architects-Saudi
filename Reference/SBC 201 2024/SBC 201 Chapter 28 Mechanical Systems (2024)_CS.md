# SBC 201 Chapter 28 Mechanical Systems
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
2024 | SBCNC

> Scope note: This chapter **only points** mechanical appliances, equipment and systems to **SBC 501** and **SBC 1201**. It contains **no** shaft, plant-room, or kitchen-hood criteria of its own. Masonry chimneys, fireplaces and barbecues: **SBC 501** and **Chapter 21**. Use/maintenance: **SBC 801**, IPMC, SBC 501 and SBC 1201. Alterations: **SBC 901**, SBC 501 and SBC 1201.

## Regulatory Overview
Section 2801.1 assigns design, construction, erection and installation of mechanical systems to this chapter, SBC 501 and SBC 1201. Commentary: the chapter was written as a link so SBC 201 does not duplicate or conflict with those volumes.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Mandatory pointer (2801.1)
SBC 501 regulates air distribution and ductwork; boilers and hydronic systems; exhaust; fuel-oil piping; combustion air; chimneys and vents; ventilation air; refrigeration; solar-powered systems; and specific appliances. SBC 1201 regulates fuel-gas piping, fuel-gas utilization equipment and related accessories (combustion air, chimneys/vents for gas appliances, piping, specific gas appliances). Commentary: Chapter 3 of both volumes requires equipment to be listed and labeled; Chapter 2 definitions in those volumes supersede others for mechanical/gas work. “Noncombustible material” in SBC 501 does **not** include the composite materials of SBC 201 **§703.5.2**.

Masonry chimneys, fireplaces and barbecues: **SBC 501** and **Chapter 21**.

**SD takeaway:** Put SBC 501 / SBC 1201 on the mechanical sheet; Chapter 28 adds no plant-room, shaft, or Type I hood locks.
