# SBC 201 Chapter 35 Referenced Standards
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
2024 | SBCNC

> Scope note: This chapter is the **edition list** for standards already cited in SBC 201. It is **not** a design manual and is **not** the full ASTM/UL dump. Application is **Section 102.4**. For enforcement, listed standards shall be adopted and/or approved by **SASO or SBCNC** as per their mandates before being considered part of this code. Architect SD use: confirm the **printed** ICC A117.1 year and the elevator / sprinkler companion editions named below.

## Regulatory Overview
Chapter 35 identifies the promulgating agency, designation, **effective date**, title, and the code sections that cite each standard. Where this list and the charging section disagree, **Section 102.4** governs how the reference is applied.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Status of the list (chapter preamble, 102.4)
The standards are listed for **guidance**. They become enforceable when the code is adopted **and** the standard is adopted/approved by the appropriate governmental authority (**SASO or SBCNC**). ICC-style criteria in the commentary: mandatory language, non-proprietary, consensus process. Edition year in this chapter is the one to specify — do not substitute a later A117.1 or ASME from memory.

**SD takeaway:** Put the Chapter 35 designation-and-year on the spec sheet; do not treat an unlisted newer edition as the SBC 201 reference.
