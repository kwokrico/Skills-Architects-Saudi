# SBC 201 Chapter 21 Masonry
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
2024 | SBCNC

> Scope note: This chapter governs **materials, design, construction, and quality of masonry** (2101.1). Masonry shall comply with **SBC 305** as well as this chapter (2101.2). It is **not** fire-resistance of masonry assemblies (**Chapter 7**), **not** masonry veneer detailing (**Chapter 14**), **not** foundations (**Chapter 18** / SBC 303), and **not** loads (**Chapter 16** / SBC 301).

## Regulatory Overview
Engineered and empirical masonry design methods live in **SBC 305**, with this chapter’s construction-document, materials, and construction rules. Special inspection of masonry is **Chapter 17**, or an itemized program that meets or exceeds Chapter 17 (2101.3).

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Companion — SBC 305 (2101.2, 2106)
Masonry shall comply with **SBC 305** and applicable requirements of this chapter. Seismic design of masonry structures and components: **SBC 305 Chapter 7**, by seismic design category (2106.1). Quality assurance shall comply with Chapter 17 **and** SBC 305.

**SD takeaway:** Structural masonry is SBC 305 — this chapter does not give wall thicknesses or f'm for SD.

### 2. Veneer, fire, foundations (2101.2 commentary, 2101.2.1)
| Topic | Where it actually governs |
|-------|---------------------------|
| Masonry veneer | **Chapter 14** (2101.2.1); those sections reference SBC 305 Chapter 12 |
| Fire-resistant masonry construction | **Chapter 7** (commentary to 2101.2) |
| Masonry foundations | **Chapter 18** (commentary to 2101.2) → **SBC 303** |
| Design loads / seismic | **Chapter 16** and **SBC 301** (commentary to 2101.2) |
| Special inspections | **Chapter 17** (2101.3) |

Empirical adobe is limited by **SBC 305** Appendix A; buildings that exceed those limits go to engineered design or foundation walls in **SBC 303** §6.5 (2109.1.1).

**SD takeaway:** A masonry rain-screen or fire wall is Ch. 14 / Ch. 7, not a Chapter 21 mix design.
