# SBC 201 Chapter 2 Definitions
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
2024 | SBCNC

> Scope note: This chapter **defines terms used in SBC 201**. It is **not** occupancy (Ch. 3), height/area (Ch. 5), fire construction (Ch. 7), egress (Ch. 10), or accessibility scoping (Ch. 11). Terms not defined here take the meaning in other referenced Saudi codes (201.3), else ordinary context (201.4).

## Regulatory Overview
Unless expressly stated otherwise, words and terms have the meanings in this chapter (201.1). Gender, tense, and number are interchangeable (201.2).

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Height, grade plane, high-rise (202)
| Term | Lock (as published) |
|------|---------------------|
| **Grade plane** | Average of finished ground adjoining the building at exterior walls. Where ground slopes away, use the lowest points between the building and the lot line, or a point **1.8 m** from the building if the lot line is farther. |
| **Story above grade plane** | Finished floor entirely above grade plane; **or** the finished surface of the floor next above is more than **1.8 m** above grade plane; **or** more than **3.6 m** above finished ground at any point. Any level that does not qualify is a **basement** (flood §1612 uses a different basement definition). |
| **High-rise building** | Occupied floor more than **23 m** above the lowest level of fire department vehicle access — not building height from grade plane. Must comply with **§403**. Commentary: additional rules at floors higher than **36 m** and **126 m**. |

**SD takeaway:** Count stories and high-rise from grade plane / FD access as defined here; a buried podium level can still be a story above grade plane if the floor above is > 1.8 m above grade plane or > 3.6 m above ground at any point.

### 2. Fire area, fire separation distance, atrium (202)
| Term | Lock (as published) |
|------|---------------------|
| **Fire area** | Aggregate floor area enclosed and bounded by fire walls, fire barriers, exterior walls, or horizontal assemblies. Unwalled areas count if they sit under the horizontal projection of the roof or floor next above. Mezzanine area is in the fire-area sum (commentary). |
| **Fire separation distance** | Distance from the building face, measured at right angles from the wall, to: (1) the closest interior lot line; (2) the centerline of a street, alley, or public way; or (3) an imaginary line between two buildings on the lot. |
| **Atrium** | Vertical space closed at the top, connecting **two or more stories** in Group I-2 and I-3, or **three or more stories** in all other occupancies. Open-to-sky courts are not atria; balconies of assembly occupancies and mezzanines do not count as stories for this test (commentary). Separation: **§404.6**. |

**SD takeaway:** Lock lot-line / imaginary-line FSD and whether the void is an atrium (closed top; 2 stories in I-2/I-3, 3 elsewhere) before drawing the plate.

### 3. Dwelling unit, sleeping unit, townhouse (202)
| Term | Lock (as published) |
|------|---------------------|
| **Dwelling unit** | Single unit with complete independent living facilities: permanent living, sleeping, eating, cooking, **and** sanitation. |
| **Sleeping unit** | Rooms/spaces for one or more persons with permanent sleeping; may include living, eating, and **either** sanitation **or** kitchen facilities **but not both**. Spaces that are part of a dwelling unit are not sleeping units. |
| **Townhouse** | Single-family dwelling unit in a group of **three or more** attached units; each unit foundation to roof and open space on at least two sides. |

Commentary townhouse path off this code onto **SBC 1101/1102** (exception to **101.2**) if all four: foundation-to-roof with no vertical overlap; open space on at least two sides; separate means of egress; **≤ 3 stories** above grade plane. Otherwise stay on SBC 201 (typically Group R-2, or R-3 if fire-walled to one or two units).

**SD takeaway:** A hotel room or studio with only a kitchenette is a sleeping unit; apartments with full kitchens are dwelling units. ≤ 3-story townhouses meeting the four tests leave SBC 201 for 1101/1102.

### 4. Accessible / Type A / Type B units (202)
| Term | Lock (as published) |
|------|---------------------|
| **Accessible** | Site, building, facility, or portion that complies with **Chapter 11**. Commentary: technical criteria via **ICC A117.1**. |
| **Accessible unit** | Dwelling or sleeping unit that complies with this code **and** Accessible units in ICC A117.1. Fully accessible at first occupancy; no adaptable features (commentary). Commentary cites **2017 ICC A117.1** §1102. |
| **Type A unit** | Dwelling or sleeping unit to this code and Type A in ICC A117.1. Commentary: some accessible, some adaptable; scoped only in large Group R-2 apartments; A117.1 §1103. |
| **Type B unit** | Dwelling or sleeping unit to this code and Type B in ICC A117.1. Commentary: lowest of the three levels; A117.1 §1104. |

**SD takeaway:** Unit-type labels (Accessible / A / B) are defined here; counts and which occupancies get them are Chapter 11, not this chapter.
