# SBC 201 Chapter 12 Interior Environment
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
2024 | SBCNC

> Scope note: This chapter sets **ventilation, heating, light, yards/courts, sound, classroom acoustics, room dimensions, and toilet finishes** for interior spaces. It is not occupancy, egress illumination (except the 1008 pointer), or fixture counts (Ch. 29). Mechanical ventilation and exhaust: **SBC 501**. Energy/air-leakage tests named here: **SBC 601 / SBC 602** (or IECC equivalent). Plumbing drainage of courts and bathroom fixtures: **SBC 701**. Enhanced classroom acoustics, where required, are **ICC A117.1 §808** (no edition year in charging text).

## Regulatory Overview
Every occupied room gets natural ventilation (1202.5) or mechanical ventilation per SBC 501, plus light (natural 8% glazed or artificial **107 lux**). Schematic plan locks are openable area, yard/court size if you rely on daylight/air, ceiling heights, and the dwelling STC/IIC wall-floor sandwich.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Ventilation (1202)
Natural **or** mechanical (SBC 501). Dwelling units with infiltration **< 5 ACH** at **50 Pa** (IECC residential / **SBC 602** equivalent): mechanical ventilation per **SBC 501 §403**. Ambulatory care and **Group I-2**: mechanical per **SBC 501 §407**. Bathrooms with tubs/showers/spas: mechanical per SBC 501 (1202.5.2.1). Contaminants: SBC 501 and **SBC 801**.

**Natural ventilation (1202.5):** openable area ≥ **4%** of the floor area served. Borrowed ventilation: opening to the outer room ≥ **8%** of the interior room (min **2.3 m²**), unobstructed (no door); outer-room openable area based on **combined** floor area. Sunroom/patio exception: min opening **1.86 m²**. Openings below grade: horizontal clear space **1.5 ×** depth of the opening.

**Attics (1202.2.1):** net free vent area ≥ **1/150** of the space (example in commentary: **100 m²** attic → **0.67 m²**); **1/300** if both listed conditions are met (Climate Zones 6–8 Class I/II VR on the warm-in-winter ceiling **and** 40–50% of venting in the upper portion, ≤ **900 mm** below the ridge). **≥ 25 mm** airspace between insulation and roof sheathing. Unvented attics: 1202.3 (thermal envelope, no interior Class I VR on the attic floor, Table 1202.3 R-values; Climate Zones 1–3 vapor-diffusion port ≥ **1/600** of ceiling, roof slope ≥ **3:12**). Crawl spaces: **0.67 m² / 100 m²** (open earth) or **0.67 m² / 1000 m²** (Class I VR on ground); mechanical option **1.02 L/s per 10 m²**.

**SD takeaway:** Deep-plan rooms without 4% openable area (or a 2.3 m² unobstructed borrow opening) are mechanical-ventilation rooms — lock SBC 501 before sealing the envelope.

### 2. Heating and light (1203–1204)
Occupied interiors: active or passive heat capable of **20°C** at **900 mm** above the floor on the design heating day. Exceptions: spaces not for human comfort; Groups **F, H, S, U**. Outdoor design temps: Appendix D of **SBC 701**.

Light: natural **or** artificial. Natural: net glazed ≥ **8%** of floor area, onto a public way/yard/court (1205). Borrowed light: common wall ≥ **½** open, opening ≥ **1/10** of interior room or **2.3 m²**. Roofed porch exception: ceiling ≥ **2.1 m**, longer side ≥ **65%** open. Skylights need not face a yard. Artificial: average **107 lux** at **750 mm** above the floor. Dwelling-unit stairs (interior and exterior serving the unit): **11 lux** on treads; controls per NFPA 70 / **SBC 401**. Other occupancy stairs: Chapter 10. MOE illumination: **1008.1**.

**SD takeaway:** Interior rooms without 8% glazing to a complying yard need 107 lux artificial light; do not count borrowed light through a door.

### 3. Yards and courts (1205)
Only where openings provide **required** natural light or ventilation (skip if the rooms are mechanically ventilated and artificially lit). Same lot as the building.

| | Width (≤ 2 stories) | Extra per story (stories 3–14) | Cap (> 14 stories) |
|--|---------------------|--------------------------------|--------------------|
| Yard | **900 mm** | **+300 mm**/story | **4500 mm** |
| Court | **900 mm** (**1800 mm** if windows on opposite sides); length **≥ 3000 mm** unless one end is a public way/yard | **+300 mm** width and **+600 mm** length per story | Width **4500 mm** / **5500 mm** opposite openings; length **10360 mm** if bounded |

Courts > 2 stories, fully bounded: bottom air intake ≥ **1.0 m²** to the exterior unless abutting a yard or public way. Court drainage: **SBC 701**.

**SD takeaway:** If you rely on courtyard daylight, a five-story court is already 1800 mm (one-sided) or 2700 mm (opposite windows) — artificial light/ventilation is cheaper than growing the void.

### 4. Sound and classroom acoustics (1206–1207)
Dwelling/sleeping units vs each other or public/service areas: airborne **STC ≥ 50** (ASTM E90) or field **NNIC ≥ 45** (ASTM E336); entrance doors exempt but tight-fitting. Floor-ceilings: **IIC ≥ 50** (ASTM E492) or field **NISR ≥ 45** (ASTM E1007). Masonry STC may use TMS 302.

**Group E** classrooms with volume **≤ 565 m³**: enhanced classroom acoustics per **ICC A117.1 §808** (1207). Commentary: at **3 m** ceiling that is about **185 m²**; not for band/gym/lecture halls or corridors.

**SD takeaway:** Apartment demising walls/floors are an STC 50 / IIC 50 sandwich at SD; elementary classrooms ≤ 565 m³ pick up A117.1 §808.

### 5. Room dimensions (1208)
| Rule | Dimension |
|------|-----------|
| Habitable rooms (not kitchens) | ≥ **2100 mm** in any plan dimension |
| Kitchen clear passage | ≥ **900 mm** |
| Occupiable / habitable / corridors ceiling | ≥ **2250 mm** |
| Bath, toilet, kitchen, storage, laundry ceiling | ≥ **2100 mm** |
| R-occupancy corridors **inside** a dwelling/sleeping unit | ≥ **2100 mm** (aligns with SBC 1101/1102) |
| One dwelling room | ≥ **11.2 m²** net; other habitable rooms ≥ **6.5 m²** (kitchens no min area) |
| Efficiency unit living room | ≥ **17.7 m²**; separate closet; separate bathroom (WC, lav, tub or shower); 750 mm clear at sink/cooktop/fridge unless Accessible / Type A / Type B |

Sloped ceilings: prescribed height over ≥ **½** the area; floor under **< 1500 mm** height does not count toward 1208.3 area. Mezzanines: Section 505. Crawl access **450 × 600 mm**; attic access **560 × 760 mm** where clear height > **760 mm**.

**SD takeaway:** Habitable rooms are a 2100 mm plan cylinder with 2250 mm ceilings; efficiency flats need a 17.7 m² living room plus a separate bathroom.

### 6. Toilet and bathroom finishes (1210)
Fixture **counts**: Chapter 29. Other than dwelling units: floor smooth, hard, non-absorbent; wall base ≥ **100 mm** (charging 1210.2.1; commentary also says 150 mm). Walls within **600 mm** of service sinks, urinals, WCs: same finish to **≥ 1200 mm** (not dwelling/sleeping units; not private single-WC rooms). Showers and tubs with shower heads: non-absorbent to **≥ 1800 mm** above the drain (aligns with SBC 1101/1102). Public restrooms visually screened from the door; each public/employee WC in a compartment; urinal screens from ≤ **300 mm** AFF to ≥ **1500 mm**, projecting ≥ **450 mm** or **150 mm** beyond the lip.

**SD takeaway:** Public toilets are hard-floor with 100 mm base, 1200 mm wet walls, and 1800 mm shower surrounds — not painted gypsum in the splash zone.
