# SBC 201 Chapter 4 Special Occupancy and Use
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
2024 | SBCNC

> Scope note: Chapter 4 is **supplemental** — these special occupancies and uses apply **in addition to** the rest of SBC 201, and where this chapter sets height, area, or construction rules they **supersede** Chapters 5–6 (example: open-parking Table 406.5.4 vs Tables 504.3 / 504.4 / 506.2). It is **not** occupancy classification (Ch. 3), mixed-use options (508), general egress (Ch. 10), or fire-protection thresholds (Ch. 9). Hazardous-material operations, hangars, fuel dispensing, and emergency planning are **SBC 801**. Garage/lab/spray exhaust and domestic cooking hoods: **SBC 501**. Floor drains: **SBC 701**. Hazardous exhaust/piping also **SBC 701**. EVCS accessibility geometry: commentary points to **ICC A117.1 (2017)**; this chapter’s bibliography lists **ICC/ANSI A117.1-09**. Storm shelters when 423 applies: **ICC 500**.

## Regulatory Overview
Section 401.1 applies Chapter 4 only to the occupancies and uses described herein; all other code provisions still apply except as this chapter modifies them. A covered mall that fully complies with the rest of the code need not use Section 402, but use-specific packages (atrium, high-rise, H, stages, amusement, control areas) cannot be skipped by that route.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Covered and open malls (402)
Applies only if the mall does **not exceed three floor levels at any point nor three stories above grade plane**. Foyers/lobbies of B, R-1, R-2 are out. Open-mall **perimeter line** (402.1.1) encircles the mall complex; **anchor buildings and parking structures sit outside** that line.

| Rule | Lock |
|------|------|
| Open space (402.2) | **18 m** on all sides of mall + attached anchors + parking garages. Reduce to **12 m** on ≤ **75%** of perimeter if: 3-hour exterior wall + 3-hour opening protectives, and **no Group E, H, I or R** in mall or anchors |
| Unlimited area (402.4.1.1) | Type **I, II, III or IV**; ≤ 3 stories; Table 504.3 **metre** height still applies |
| Anchor height/area (402.4.1.2) | Separate building under 503/504/506. Exception: unlimited area if ≤ 3 stories and Type I–IV |
| Tenant vs mall | **No** fire-resistance-rated separation to the mall or food court (402.4.2). Tenant-to-tenant: **fire partitions** (708); not required to the mall |
| Anchor separation (402.4.2.2) | **Fire wall** (706). Exception 1: ≤ 3 stories and occupancy same as mall tenants → **2-hour fire barriers**. Exception 2 (open mall): exterior walls vs open mall per Table 705.5 |
| Parking attached (402.4.2.3) | Treat as separate building if **2-hour** fire barriers and/or horizontal assemblies; walkways/tunnels to mall/anchor → **Section 3104** |
| Open mall void (402.4.3) | Unroofed opening **≥ 6 m** clear, grade to sky; balconies shall not project into that width. Pedestrian walkways connecting balconies **≥ 6 m** from any other walkway (402.4.3.1) |
| Sprinklers (402.5) | NFPA 13 throughout mall and connected buildings. Mall sprinklers **independent** of tenants/anchors. Exception: separated open parking per 402.4.2.3 / 406.5 |
| Mall width (402.8.1.1) | Aggregate clear egress width **≥ 6 m**; any portion of that width **≥ 3 m** to 2400 mm height between tenant projection and kiosk/obstruction |
| Tenant 2nd MOE (402.8.3) | **Two means of egress** if travel to the mall **> 23 m** (persons other than employees) **or** tenant OL **≥ 50** |
| Travel (402.8.5) | Tenant to mall or exit **≤ 60 m**. Covered mall to exit **≤ 60 m**. Open mall to perimeter line **≤ 60 m** |
| Assembly OL ≥ 500 (402.8.4) | Entrance immediately adjacent to a principal mall entrance; **≥ half** of required MOE opens **directly to the exterior**. Open-mall: main exit may open to the open mall |
| Anchor egress (402.8.4.1) | Independent of the mall; mall shall not exit through an anchor |
| Kiosks (402.6.2) | Combustible kiosks only if FRT wood / listed foam / Class A ACM; **6 m** from other structures; area **≤ 30 m²** |

Mall occupant load for mall exits uses Equation 4-1: \(OLF = (0.00007)(GLA) + 2.3\) (m²/person). Commentary range **2.8–4.65 m²/person**; charging 402.8.2.2 in this extract also says “30” and “50” (imperial leftover — use the SI equation and commentary). Food-court OL (1004) is **added** to mall OL. Anchor OL is **not**.

**SD takeaway:** A 402 mall is a three-story, Type I–IV, 18 m (or 12 m/3-hour) open-space diagram with a 6 m mall void and tenant second exits at 23 m or 50 occupants — lock that before leasing the plan.

### 2. High-rise buildings (403)
**Definition used here (commentary to 403.1 / Ch. 2):** occupied floors **more than 23 m** above the lowest level of fire department vehicle access.

**Not** high-rise under 403: ATC towers (412.2); open parking (406.5); Group A-5 portions (303.6); special industrial (503.1.1); H-1; specified H-2/H-3 detached or process buildings (415.8, 415.9.2, 415.9.3, 426.1).

| Height trigger | Package |
|----------------|---------|
| Occupied floor **> 23 m** | Full 403: NFPA 13 throughout (403.3); smokeproof interior exit stairs serving those floors (403.5.4 / 909.20 / 1023.12); fire command center (911); EVAC (907.5.2.2); standpipes (905.3); smoke detection (907.2.13.1); SBC 801 §510 in-building responder radio; luminous path markings (1025) for A, B, E, I, M, R-1 |
| Occupied floor **> 36 m** | **≥ 2 fire service access elevators** (or all elevators, whichever is less), each **≥ 1600 kg**, Section 3007 + stretcher 3002.4. Type **IV-A / IV-B** above 36 m: fire pumps from **two water mains on different streets** (403.3.2) |
| Building height **> 128 m** | No 403.2.1 rating reductions. Extra interior exit stair (not R-2 + ancillary), sized so remaining stairs with **one removed** still meet 1005.1 — **or** occupant-evacuation elevators (3008). Sprinkler zone **≥ 2 risers** (alternate floors). Fire pumps: two street mains. Stair/elevator enclosure **structural integrity** (403.2.2) also for Risk Category III/IV at any high-rise height. SFRM bond **48 kN/m²** (vs 21 kN/m² for 23–128 m) |

Stair remoteness (403.5.1): charging text — not less than **10 m** **or** **¼ of the maximum overall diagonal**, **whichever is less**, measured enclosure-to-enclosure. Commentary in this extract repeatedly uses **9 m** (and ¼ diagonal when diagonal < 36 m). Scissor/interlocking stairs count as **one**. Only **two** of three-plus stairs must meet this.

Fire-resistance reductions (403.2.1), only with per-floor supervised valves/waterflow, and **not** above 128 m: Type IA → IB ratings (columns supporting floors **not** reduced); Type IB → IIA ratings except F-1, H-2, H-3, H-5, M, S-1. Other shafts (not exit stairs/elevators) may drop to **1 hour** if sprinklered at top and alternate floors.

Post-fire smoke **removal** (403.4.7) is **not** Section 909 smoke control: operable panels **4 m² per 15 m** of perimeter at **≤ 15 m** spacing, or 1 air change / 15 min exhaust to exterior.

**SD takeaway:** Occupied floor above 23 m locks a smokeproof-stair, sprinklered high-rise core; above 36 m lock two FSAE; above 128 m lock a third stair (or 3008 elevators) and two-riser / two-main water.

### 3. Aircraft-related occupancies and ATC towers (412)
ATC under 412.2 **only** if limited to cab, equipment, radar/electronics, incidental offices, and employee lounges. Those towers are **exempt from 403** even when cab height would otherwise be high-rise.

| ATC lock (412.2) | Value |
|------------------|-------|
| Height to **cab floor** (Table 412.2.1.1) | IA unlimited; IB **73 m**; IIA **30 m**; IIB **26 m**; IIIA **20 m**. Types IV and V **not** permitted |
| Cab **> 23 m** above FD access | Stair/hoistway integrity 403.2.2; SFRM bond 403.2.3; fire command center (911) with listed exceptions (may be in adjacent interdependent building; room **≥ 14 m²**, min dimension **3000 mm**) |
| Occupied floor **> 10.5 m** | NFPA 13 throughout |
| Single exit (any height) | OL per floor **≤ 15** **and** area per floor **≤ 140 m²**; then Class A or B interior finish |
| Exit access from cab | Single path **≤ 30 m**; cab stair need not be enclosed. Smokeproof exit stairs (909.20). Stair to roof (1011.12) **not** required |
| Two exits (sprinklered) | Separation **≥ ¼** of maximum overall dimension (412.2.2.3.2) |
| Accessibility | Cab and floor immediately below cab: Ch. 11 / 1104.4 Exception 3 |

Hangars (412.3): exterior walls **< 9 m** from lot line or public way → **2-hour**. Basements: Type IA floor, no openings to hangar, access from outside only. Heating in **2-hour** rooms with vestibule (exceptions for high unit heaters). Suppression group from Table 412.3.6 / **NFPA 409** (door height **> 8.5 m** forces Group I hangar). Helistops: **NFPA 418** + SBC 801 Ch. 20.

**SD takeaway:** An ATC cab is a 412 tower, not a 403 high-rise — lock cab height vs Table 412.2.1.1 and the 140 m² / 15-person single-stair box before drawing the shaft.

### 4. Atriums (404)
**Not permitted in Group H.** Vertical openings that already comply with 712.1.1–712.1.3 or 712.1.9–712.1.14 are not atriums under 404.

| Rule | Lock |
|------|------|
| Sprinklers (404.3) | **Throughout the entire building.** Adjacent/above the atrium may omit sprinklers if separated by **2-hour** barriers/horizontal assemblies. **Ceiling sprinklers in the atrium optional** if atrium ceiling is **more than 16 m** above the floor (commentary: 16.5 m) |
| Smoke control (404.5 / 909) | Required. Exceptions: (1) not I-2 or I-1 Condition 2, atrium connects **only two** stories; (2) only lowest **two** stories open to atrium and stories above separated as a **shaft** (713.4) |
| Enclosure (404.6) | **1-hour** fire barrier / horizontal assembly, unless: sprinkler-protected glass wall (heads **100–300 mm** from glass, **≤ 1800 mm** o.c.); ¾-hour glass block; up to **three** floors open if accounted for in 909; no smoke control required; escalator/exit-access-stair exceptions |
| Atrium floor use (404.2) | Low fire-hazard only (walk-through, reception) unless that floor area is itself NFPA 13 sprinklered. Decorations: **SBC 801** |
| Interior finish | Class **B**; sprinkler reduction **not** allowed |
| Exit stairs in atrium (404.10) | Entry = closest riser; access from **two directions**; **≤ 50%** of exit stairs in the same atrium; remoteness vs an enclosed 1023.2 stair per 1007.1.1 |

Standby power for smoke-control equipment: 909.11.

**SD takeaway:** An atrium is a sprinklered-building + 909 problem, banned in H; skip ceiling sprinklers only if the void is taller than 16 m, and skip 909 only for a two-story (non-I-2 / non-I-1 Condition 2) void.

### 5. Underground buildings and motor-vehicle occupancies (405, 406)
**Underground (405)** — occupied floor **more than 9 m below** the finished floor of the lowest level of exit discharge. Exempt: 13D dwellings; sprinklered parking per 405.3; transit; grandstands/arenas; lowest story only, **≤ 140 m²** and OL **< 10**; limited mechanical spaces.

| Underground lock | Value |
|------------------|-------|
| Construction | Underground portion **Type I** |
| Sprinklers | Highest LED serving underground **and all levels below** (903.3.1.1, supervised) |
| Compartments (405.4.1) | Floor **> 18 m** below LED: **≥ two** approx. equal smoke compartments through LED and below |
| Exits | **≥ 2** per floor; each compartment: **≥ 1 exit** + **≥ 1** door to the adjoining compartment |
| Stairs **> 9 m** below their LED | Smokeproof enclosure (1023.12) |
| Smoke control | 909; independent system per 405.4 compartment |

**Parking / MV (406)**

| Item | Lock |
|------|------|
| Clear height (406.2.2) | Vehicle/pedestrian areas **≥ 2100 mm**. Fuel canopies: **4150 mm** to lowest projecting element (406.7.2); 406.2.2 commentary also states 4050 mm |
| Accessible parking | Section **1106**. EVCS: NFPA 70 / SBC 401, UL 2202 / UL 2594; accessible EVCS scoping **1107**; commentary: technical criteria in **ICC A117.1 (2017)** |
| No openings | Between any MV occupancy and a **sleeping room** (406.2.5) |
| Private garage (406.3) | Group U; each **≤ 100 m²**; multiple garages: **1-hour** barriers between. Dwelling separation: 12.5 mm GWB garage side; garage under habitable: **16 mm Type X**; doors 35 mm solid / 20-min, self-closing; **not** into sleeping rooms |
| Open parking (406.5) | Type **I, II or IV**. Openings: **≥ 20%** of perimeter wall area per tier **and** aggregate opening length **≥ 40%** of perimeter (waived if two opposing sides). Interior walls **≥ 20%** open |
| Table 406.5.4 (single-use open parking only) | **IA:** unlimited area/height. **IB:** unlimited area; **12** ramp tiers (**18** mechanical + sprinkler). **IIA:** **4645 m²**/tier, **10** ramp / **15** mech+sprinkler. **IIB:** **4645 m²**, **8** / **12**. **IV:** **4645 m²**, **4** tiers. Type II, all sides open, height **≤ 23 m** → unlimited area |
| Enclosed parking (406.6) | Height/area from **504/506** (not Table 406.5.4). Mechanical ventilation **SBC 501**. Sprinklers per **903.2.10** (commentary: enclosed fire area **> 1100 m²** or under occupancies other than R-3; open parking fire area **> 4460 m²** unless 507.3) |
| Vehicle barriers (406.4.2) | **≥ 850 mm** high where drop **> 300 mm** |
| Vehicle ramps as exits | Only with pedestrian facilities; combined parking/circulation slope **≤ 1:15** |

**SD takeaway:** Occupied floor more than 9 m below LED is a Type I, sprinklered, 909 underground building; an “open” garage is a 20%/40% opening diagram plus Table 406.5.4 — miss the openings and you are in enclosed S-2 with Ch. 5 areas and SBC 501 exhaust.

### 6. Group I-2 smoke compartments (407)
Defend-in-place: every story used for care, treatment or sleeping — and other stories with OL **≥ 50** — subdivided into **≥ two** smoke compartments (709).

| I-2 lock | Value |
|----------|-------|
| Compartment area (407.5.1) | **≤ 2100 m²**. I-2 Condition 2: **≤ 3700 m²** if all sleeping rooms are **single-patient** (and suites comply) **or** the compartment has **no** patient sleeping rooms |
| Travel to smoke-barrier door | **≤ 60 m** (also 60 m to an exit) |
| Refuge area (407.5.3) | **2.8 m²** per bed/stretcher care recipient; **0.56 m²** per ambulatory care recipient and other occupants. Size for the **largest adjoining** compartment |
| Independent egress (407.5.4) | Leave each compartment without returning through the origin compartment; if no exit in the compartment → direct access to **≥ two** adjacent compartments |
| Corridors | Smoke **partitions** (710), not fire-rated. Habitable rooms: door **directly to corridor** (exceptions: grade exit; care suites) |
| Sleeping-room travel to corridor door | **≤ 15 m** (not in a care suite) |
| Care suites | Separated by smoke partitions. Travel in suite to corridor/horizontal-exit door **≤ 30 m**. Sleeping suites **≤ 700 m²** (**900 m²** with suite-wide smoke detection); **2** exits if **> 100 m²**. Non-sleeping suites **≤ 1200 m²** (**1400 m²** with detection); **2** exits if **> 230 m²** |
| Corridor furniture (I-2 Condition 1) | Corridor **≥ 2400 mm**; furniture one side; clear **≥ 1800 mm**; each grouping **≤ 4.5 m²**; **≥ 3 m** apart |
| Sprinklers in sleeping compartments | NFPA 13 + **quick-response / residential** (903.3.2) |

**SD takeaway:** Draw I-2 as paired smoke compartments ≤ 2100 m² (3700 m² only in the listed Condition 2 cases) with 60 m to the barrier door and 2.8 m² bed refuge — not as a single floor plate with only vertical exits.

### 7. Stages and special amusement (410, 411)
Pedestrian walkways are **not** a Chapter 4 section; mall/garage connectors use **3104**, and open-mall balcony walkways need **6 m** mutual separation (402.4.3.1).

| Stages (410) | Threshold |
|--------------|-----------|
| Area includes performance + unseparated backstage | Height = stage floor to roof/floor deck above |
| Proscenium wall **2-hour**, foundation to roof | Stage height **> 15 m** |
| Emergency ventilation (roof vents **≥ 5%** of stage area, ≥ 2 vents, or 909 keeping smoke **≥ 1.8 m** above seating / proscenium) | Stage **> 100 m²** **or** height **> 15 m** |
| Sprinklers under roof, gridiron, catwalks, galleries, dressing/shops | Required except: (2) stage **≤ 100 m²** **and** height **≤ 15 m** with no vertically retractable combustibles (single main curtain/borders/legs/backdrop only); understage < 1.2 m chair storage behind 16 mm Type X |
| Appurtenant rooms vs stage | **2-hour** if stage height > 15 m; **1-hour** if ≤ 15 m. Rooms vs each other: **1-hour** |
| Two+ exits from stage | **≥ one** exit/door **each side** |
| Technical production egress | One MOE minimum; travel **90 m** unsprinklered / **120 m** NFPA 13; common path **30 m** if two required; path width **≥ 560 mm**; ladders/alternating treads/spirals allowed |

| Amusement (411) | Lock |
|-----------------|------|
| Occupancy | OL **≥ 50** → Group A + 411; OL **< 50** → Group B + 411. Open-air, no smoke accumulation: 411 N/A |
| Sprinklers | Throughout. Temporary < **100 m²** and travel **< 15 m**: sprinklers not required |
| Finish | Class **A** (no sprinkler reduction) |
| Confused egress | Low-level exit signs + UL 1994 path markings **≤ 200 mm** above floor, activated by detection/sprinklers. Puzzle rooms: Ch. 10, approved alt, **or** exits forced open by alarm/sprinkler + attended manual control |

**SD takeaway:** A fly loft taller than 15 m is a 2-hour proscenium + ventilation/sprinkler stage; a maze with OL ≥ 50 is a sprinklered Group A with Class A finish and floor-level exit markings — not a dark-shell fit-out.

### 8. Residential separations, live/work, and ambulatory care (420, 422, 508.5)
**Section 419 in this extract is artificial decorative vegetation** (height > 1.8 m, within 1.5 m of a building or on a roof → SBC 801 §321.1), **not** live/work. Live/work is named in 420 commentary: separations **between** live/work units follow **508.5**; **no** rated split required **inside** a unit between dwelling and work.

| 420 I-1 / R-1 / R-2 / R-3 / R-4 | Lock |
|---------------------------------|------|
| Unit-to-unit and unit-to-other occupancy | **Fire partitions** (708) and **horizontal assemblies** (711): **1-hour**, or **30 minutes** if NFPA 13 throughout |
| Sprinklers | Group R: 903.2.8. Group I-1: 903.2.6. QR/residential heads: 903.3.2 |
| I-1 Condition 2 smoke compartments | Every care/sleeping story and other stories OL ≥ 50: **≥ two** compartments, each **≤ 2000 m²**, travel to barrier door **≤ 60 m**. Refuge **1.4 m²**/care recipient + **0.56 m²**/other occupant |
| R-2 dorm cooktops | Not in **sleeping rooms** (420.11.2) |

| Ambulatory care (422) — Group B with I-2-like extras | Lock |
|------------------------------------------------------|------|
| Fire partition vs adjacent tenants (708) | **Four or more** care recipients incapable of self-preservation at one time |
| Smoke compartments | Aggregate ambulatory care **> 900 m²** on a story → **≥ two** compartments, each **≤ 2000 m²**, travel to barrier door **≤ 60 m** |
| Refuge | **2.8 m²** per non-ambulatory care recipient; access without passing through adjacent tenants |
| Sprinklers (903.2.2 via 422.4) | **Four or more** incapable of self-preservation, **or** any incapable recipient **not on the level of exit discharge** — then the whole such floor (and floors below if not on LED) |

**SD takeaway:** R/I-1 unit walls are 1-hour (30 min with 13) on day one; day-surgery is still Group B until four incapable patients or a non-LED treatment floor — those two triggers lock 708 partitions, 900 m² smoke splits, and 903.2.2 sprinklers.

### 9. Control areas and Group H (414, 415)
Stay under Table 307.1(1)/(2) MAQ **per control area** and the building is **not** Group H. Control-area design is Table 414.2.2 (extract OCR-compressed; percentages are of Ch. 3 MAQ, including sprinkler/cabinet increases).

| Floor (above grade) | % of MAQ per control area | Max control areas / floor | Fire-barrier hours |
|---------------------|---------------------------|---------------------------|--------------------|
| Higher than 9 | 5 | 1 | 2 |
| 7–9 | 5 | 2 | 2 |
| 4–6 | 12.5 | 2 | 2 |
| 3 | 50 | 2 | 1 |
| 2 | 75 | 3 | 1 |
| 1 | 100 | 4 | 1 |
| 1 below grade | 75 | 3 | 1 |
| 2 below grade | 50 | 2 | 1 |
| Lower than 2 below | Not allowed | — | — |

Floor of the control area and its support: **2-hour** (exception: 1-hour in IIA/IIIA/IV/VA, NFPA 13, **≤ 3 stories**). Fire walls (706) reset the building for counting control areas. Higher-education labs that use 428 are **outside** 414.2.

**Group H SD locks (415)** — only when MAQ is exceeded:

| Occupancy | Site / building lock |
|-----------|----------------------|
| H-1 | **Detached**, not mixed; setback **≥ 23 m** (and SBC 801). Lightweight insulated roof |
| H-2 > 100 m² (not detached-required) | Setback **≥ 9 m** |
| H-2 / H-3 requiring detached building (Table 415.6.5) | Setback **≥ 15 m** |
| H-2 and H-3 | **≥ 25%** of the occupancy perimeter shall be an **exterior wall** (small interior liquid rooms/spray booths excepted) |
| All H | Sprinklers per 903.2.5; detection 907.2.5; explosion control Table 414.5.1 |

**SD takeaway:** Keep hazardous quantities inside Table 414.2.2 control areas (four at grade, collapsing with height) or the project becomes detached H with 9–23 m setbacks — decide that before the massing.

### 10. Higher education laboratories (428)
Optional package with **SBC 801 Ch. 38** to exceed 414 control-area MAQs **without** Group H. Entire building NFPA 13. Laboratory suites separated per Table 428.3 (extract OCR-compressed). Supporting floor **2-hour** (1-hour exception: IIA/IIIA/VA, **≤ 3 stories**). Liquid-tight floors where hazmat is present. Ventilation: **NFPA 45 Ch. 7** + SBC 501. Standby/emergency power (414.5.2) if suites are **above the sixth story** or **below grade**. Combined number of lab suites **plus** control areas cannot exceed Table 428.3 suite caps. Not allowed above **floor 21** (table: 21+ not permitted).

**SD takeaway:** A university wet lab that needs more than 414 MAQ is a 428 suite building — sprinklered, 2-hour floors, and no lab suites above the 20th floor — not a late hazardous-materials overlay.
