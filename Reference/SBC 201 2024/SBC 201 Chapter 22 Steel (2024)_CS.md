# SBC 201 Chapter 22 Steel
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
2024 | SBCNC

> Scope note: This chapter governs **quality, design, fabrication, and erection of steel construction** (2201.1) as a road map to companion standards. Structural steel → **SBC 306**; cold-formed carbon/low-alloy → **SBC 308**; cold-formed light-frame → **SBC 309**. **SBC 307 is not cited** in this extract. Fire-resistance of steel assemblies is **Chapter 7**, not here.

## Regulatory Overview
Identification and painting/corrosion protection of structural steel follow **SBC 306**; cold-formed members **SBC 308**; light-frame also **SBC 309** (2202–2203). Welding and high-strength bolting point to the design sections below and to special inspection **§1705.2**.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Companion map (2205–2211)
| System | Design standard in this chapter |
|--------|----------------------------------|
| Structural steel (2205.1) | **SBC 306** |
| Composite steel + concrete (2206.1) | **SBC 306** and **SBC 304**, excluding SBC 304 Chapter 14 |
| Open-web joists (2207.1) | SJI 100 or SJI 200 |
| Steel cables (2208.1) | ASCE 19 |
| Storage racks (2209) | RMI ANSI/MH 16.1 (cantilever: MH 16.3); seismic where required by **SBC 301** §15.5.3. Certificate of compliance if rack **≥ 2400 mm** to top load level in SDC **D, E or F** (2209.3) |
| Cold-formed carbon/low-alloy (2210.1) | **SBC 308**; stainless: ASCE 8 |
| Cold-formed light-frame (2211.1) | **SBC 309** (floors/roofs, structural walls, shear/strap walls, trusses). Nonstructural members: **SBC 308** and **309** (2211.2) |

**SD takeaway:** Hot-rolled frame is SBC 306; metal studs/deck are 308/309 — **SBC 307 is not named here**.

### 2. Seismic and light-frame locks (2205.2, 2211.1.1–2211.1.2)
SDC **B or C** structural steel: any construction permitted in 2205; if \(R\) from **SBC 301** Table 12.2-1 is used, detail to **SBC 306 Chapter 12**, except “steel systems not specifically detailed for seismic resistance” may use SBC 306 without AISC 341. SDC **D, E or F**: **SBC 306 Chapter 12** mandatory except as permitted in **SBC 301** Table 15.4-1. Composite systems using \(R\) from Table 12.2-1: **SBC 306 Chapter 12** (no SDC split except SDC A in commentary).

Cold-formed light-frame SDC B/C: AISI S400 if \(R\) is used, with the same “not specifically detailed” exception via **SBC 309**. SDC D–F: AISI S400. **Prescriptive:** detached one- and two-family dwellings and townhouses **≤ 3 stories** above grade plane may use **AISI S230** subject to its limits (2211.1.2). Cold-formed trusses spanning **≥ 18 m**: RDP for temporary and permanent bracing; SI per **§1705.2** (2211.1.3.2).

**SD takeaway:** A steel or CFS seismic frame is an SDC/R choice with the structural engineer at SD; ≤ 3-story townhouse CFS may be prescriptive AISI S230, not a moment frame.
