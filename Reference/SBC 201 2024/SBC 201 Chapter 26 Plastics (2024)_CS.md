# SBC 201 Chapter 26 Plastics
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
2024 | SBCNC

> Scope note: This chapter governs **foam plastic, plastic veneer, interior plastic finish/trim, light-transmitting plastics and plastic composites** (2601.1). Exterior wall covering/trim still **Chapter 14**. Interior finish classes still **Chapter 8**. Opening % vs fire-separation distance still **§705.8**. Do not dump ASTM foam-spec tables.

## Regulatory Overview
Plastics are combustible; this chapter is the permission slip and the area/height cap. Foam in Type I–IV walls of any height is an **NFPA 285** wall assembly unless a 2603.5.5 exception applies.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Foam plastic insulation — thermal barrier and NFPA 285 (2603)
Surface burning unless excepted: FSI **≤ 75**, SDI **≤ 450**, ASTM E84 / UL 723, maximum thickness intended for use (2603.3). **Thermal barrier** to the interior: **12.5 mm** gypsum wallboard, heavy timber per **§602.4**, or NFPA 275 (both tests). Concealed spaces: **§718**. Prescriptive thermal-barrier omissions: 2603.4.1.1–2603.4.1.14 (e.g. **≥ 25 mm** masonry/concrete each face).

**Exterior walls, Type I, II, III or IV, any height (2603.5):** 2603.5.1–2603.5.7. Type V: 2603.2–2603.4. Foam, coatings and facings each FSI **≤ 25** / SDI **≤ 450** (tested ≤ 100 mm). **Vertical and lateral fire propagation: NFPA 285** (2603.5.5). Exceptions: one-story buildings per 2603.4.1.4; or foam covered each face by **≥ 25 mm** masonry/concrete with no airspace, or FSI **≤ 25** and airspace **≤ 25 mm**. Ignition: NFPA 268 unless an exterior protective in 2603.5.7 applies (thermal barrier; **25 mm** concrete/masonry; **10 mm** GFRC; metal-faced panels; **20 mm** stucco; **6 mm** fiber-cement). Potential heat: NFPA 259, not more than the NFPA 285-tested wall (2603.5.3).

**SD takeaway:** Foam in a Type I–IV rainscreen is an NFPA 285 assembly plus a 12.5 mm interior thermal barrier — not a cladding swap on SD.

### 2. Plastic veneer (2605)
Interior: Chapter 8. Exterior (other than plastic siding): CC1 or CC2 per 2606.4; **not higher than 15 m** above grade; sections **≤ 28 m²**, separated **≥ 1.2 m** vertically. Exception: Type **VB** walls not required to be rated — area, separation and smoke-density limits do not apply. Plastic siding: **§1403** and **§1404**.

**SD takeaway:** Plastic veneer stops at 15 m and 28 m² panels unless the building is unrated Type VB.

### 3. Light-transmitting plastics — walls, glazing, skylights (2606–2610)
Approved plastic: self-ignition **≥ 343 °C** (ASTM D1929); SDI **≤ 450** or ASTM D2843 smoke density **≤ 75**; Class **CC1** (burn extent **≤ 25 mm**) or **CC2** (burn rate **≤ 1 mm/s**) per ASTM D635 (2606.4). Bathroom plastic glazing: also Chapter 24 safety glazing (2606.9). Light-diffusing systems prohibited in listed A / I-2 / I-3 / exit locations unless the building is 903.3.1.1 sprinklered (2606.7).
