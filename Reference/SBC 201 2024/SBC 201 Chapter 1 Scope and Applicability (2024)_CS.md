# SBC 201 Chapter 1 Scope and Applicability
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
2024 | SBCNC

> Scope note: Part 1 (§§101–102) governs **when SBC 201 applies**, which companion codes are part of the suite, and how conflicts are resolved. It is **not** occupancy, height/area, or construction type (Chs. 3–6). Administration and enforcement (§§103–116) are skipped here. Detached one- and two-family dwellings and townhouses **≤ 3 stories** above grade plane with a separate means of egress, and their ≤ 3-story accessory structures, leave this code for **SBC 1101/1102**.

## Regulatory Overview
The official text is the **English** edition with **SI units**, published by SBCNC; three editions exist (CC, CR, AR). Where sections conflict, the specific requirement governs a general one, and the **most restrictive** of differing methods or materials governs; this code (and the 101.4 suite) takes precedence over referenced standards.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Official text and editions (101.1–101.1.3)
| Item | Lock |
|------|------|
| Title | General Saudi Building Code, **SBC 201** (“this code”) |
| Official version | **English language**, **SI units**, SBCNC |
| Editions | **I** Code and commentary (**SBC 201-CC**); **II** Code Requirements (**SBC 201-CR**); **III** Code Summary Requirements in Arabic (**SBC 201-AR**) |
| CC | Most comprehensive formal edition |

**SD takeaway:** Design and cite from the English SI CC/CR text; do not treat the Arabic summary as the charging edition.

### 2. Scope and dwelling exception (101.2)
Applies to construction, alteration, relocation, enlargement, replacement, repair, equipment, use and occupancy, location, maintenance, removal and demolition of every building or structure and attached appurtenances.

**Exception:** Detached one- and two-family dwellings and multiple single-family dwellings (townhouses) **not more than three stories** above grade plane with a **separate means of egress**, and their accessory structures **not more than three stories** above grade plane, shall comply with **SBC 1101/1102**.

Commentary townhouse tests for that exception: ≤ 3 stories; separate MOE; each unit foundation-to-roof; open space on at least two sides. Four stories and up are beyond SBC 1101/1102 and stay on SBC 201. Commentary also names SBC 1101/1102 paths that pull some SBC 201 buildings into the residential codes: live/work (508.5); owner-occupied B&B with **five or fewer** guestrooms (310.4.2); care facilities in a dwelling with **five or fewer** persons (305.2.2, 308.2.4, 308.3.2, 308.5.4, 310.4.1).

**SD takeaway:** Villas, detached duplexes, and ≤ 3-story townhouses with their own exits are SBC 1101/1102 buildings — apartments, hotels, and four-story dwellings are not.

### 3. Appendices (101.2.1)
Provisions in the appendices shall **not apply unless specifically adopted**. Commentary: Appendices A through M are not part of the code unless included in the adopting law.

**SD takeaway:** Do not design to an appendix (fire district, qualifications, etc.) unless the AHJ has adopted it.

### 5. Conflicts, standards, existing buildings (102.1–102.6)
| Rule | Effect |
|------|--------|
| 102.1 | Specific over general. Differing materials/methods in this code → **most restrictive** governs |
| 102.2 | Does not nullify other government law |
| 102.4 / 102.4.1 | Referenced codes/standards are enforceable to the extent cited; **this code applies** where it conflicts with them |
| 102.4.2 | Where a referenced standard covers matter in this code or the 101.4 codes, **this code / 101.4 codes take precedence** |
| 102.6 | Legal occupancy of existing structures may continue except as this code, **SBC 901**, or **SBC 801** requires |
| 102.6.1 | Never occupied → treat as **new** under SBC 201 or SBC 1101/1102 (or current permit) |

**SD takeaway:** If NFPA or ASHRAE text fights SBC 201 or a 101.4 companion, the Saudi code wins; among two SBC 201 options, use the stricter.
