# SBC 201 Chapter 24 Glass and Glazing
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
2024 | SBCNC

> Scope note: This chapter governs **glass, light-transmitting ceramic, and light-transmitting plastic panels** in vertical and sloped use (2401.1). Plastic glazing also meets **Chapter 26**. Wind/seismic glass pointing is **§1609** and **SBC 301 §13.5.9** — not a thickness table in this CS. Safety-glazing **locations** are the architect lock.

## Regulatory Overview
Every pane in a 2406.4 hazardous location is safety glazing (CPSC 16 CFR Part 1201 Category II unless the tables relax it). Wind and dead-load thickness is ASTM E1300 against Chapter 16; this chapter does not pick the millimetre for you.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Wind / structural pointing (2403, 2404)
Vertical glass (slope **≤ 15°** from vertical): wind as components and cladding per **§1609**; load resistance ASTM E1300; Equation 24-1. Glazed curtain walls, storefronts and partitions: seismic **SBC 301 §13.5.9**. Sloped glass (**> 15°** from vertical): Equations 24-2 to 24-5. Framing deflection: **1/175** of edge length (edge **≤ 4050 mm**) or **1/240 + 6.25 mm** (edge **> 4050 mm**) under the larger combined load from **§1605** (2403.3). Unsupported edges: RDP analysis (2403.2). Interior adjacent panes: differential deflection **≤ pane thickness** at **730 N/m** applied up to **1.0 m** AFF (2403.4).

**SD takeaway:** Size glass to §1609 / ASTM E1300 on the structural sheet; keep four-side support or hire the analysis.

### 2. Safety glazing — hazardous locations (2406.4)
Default test: **CPSC 16 CFR Part 1201 Category II**. Exception: glazing **not** in doors or hot-tub / whirlpool / sauna / steam / bathtub / shower enclosures may use **ANSI Z97.1 Class A** unless Table 2406.2(2) says otherwise (2406.2). Mirrors with continuous backing: not safety-glazed (2406.1 exception). Louvered/jalousie: 2403.5, not a 2406 hazardous location. Glass block: **§2110**.

| Location | Trigger | Key exceptions | Section |
|----------|---------|----------------|---------|
| Doors | All fixed and operable panels of swinging, sliding and bifold doors | Opening a **75 mm** sphere cannot pass; decorative; curved revolving-door panels; commercial refrigerated-cabinet doors | 2406.4.1 |
| Adjacent to doors | Nearest vertical edge within **600 mm** arc of either door edge **and** bottom exposed edge **< 1.5 m** AFF | Decorative; intervening wall/barrier; closet/storage **≤ 900 mm** deep (then 2406.4.3); latch-side wall perpendicular to the door in 1- and 2-family or R-2 dwelling units | 2406.4.2 |
| Windows (large lites) | **All** of: pane **> 0.85 m²**; bottom **< 450 mm** AFF; top **> 900 mm** AFF; walking surface within **900 mm** horizontally | Decorative; rail **850–950 mm** AFF, **730 N/m**, **≥ 40 mm** high; outboard IGU pane if bottom **≥ 7.5 m** above grade/roof/walking surface | 2406.4.3 |
| Guards and railings | Structural balusters **and** in-fill, **any** area or height | None | 2406.4.4 |
| Wet surfaces | Walls/enclosures/fences containing or facing hot tubs, spas, whirlpools, saunas, steam rooms, bathtubs, showers, indoor/outdoor pools; bottom **< 1.5 m** above standing/walking surface — **all** panes | More than **1.5 m** horizontally from water’s edge of bathtub, hot tub, spa, whirlpool or pool | 2406.4.5 |
| Stairs and ramps | Bottom **< 1.5 m** above the walking surface of stairways, landings between flights, ramps | Guard per **§1015** and **§1607.9** with glass **> 450 mm** from the rail; **or** **≥ 900 mm** horizontally from the walking surface | 2406.4.6 |
| Bottom stair landing | **< 1.5 m** AFF **and** within a **1.5 m** horizontal arc **< 180°** from the bottom-tread nosing | Guard per 1015 / 1607.9 with glass **> 450 mm** from the guard | 2406.4.7 |

FD access panels: tempered; all panes of an IGU tempered (2406.5).

**SD takeaway:** Doors, 600 mm sidelights, wet rooms to 1.5 m, guards, and stair glass are safety glazing on day one — not a shop-drawing swap.

### 3. Glass in handrails and guards (2407)
Laminated fully tempered or heat-strengthened; Category II / Class A; nominal thickness **≥ 6.5 mm** (2407.1). Loads: **§1607.9**, glass factor of safety **four**. Structural glass balusters: top rail/handrail on **≥ 3** balusters (or otherwise stay up if one fails). Exception: laminated **≥ 2** equal plies, ASTM E2353 remains a barrier after breakage. **No** glass in parking-garage rails except pedestrian areas not exposed to vehicles (2407.1.3). Wind-borne debris regions: laminated Category II / Class A; top-rail-on-glass also **§1609.2** (2407.1.4).

**SD takeaway:** A glass guard is laminated 6.5 mm Category II with a top rail unless the ASTM E2353 laminated exception is the design.

### 4. Sloped glazing and skylights (2405)
Applies at slope **> 15°** from vertical. Allowable materials: laminated (**≥ 0.7 mm** PVB or equivalent), wired, Chapter 26 plastic, heat-strengthened, fully tempered (2405.2). Screens under annealed / HS / FT / wired unless an exception (laminated and plastic skip screens). Types I and II: noncombustible frames (acid-fume exception: pressure-treated wood or noncorrosive). Slope **< 45°** from horizontal: curb **≥ 100 mm** above the roof plane; not in the roof plane if pitch **< 45°**. R-3 exception: no curb if roof **≥ 14°** (3:12). Unit skylights / TDDs: AAMA/WDMA/CSA 101/I.S.2/A440 (2405.5). Plastic skylights: **§2610**.

**SD takeaway:** Skylights on a roof under 45° sit on a 100 mm curb; fully tempered over occupied space needs a screen unless a 2405.3 exception fits.
