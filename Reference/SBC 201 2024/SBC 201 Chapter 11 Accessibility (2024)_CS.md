# SBC 201 Chapter 11 Accessibility
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
2024 | SBCNC

> Scope note: This chapter is **scoping** — what, where, and how many accessible features. Technical dimensions are **ICC A117.1**; General Comments to Section 1102 name the **2009** edition. Charging Section 1102.1 does not state a year. Commentary discusses 2017 anthropometry revisions — that is commentary, not a substitution of the 2009 reference. Detached one- and two-family dwellings are exempt here (1103.2.3). Existing buildings: **SBC 901** Section 306. SBC 1101/1102 points into this chapter for housing it covers; those structures are treated as Group R-3 for this chapter’s application.

## Regulatory Overview
Everything is accessible unless an exception in 1103–1112 says otherwise. Schematic locks are the accessible route to every required story, 60% of public entrances, parking counts, and the Accessible / Type A / Type B dwelling mix — not the millimetre details, which live in ICC A117.1 (2009 per this chapter’s General Comments).

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Scoping vs technical standard (1101–1102)
| Text | What it says |
|------|----------------|
| 1101.1 | This chapter controls design for accessibility |
| 1102.1 | Accessible in accordance with **this code and ICC A117.1** (no year) |
| General Comments | Section 1102 references the **2009** edition of ICC A117.1 |
| Commentary after 1102.1 | 2017 A117.1 revised turning spaces and EV stations — do not treat as the charging edition |

Items in A117.1 that Chapter 11 does not scope (Type C units, ATMs, etc.) are not required unless the building official scopes them.

**SD takeaway:** Design to Chapter 11 counts plus ICC A117.1 **2009** technical criteria as this extract ties to §1102; do not silently switch to 2017.

### 2. Major exceptions (1103.2)
Detached one- and two-family dwellings and their sites; Group U except paved public/ag work areas and private garages that contain required accessible parking; employee work areas **< 28 m²** with **≥ 175 mm** essential level change; construction sites; equipment spaces; R-1 **≤ 5** sleeping units for rent in the proprietor’s residence.

**SD takeaway:** Villas and detached duplexes skip this chapter; apartments, hotels, and offices do not.

### 3. Accessible routes and stories (1104)
≥ 1 accessible route from site arrival points to the accessible entrance, between accessible buildings on a site, and to each required accessible story, mezzanine, and occupied roof.

Exception 1 to 1104.4 (aggregate area above + below accessible levels **≤ 279 m²**) does **not** apply to: mercantile with **≥ 5** sales tenants and any floor above/below; health-care provider offices; passenger transport/airports; government buildings; structures with **≥ 4** dwelling units.

Press boxes: exception if bleacher-served aggregate ≤ **46 m²** per field, or freestanding **> 3600 mm** above grade with the same area cap.

**SD takeaway:** Four or more dwellings, clinics, government, and multi-tenant retail cannot use the 279 m² story-skip — plan a lift or ramp to every occupied level that has required accessible elements.

### 4. Entrances and power doors (1105)
≥ **60%** of public entrances shall be accessible, plus listed special entrances (garage, tunnel, tenant, dwelling unit, etc.).

**Table 1105.1.1** — at least one full-power or low-energy power-operated door at each required accessible public entrance when building occupant load exceeds:

| Occupancy | Building OL greater than |
|-----------|--------------------------|
| A-1, A-2, A-3, A-4 | 300 |
| B, M, R-1 | 500 |

Vestibules: one powered leaf in and one out. Mixed-use: most restrictive OL if the summed load exceeds the table.

**SD takeaway:** Count public doors — 60% accessible, and assembly over 300 (or B/M/hotel over 500) gets a power operator on those accessible entries.

### 5. Parking, vans, passenger loading (1106)
**Table 1106.2** (per parking facility):

| Total spaces | Min accessible |
|--------------|----------------|
| 1–25 | 1 |
| 26–50 | 2 |
| 51–75 | 3 |
| 76–100 | 4 |
| 101–150 | 5 |
| 151–200 | 6 |
| 201–300 | 7 |
| 301–400 | 8 |
| 401–500 | 9 |
| 501–1,000 | 2% of total |
| > 1,000 | 20 + 1 per 100 over 1,000 |

R-2/R-3/R-4 with Accessible/Type A/B units: **≥ 2%** (≥ 1) of **each type** of parking. Hospital outpatient: **≥ 10%**; mobility rehab / outpatient PT: **≥ 20%**. Van-accessible: **1 in 6** (or fraction) of the accessible spaces.

Group U private garages serving R-2/R-3: van vertical clearance exception **2100 mm** (charging text). Commentary describes **2050 mm** vs A117.1 **2500 mm** — report both; do not reconcile.

Passenger loading: where provided, accessible; continuous kerbside — accessible zone at least every **30 m**.

**SD takeaway:** Parking count is per facility from Table 1106.2, plus 2% residential-by-type and 1-in-6 vans; hospital outpatient is a 10% row, not the general table.

### 6. EV charging (1107)
≥ **5%** (≥ 1 of each charger type) of vehicle spaces served by EV systems shall be accessible. Vehicle space **≥ 3300 mm** wide + access aisle **≥ 1500 mm**. R-2/R-3/R-4 EV spaces are not required to comply with 1107.2.

**SD takeaway:** If the plot has public/commercial EV, reserve 5% as 3300 + 1500 stalls on an accessible route.

### 7. Dwelling and sleeping units (1108)
Units: **Accessible** (full, A117.1), **Type A** (adaptable/higher), **Type B** (FHAG-level). Type A may be built as Accessible; Type B as Accessible or Type A.

| Occupancy | Accessible / Type A | Type B |
|-----------|---------------------|--------|
| R-1 | Table 1108.6.1.1 (roll-in mix); e.g. 1–25 units → 1 Accessible | All units if 4+ used as residence (reduced only per 1108.7) |
| R-2 apartments | **> 20** units on the **site** → Type A **≥ 2%** (≥ 1), dispersed | All units if 4+ in a structure |
| R-2 dorms / boarding | Accessible per Table 1108.6.1.1 (bedrooms counted) | Same Type B rule |
| R-3 | — | 4+ as residence → all Type B |
| R-4 Condition 1 | ≥ 1 Accessible sleeping unit | Type B if 4+ |
| I-1 Cond. 1 / 2 | **4%** / **10%** Accessible | Type B if 4+ as residence |
| I-2 nursing Cond. 1 | **50%** Accessible | Type B if 4+ |
| I-2 hospitals | **10%** Accessible | Type B if 4+ |
| I-2 mobility rehab | **100%** Accessible | — |
| I-3 | **3%** Accessible (plus special-purpose cells) | Not required |

**Table 1108.6.1.1** (R-1 / R-2 congregate) includes a with/without roll-in split (e.g. 51–75 units: 3 without + 1 with = 4 Accessible). No 1108.7 reductions for Accessible units.

**SD takeaway:** R-2 over 20 units on the site needs 2% Type A; four or more dwellings in a building needs Type B throughout (unless a 1108.7 exception applies).

### 8. Assembly seating and toilets (1109, 1110)
**Table 1109.2.2.1** wheelchair spaces: 4–25 → 1; 26–50 → 2; 51–100 → 4; then stepping to **36 + 1 per 200** over 5,000. ≥ 1 companion seat per wheelchair space. Designated aisle seats **≥ 5%**.

Toilets: each toilet/bathing room accessible; clustered single-user **≥ 50%**. Family/assisted-use required for assembly/mercantile when aggregate male+female WCs **≥ 6**; travel ≤ **150 m** and ≤ 1 story from the separate-sex rooms. Drinking fountains: ≥ 2 (wheelchair + standing) or dual-spout. Check-out aisles: Table 1110.13.1 (1–4 aisles → 1 accessible).

**SD takeaway:** Mosque/theatre seating gets wheelchair spaces from Table 1109.2.2.1 plus companions; any public toilet room is accessible — do not park the only WCs on a non-accessible floor.
