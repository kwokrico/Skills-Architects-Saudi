# SBC 201 Chapter 25 Gypsum Board, Gypsum Panel Products and Plaster
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
2024 | SBCNC

> Scope note: This chapter governs **gypsum board, gypsum panel products, lath, gypsum plaster, cement plaster and reinforced gypsum concrete** (2501.1). It is **not** the fire-rating chapter — rated gypsum follows **Chapter 7**. Wet-area wall/ceiling finish also **§1210**. Exterior WRB behind stucco: **§1403.2**. Do not dump ASTM material tables.

## Regulatory Overview
Chapter 25 is installation and wet-area backing, not occupancy or construction type. Where gypsum is the fire-resistance membrane, the tested Chapter 7 assembly controls fastening and joints.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Weather and wet-area gypsum (2508.2, 2509, 2511.5)
Gypsum wallboard or gypsum plaster shall **not** be used on any exterior surface exposed directly to the weather. Gypsum wallboard shall **not** be used where there is direct exposure to water or continuous high humidity. Gypsum sheathing on exterior surfaces: ASTM C1280 (2508.2).

**Showers and water closets (2509):** showers and public toilet walls conform to **§1210.2**. Tile backer in tub/shower walls and shower wall/ceiling panels: **Table 2509.2** (glass-mat gypsum backing ASTM C1178; fiber-cement ASTM C1288 or ISO 8336 Category C; fiber-mat cementitious ASTM C1325). Water-resistant gypsum backing board is the tile base on **water-closet compartment walls** (GA-216 or ASTM C840). Regular gypsum wallboard is permitted under tile or wall panels in **other** wall and ceiling areas.

Water-resistant gypsum backing board shall **not** be used (2509.3): (1) over a vapor retarder in shower or bathtub compartments; (2) where there will be direct exposure to water or continuous high humidity. Commentary: saunas, steam rooms, gang showers, indoor pools — use cement backer, cement plaster, masonry, or similar, not “green board.”

**Interior plaster wet areas (2511.5):** showers and public toilet walls also **§1210.2** and **§1210.3**. Wood-frame walls covered with cement plaster or tile and subject to water splash: framing protected with an **approved moisture barrier**. Commentary: gypsum plaster is **not** permitted in wet areas.

**SD takeaway:** Wet rooms are 1210 finish plus Table 2509.2 backer — not paper-faced gypsum in showers, and never green board over a vapor retarder.

### 2. Cement plaster (stucco) — architect envelope (2510, 2512)
Gypsum plaster shall **not** be used on exterior surfaces (2512.3). Cement plaster is the exterior plaster. WRB: **§1403.2**; over wood-based sheathing, 2510.6.1 (dry B zones) or 2510.6.2 (moist A / marine C, including **≥ 5 mm** drainage space or **90%** ASTM E2273 / ASTM E2925). Weep screed: **≥ 0.5 mm** corrosion-resistant, **≥ 90 mm** vertical flange; **≥ 100 mm** above earth or **50 mm** above pavement (2512.1.2). Gypsum lath/wallboard is not a cement-plaster backing except the 2510.5.2.1 water-resistive-barrier + self-furred lath cases (horizontal ceilings/soffits or interior walls).

**SD takeaway:** Exterior stucco is cement plaster on a WRB and a weep screed, not gypsum plaster.
