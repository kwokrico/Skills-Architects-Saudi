# SBC 201 Chapter 13 Energy Efficiency
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
2024 | SBCNC

> Scope note: This chapter **only points** energy-efficiency design and construction to the Saudi Energy Conservation Code. It is **not** a thermal-envelope or lighting-power chapter. Technical criteria live in **SBC 601** and **SBC 602**.

## Regulatory Overview
Section 1301.1 states that this chapter governs design and construction of buildings for energy efficiency. Compliance with the technical provisions is exclusively by **SBC 601** and **SBC 602**.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Mandatory pointer (1301.1, 1301.1.1)
Buildings shall be designed and constructed in accordance with the Saudi Energy Conservation Code (**SBC 601** and **SBC 602**). Commentary: those volumes address envelopes and the selection/installation of mechanical, service-water heating, electrical distribution, and illumination systems for residential and commercial buildings; climate basis is geographical zones covering heating and cooling.

**SD takeaway:** Put SBC 601/602 on the SD code sheet; Chapter 13 adds no envelope R-values, SHGC, or LPD of its own.
