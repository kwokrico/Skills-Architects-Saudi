# SBC 201 Chapter 3 Use and Occupancy Classification
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
2024 | SBCNC

> Scope note: This chapter classifies buildings and portions thereof into occupancy groups (A–U) that then drive height/area, construction type, fire protection, egress, and finishes. It is not the mixed-use, height/area, or special-occupancy chapter — those are Chapters 5, 6, and 4. Detached one- and two-family dwellings and townhouses not more than three stories with a separate means of egress are not Group R under this code; they go to SBC 1101/1102. Hazardous-materials operations that stay under MAQ are not Group H — they remain the host occupancy plus control areas (Section 414) and SBC 801.

## Regulatory Overview
Every structure, occupied roof, and multipurpose space is classified into one or more of the ten occupancy families in Section 302.1; mixed groups are resolved in Section 508, not here. Occupancy is the first schematic lock because Tables 504.3, 504.4, 506.2, 803.13, 903, and 1004.5 all key off the group assigned in this chapter.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Occupancy families and mixed-use pointer (302.1, 302.2)
| Group family | Subgroups | Typical schematic uses |
|--------------|-----------|------------------------|
| Assembly A | A-1, A-2, A-3, A-4, A-5 | Performance, dining, worship/recreation, indoor sports with seating, outdoor sports |
| Business B | B | Offices, professional services, outpatient (non-I-2) |
| Educational E | E | Six or more persons, through high school |
| Factory F | F-1, F-2 | Moderate-hazard vs low-hazard manufacturing |
| High hazard H | H-1 through H-5 | Only if MAQs in Tables 307.1(1)/(2) are exceeded |
| Institutional I | I-1, I-2, I-3, I-4 | Custodial, medical, detention, day care |
| Mercantile M | M | Display and sale of goods |
| Residential R | R-1, R-2, R-3, R-4 | Sleeping, except SBC 1101/1102 dwellings |
| Storage S | S-1, S-2 | Moderate- vs low-hazard storage |
| Utility U | U | Accessory/miscellaneous |

Unlisted uses are classified as the group they most nearly resemble. Occupied roofs are classified the same way and then checked against Section 503.1.4. Multiple groups in one building → Section 508 (accessory / nonseparated / separated).

**SD takeaway:** Lock primary and accessory groups on day one; a mosque is A-3, a restaurant A-2, apartments R-2 — those three choices set sprinkler, travel-distance, and finish tables before the plan is drawn.

### 2. Assembly thresholds that drop Group A (303.1.1–303.1.3)
| Condition | Classification |
|-----------|----------------|
| Assembly building or tenant space, occupant load **< 50** | Group B (303.1.1) |
| Accessory assembly room, OL **< 50** | Group B **or** part of the main occupancy (303.1.2 Item 1) |
| Accessory assembly room **< 70 m²** | Group B **or** part of the main occupancy (303.1.2 Item 2) |
| Assembly associated with Group E, used only by students/staff | Not a separate occupancy — remains E (303.1.3) |
| Same E spaces used by the public (community events) | Classify as Group A |

The 70 m² / OL < 50 tests do **not** require the 10% accessory-occupancy limit of Section 508.2.

**SD takeaway:** A 40-person conference room or a 67.5 m² staff lounge can stay B (or the host group) — do not default every gathering room to A-2/A-3.

### 3. Assembly subgroups (303.2–303.5)
| Group | Distinguishing use | File examples |
|-------|--------------------|---------------|
| A-1 | Performing arts / motion pictures, usually fixed seating | Theaters, concert halls, TV studios with audience |
| A-2 | Food and/or drink consumption | Restaurants, banquet halls, gaming; associated commercial kitchens stay A-2 |
| A-3 | Worship, recreation, other assembly not in A-1/2/4/5 | **Mosques, prayer halls**, galleries, libraries, gyms without spectator seating, lecture halls |
| A-4 | Indoor sport **with** spectator seating | Arenas, indoor pools/courts with stands |
| A-5 | Outdoor assembly (products of combustion freely vented) | Stadiums, grandstands, bleachers |

Indoor tennis without seating = A-3; add seating = A-4; remove the roof = A-5. Retractable-roof venues take the more stringent indoor classification (A-4).

**SD takeaway:** Classify mosques as A-3 and food halls as A-2; those two labels change sprinkler thresholds in Chapter 9 even when travel-distance tables treat “Group A” as one family.

### 4. Educational, day care, and nursery location (305, 308.5)
| Trigger | Group |
|---------|-------|
| Educational purposes through high school, **6 or more** persons | E (305.1) |
| Day care, **> 5** children older than **2.5 years**, < 24 h | E (305.2) |
| Five or fewer children in a dwelling unit | R-3 (305.2.3) |
| Day care **> 5** persons of any age, < 24 h, not in the home | I-4 (308.5) |
| Child day care, **> 5 but ≤ 100** children **2.5 years or less**, rooms on level of exit discharge **and** each room has an exit door to the exterior | May be classified E (308.5.1) |
| Nursery and kindergarten (children under 6) | Ground floor; first floor only if ramp access per 308.5.2.1 |

**SD takeaway:** Put nursery/kindergarten on the ground floor unless the 308.5.2.1 ramp path is designed in; do not park early-years rooms on typical upper-floor classroom stacks.

### 5. Institutional and small-care split (308, 310.4.1)
| Occupancy | Threshold | Schematic note |
|-----------|-----------|----------------|
| I-1 custodial, 24 h | **> 16** persons excluding staff | Conditions 1 / 2 (self-evacuation vs limited assistance); 6–16 → R-4; ≤ 5 → R-3 or SBC 1101/1102 + sprinkler |
| I-2 medical, 24 h | **> 5** incapable of self-preservation | Cond. 1 nursing (no emergency/surgery); Cond. 2 hospitals; ≤ 5 → R-3 or 1101/1102 + sprinkler |
| I-3 detention | **> 5** under restraint | Conditions 1–5 (freedom of movement) |
| I-4 day care | **> 5**, < 24 h | See §4 |

Five-or-fewer care in a dwelling may use SBC 1101/1102 if sprinklers are installed per 903.3.1.3 or SBC 1101/1102 Section 2904.

**SD takeaway:** Crossing 5 (medical) or 16 (custodial) persons is a occupancy-group change, not a furniture count — lock the care census before selecting I vs R vs 1101/1102.

### 6. Residential groups vs SBC 1101/1102 (310)
| Group | Occupants | Typical buildings |
|-------|-----------|-------------------|
| R-1 | Transient (stay not more than **30 days**) | Hotels, motels; boarding/congregate transient **> 10** occupants |
| R-2 | Permanent; sleeping units **or more than two** dwelling units | Apartments, dorms, live/work (Section 419); congregate nontransient **> 16** |
| R-3 | Permanent, not R-1/R-2/R-4/I | One–two dwellings; congregate nontransient ≤ 16; transient congregate ≤ 10; lodging houses ≤ 5 guest rooms **and** ≤ 10 occupants |
| R-4 | Custodial, **> 5 but ≤ 16** excluding staff | Group homes; built to R-3 construction provisions |

Detached one- and two-family dwellings and townhouses **not more than three stories** with a separate means of egress are **not** classified R-3 here — they are regulated by **SBC 1101/1102**. Four stories or more, or attached duplexes that fail 1101/1102 townhouse criteria, come into SBC 201 as R-2 or R-3.

**SD takeaway:** Townhouses ≤ 3 stories with separate egress leave this code; anything taller, or apartments with more than two units, is R-2 under SBC 201 plus Section 420 separations.

### 7. High hazard only above MAQ (307, 309.2)
Group H applies only when quantities exceed Tables 307.1(1) (physical hazards) and 307.1(2) (health hazards) per control area under Section 414. Below MAQ, classify as the occupancy the use most nearly resembles. H-1 detonation; H-2 deflagration/accelerated burning; H-3 physical hazard; H-4 health hazard; H-5 semiconductor HPM (Section 415.11). Hazardous materials on roofs/canopies are outdoor storage under SBC 801, not building occupancy.

Group M display of nonflammable solids/liquids is capped per control area by Table 414.2.5(1).

**SD takeaway:** Do not label labs, pharmacies, or retail chemical aisles as H until MAQ/control-area math is done; most stay B/M/F plus Section 414.
