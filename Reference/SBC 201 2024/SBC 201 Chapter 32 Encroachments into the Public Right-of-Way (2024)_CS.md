# SBC 201 Chapter 32 Encroachments into the Public Right-of-Way
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
2024 | SBCNC

> Scope note: This chapter regulates projections **across the street lot line into the public right-of-way** (sidewalk, parking lot, street). It is **not** interior lot-line setbacks or fire-separation distance (those stay in Ch. 7). Drainage of roofs/awnings/marquees must not flow over a public walking surface (3201.4). Pedestrian walkway construction: **§3104**; structural design of awnings/signs: **Chapter 16**. Other laws still apply (3201.3).

## Regulatory Overview
Measure projection horizontally from the lot line to the outermost point (3201.2). Below grade, above grade but under **2.4 m**, and **2.4 m** or more are three different boxes; public-way walkways and temporary vestibules need the **applicable governing authority**.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Below grade (3202.1)
| Element | Lock |
|---------|------|
| Structural support generally | Shall **not** project beyond lot lines |
| Street-wall **footings** | If the footing (or its support) is **≥ 2.4 m** below grade: project **≤ 300 mm** beyond the street lot line |
| Vaults / enclosed spaces | Terms of the **applicable governing authority** |
| Areaways | Protect with grates, guards, or other approved means |

Interior side lot-line footing projections are **not** permitted (commentary).

**SD takeaway:** A basement wall on the street line may throw footings 300 mm into the ROW only if they sit at least 2.4 m below grade.

### 2. Above grade and below 2.4 m (3202.2)
Encroachments **prohibited** except 3202.2.1–3202.2.3 (and 3202.4). **Doors and windows shall not open or project** into the public right-of-way.

| Allowed item | Max projection / clearance |
|--------------|----------------------------|
| Steps | **≤ 300 mm**; guard/device ≥ **0.9 m** high, or between columns/pilasters. (MOE steps may still need 1011.11 / 1014 handrails that eat the 300 mm.) |
| Columns / pilasters (incl. bases/moldings) | **≤ 300 mm** |
| Belt courses, lintels, sills, architraves, pediments, similar | **≤ 100 mm** |
| Awnings (incl. valances) | Lowest part **≥ 2.1 m** above the ROW |

**SD takeaway:** Nothing but a 300 mm step/column or 100 mm belt course lives in the first 2.4 m of air over the sidewalk; awnings start at 2.1 m clear.

### 3. Encroachments 2.4 m or more (3202.3)
| Item | Lock |
|------|------|
| Windows, balconies, architectural features, mechanical equipment | **25 mm** of encroachment per **25 mm** of extra clearance above **2.4 m**, **maximum 1.2 m** |
| **≥ 4.5 m** above grade | **Not limited** by this chapter |
| Awnings, canopies, marquees, signs | Ch. 16 loads. If clearance **< 4.5 m**: occupy **≤ ⅔** of sidewalk width measured from the building. Stanchions/columns **≥ 600 mm** in from the curb |
| Pedestrian walkway over the ROW | Approval of the **applicable governing authority**; lowest part **≥ 4.5 m**. Detail in **§3104** |

**SD takeaway:** Balconies grow 1:1 above 2.4 m and stop at 1.2 m; at 4.5 m the projection cap drops away — that is the balcony/bridge datum.

### 4. Temporary encroachments (3202.4)
Where the **applicable governing authority** allows: vestibules and storm enclosures **≤ 7 months** in any 1 year; project **≤ 0.9 m** and **≤ ¼** of sidewalk width (the lesser). Temporary entrance awnings: clear **≥ 2.1 m** to the lowest portion; supports removable steel or other approved noncombustible.

**SD takeaway:** Winter vestibules are 0.9 m (or ¼ sidewalk) and seven months — not a permanent lobby on the pavement.

### 5. Other laws and MoT (3201.1, 3201.3)
This chapter does not permit violation of other laws regulating public property. Commentary: streets/highways are often established by the **Ministry of Transportation** and regional transportation branches (purchase, condemnation, or easement). The building official must know how the ROW was established. Vaults, walkways over the ROW, and temporary vestibules are explicitly subject to the **applicable governing authority**.

**SD takeaway:** MoT or the municipal ROW owner can veto a code-compliant projection — confirm the street lot line and any licence before drawing a balcony over the sidewalk.
