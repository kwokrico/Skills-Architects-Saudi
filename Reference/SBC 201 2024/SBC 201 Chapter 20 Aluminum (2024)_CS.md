# SBC 201 Chapter 20 Aluminum
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
2024 | SBCNC

> Scope note: This chapter governs **quality, design, fabrication, and erection of aluminum** (2001.1) — **structural** applications only. It does **not** set standards for specialty products such as storefront framing or architectural hardware. Aluminum in HVAC and plumbing is **SBC 501** and **SBC 701**.

## Regulatory Overview
Aluminum used for structural purposes shall comply with **AA ASM 35** and **AA ADM 1**. Nominal loads shall be the minimum design loads required by **Chapter 16** (2002.1).

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Structural aluminum only (2001–2002)
Commentary: AA ADM Parts 1-A and 1-B cover aluminum building-type load-carrying members (ASD and LRFD). **ASM 35** covers flashings, sheet roofing, and similar applications.

**SD takeaway:** Structural aluminum and sheet-metal flashings/roofing follow AA ADM 1 and ASM 35 with Chapter 16 loads; curtain-wall/storefront systems are not locked by this chapter.
