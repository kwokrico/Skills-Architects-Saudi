# SBC 201 Chapter 17 Special Inspections and Tests
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
2024 | SBCNC

> Scope note: This chapter governs **quality, workmanship, tests, labeling, special inspections, and structural observation** (1701.1). It is **not** structural design (Ch. 16) and **not** the material design codes (SBC 302–309). Approved agencies for labeling/testing shall meet **SBC 302** Part III and be accredited (1703.1).

## Regulatory Overview
Where a permit is sought under §105, the owner (not the contractor) shall employ approved agencies for the special inspections and tests in **§1705**, in addition to building-official inspections under §110 (1704.2). A **statement of special inspections** is a permit condition (1704.2.3 / 1704.3).

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. When special inspections are required (1704.2, 1705.1)
SI applies to the types of work in §1705 (steel, concrete, masonry, wood, soils, deep foundations, wind, seismic, fire resistance, EIFS, smoke control, and special cases).

| Exception (1704.2) | Effect |
|--------------------|--------|
| 1 | Minor work, or as the building official approves for the jurisdiction |
| 2 | Group **U** accessory to a residential occupancy (e.g. §312.1), unless the building official requires SI |
| 3 | Portions designed/constructed to cold-formed steel light-frame **§2211.1.2** or conventional light-frame **§2308** |
| 4 | Contractor may employ the agency if the contractor is also the owner |

**Concrete SI exceptions** (1705.3, as published): isolated spread footings, buildings **≤ 3 stories** above grade plane on earth/rock; continuous footings under walls of buildings **≤ 3 stories** (light-frame walls; footings per **SBC 303**; \(f_c'\) not more than **17 MPa**); nonstructural slabs on ground; foundation walls per **SBC 303**; patios/driveways/sidewalks on grade.

**SD takeaway:** Flag SI on the SD code sheet for any engineered steel, concrete, masonry, or podium/basement — only minor U, prescriptive light-frame, and the listed small footings/slabs are off the hook.

### 2. Statement of special inspections (1704.2.3, 1704.3)
Registered design professional in responsible charge prepares the statement (exception: a qualified person approved by the building official if no RDP designed the work). Not required for the same light-frame portions as 1704.2 Exception 3.

Shall identify: materials/systems/work requiring SI or tests; type and extent of each inspection and each test; extra seismic/wind items per **1705.12–1705.14**; and whether each inspection is **continuous**, **periodic**, or per the referenced standard. Name designated seismic systems and the main wind-force-resisting system when those extra inspections apply (1704.3.2–1704.3.3).

**SD takeaway:** The statement is a permit document, not a CA afterthought — list likely SI scopes (structure, soils, firestops, smoke control) at SD.

### 3. Structural observation and high-risk flags (1704.6.1, 1705.18)
Structural observation by an RDP (in addition to §110 and §1705) where **any** of: Risk Category **IV**; **high-rise**; Seismic Design Category **E** and **> 2 stories** above grade plane; required by the structural RDP; or required by the building official.

Firestop / fire-resistant joint / perimeter-containment SI (1705.18) in: **high-rise** buildings; Risk Category **III or IV**; **or** fire areas containing Group **R** with occupant load **> 250**.

**SD takeaway:** Risk Category III/IV, high-rise, or a large residential fire area typically triggers SI (and observation if IV or high-rise) — note it on the SD sheet with construction type and risk.
