# SBC 201 Chapter 31 Special Construction
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
2024 | SBCNC

> Scope note: Architect locks in this chapter are **membrane structures (3102)**, **temporary structures < 180 days (3103)**, **pedestrian walkways (3104)**, **awnings/canopies and marquees (3105–3106)**, **solar (3111)**, and **pools → SBC 1401 (3109)**. It is not a substitute for Chs. 7–12 or 16. Walkways over the public way also use **Chapter 32**. Temporary tents/membranes < 180 days also use **SBC 801**. This CS skips intermodal-container structural calcs (3115).

## Regulatory Overview
Chapter 31 adds to the rest of the code and, where it speaks, overrides the general rule for that element. Membrane and temporary uses split at **180 days**; anything left up longer is a permanent building under the ordinary chapters.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Membrane structures (3102)
Applies to air-supported, air-inflated, membrane-covered cable/frame, and tensile membranes erected **≥ 180 days** (or **any** duration if erected on a building, balcony, or deck). Shorter independent membranes: **SBC 801**. Non-occupied covers (water/sewage plants, greenhouses, similar): only 3102.3.1 and 3102.7. Tensile and air-supported: **ASCE 55**.

| Type | Classification |
|------|----------------|
| Noncombustible membrane, or noncombustible frame/cable + approved membrane | **Type IIB** |
| Heavy-timber frame + approved membrane | **Type IV-HT** |
| Other | **Type V** |

Membrane/liner: noncombustible (703.3) or NFPA 701 Test 1 or 2. Height: **one story**, metres per §504.3 (exception: noncombustible membrane used as roof only). Area per §506. Mixed construction: noncombustible membrane as roof/skylight of any type if **≥ 6 m** above floor/balcony/gallery; NFPA 701 membrane as roof/skylight on IIB, III, IV-HT, V if **≥ 6.1 m**. Do **not** count the membrane as lateral restraint of the frame (3102.7.1). Inflation: primary **and** auxiliary systems (3102.8).

**SD takeaway:** A tensile hall is a one-story IIB/IV-HT/V building under 504/506; park it on a podium or deck and it is no longer a “tent.”

### 2. Temporary structures < 180 days (3103)
Use duration of the **structure**, not seasonal occupancy of a permanent shell. Tents/umbrellas/special-event membranes also **SBC 801**. Still required as necessary: strength, fire safety, **Chapter 10** egress, **Chapter 11** accessibility, light/ventilation/sanitation (**Ch. 12**, **SBC 701**, **SBC 501**). Permit if area **> 11 m²** and gathering of **≥ 10** persons. Location vs **Table 705.5**. Exit-access travel **≤ 30 m** (stricter than Ch. 10). Building official may extend the 180-day permit (commentary to §107).

**SD takeaway:** A pavilion up for more than 180 days is a permanent building; if it is truly temporary, cap travel at 30 m and pull a permit above 11 m² / 10 people.

### 3. Pedestrian walkways (3104)
Walkway area/stories/height **do not add** to the connected buildings. Buildings remain **separate** (exceptions: same-lot single building 503.1.2; Type B unit count = one structure). Construction: **noncombustible** unless both buildings are combustible; FRTW roof OK when both are Type I or II. Connection: 3104.5.1, .2, .3, or .4.

| Option | Lock |
|--------|------|
| 3104.5.1 fire barriers | **2 h** to the interior; exterior walls **2 h** for **3 m** around the walkway perimeter; openings ≥ **¾ h** |
| 3104.5.3 open sides | Building-to-building **> 3 m**; each sidewall **≥ 50%** open; walkway roof **≤ 12 m** above grade plane; connects only **3rd story or lower** |
| Tunnel (3104.10) | **2 h** separation; openings per 716 (commentary **1½ h**) |
| Over public way | **Chapter 32** |

Width: unobstructed **≥ 0.9 m**; charging text max **9 mm** (OCR-garbled; commentary only says a maximum exists to block full-floor extensions — no replacement number in this extract). Travel in the walkway: **≤ 61 m**; sprinklered **75 m**; ≥ 50% open sides **90 m**; open + NFPA 13 **120 m**.

**SD takeaway:** A link bridge that is to stay outside both buildings’ area needs 2-hour junctions (or 50% open sides, ≤ 12 m, ≤ third floor) and does not grow either floor plate.

### 4. Awnings, canopies, marquees (3105–3106)
Awning frames: noncombustible, FRTW, heavy timber 2304.11, or 1-hour, with combustible or noncombustible covers; fixed/retractable/folding. Covering: NFPA 701 Test 1 or 2; **or** E84/UL 723 FSI ≤ **25**; **or** NFPA 286 (no flashover, peak ≤ **800 kW**). Exception: detached one- and two-family (Items 1–2 only per commentary).

Marquees: supported **entirely from the building**, **noncombustible**, Ch. 16, drain without spilling on the sidewalk, no conflict with standpipes/exits/street lights. Thickness: ≤ **900 mm** if the projection is **> ⅔** lot-line-to-curb; ≤ **2.7 m** if **< ⅔**. Encroachment geometry: **Chapter 32**.

**SD takeaway:** Street awnings are flame-spread 25 / NFPA 701 covers on noncombustible or FRTW frames; marquees are building-hung noncombustible boxes sized by the ⅔-curb rule.

### 5. Solar (3111) and pools (3109)
Rooftop PV and solar-thermal: wind **§1609**; supporting roof live load **§1607.14.4**. Solar thermal: this section + **SBC 701**, **SBC 501**, **SBC 801**; plastic covers **§2606.12**; list ICC 900/SRCC 300 and ICC 901/SRCC 100. PV: this section + **SBC 801** + NFPA 70 / **SBC 401**; panels UL 1703 or UL 61730-1/-2; inverters UL 1741. Rooftop PV fire class **§1505.9**; BIPV roof covering **§1505.8** and **§1507**. Roof access/pathways: **SBC 801 §1205**. Ground-mounted PV: Ch. 16 + SBC 801; FSD “determined by the law” (3111.3.5.1).

**Swimming pools, spas, hot tubs (3109.1):** design and construction shall comply with **SBC 1401**.

**SD takeaway:** Roof PV is a 1505 fire-class and 1609 wind problem plus SBC 801 pathways; pools leave this chapter for SBC 1401.
