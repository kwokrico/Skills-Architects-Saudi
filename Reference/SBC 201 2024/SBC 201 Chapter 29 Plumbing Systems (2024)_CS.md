# SBC 201 Chapter 29 Plumbing Systems
**Architect critical summary for schematic design**
2024 | SBCNC

> Scope note: This chapter **and SBC 701** govern plumbing design and installation (2901.1). Commentary: fixture counts are duplicated from **SBC 701 Chapter 4** for the architect’s floor plan. Toilet and bathing rooms: **§1210**. Private sewage: **SBC 702**. Use/maintenance: **SBC 801**, IPMC and SBC 701. Alterations: **SBC 901** and SBC 701. Accessible millimetres: **Chapter 11** / ICC A117.1 (commentary also cites A117.1 via **SBC 701 §404**).

## Regulatory Overview
Fixture numbers follow **Table 2902.1** and the **actual use** of the space, with occupant load from this code — not a second occupancy classification. Room finishes and privacy are §1210; this chapter sizes and places the rooms.

## Critical main topics and subtopics

### 1. Fixture counts — Table 2902.1 (2902.1, 2902.1.1)
Plumbing fixtures in the minimum number in **Table 2902.1** based on actual use. Uses not shown: building official. Occupant load: this code. Ratios are “1 per n” applied to that load. Description of use is the table entry; it may differ from Chapter 3 occupancy (commentary: gymnasium-with-stage as assembly fixtures while the building remains Group E for egress). Outdoor seating/entertainment: Table Note d — include those areas. Service sinks: one per building except hospitals (one per floor); B/M **≤ 15** persons may omit (table note). Indoor pools: Description Row 8; outdoor public pools: Note f → ISPSC §609.

**Calculations (2902.1.1):** split occupant load **50/50** by sex; apply each sex’s ratio; round fractions **up**. Multiple occupancies: sum fractions **then** round. Exceptions: approved statistical sex split; all-gender multiple-user rooms counted on **100%** occupant load, each fixture per **ICC A117.1**, urinals in stalls; single-user / bathing rooms per **2902.1.2** (no sex split).

**SD takeaway:** Count fixtures from Table 2902.1 on the egress occupant load; do not copy the table into this CS — open the published table.

### 2. Separate vs single-user; Chapter 11 family rooms (2902.2, 2902.1.2)
Where fixtures are required, **separate facilities for each sex**, except: dwelling/sleeping units; total occupant load (employees + customers) **≤ 15**; mercantile **≤ 100**; business **≤ 25**. Two family/assisted-use rooms may serve as the two required one-WC-per-sex rooms (2902.2.1); they need not be sex-identified under 2902.4.

Single-user and family/assisted-use rooms required by **§1110.2.1** **contribute** to the building count and shall be identified for **all persons regardless of sex** (2902.1.2). Aggregate of single-user and separate facilities is permitted. Do not double-count a unisex WC toward both sexes.

**SD takeaway:** Small B/M/OL-15 spaces can share one toilet; otherwise two sexes, unless you convert to all-gender or 1110.2.1 family rooms that still add to the total.

### 3. Toilet-room location and coordination with §1210 (2902.3)
Public toilets for public-utilization buildings; employee toilets always. Combined employee/public allowed. Public toilets **not** required: parking garages with **no attendants**; quick-transaction spaces with public access **≤ 30 m²**. Route shall **not** pass through kitchens, storage rooms or closets (2902.3.1). Toilet rooms shall **not** open directly into a room used to prepare food for the public (2902.3.2). Finishes: **§1210** (scope). Commentary also cites **§1209.5** for the food-prep door.

| Occupancy | Travel | Vertical | Section |
|-----------|--------|----------|---------|
| Other than malls | **≤ 150 m** | Not more than **one story** above or below | 2902.3.3 |
| Covered / open malls | **≤ 90 m** (from store main entrance, or from employee work area if no in-store employee toilets) | Not more than **one story** above or below | 2902.3.4 |
| Drinking fountains | Same 150 m / 90 m mall split; **on an accessible route** | One story | 2902.5 |

Factory/industrial employee travel may exceed 2902.3.3 if approved. Pay toilets are **extra**; required toilets are free (2902.3.5). Multi-occupant toilet-room egress doors shall **not** lock from inside (family/assisted-use excepted) (2902.3.6).

**SD takeaway:** Place toilets within 150 m (90 m in a mall), one story, off the kitchen — and finish the rooms to §1210 / Chapter 11, not this chapter’s millimetres.
