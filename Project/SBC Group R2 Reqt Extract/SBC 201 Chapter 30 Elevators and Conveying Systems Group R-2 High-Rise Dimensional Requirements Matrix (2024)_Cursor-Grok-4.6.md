### Scope and assumptions

1. Group R-2 and high-rise status are project statements, not independently verified classifications. Chapter 30 does not restate the Chapter 2 high-rise definition.
2. The exact Riyadh AHJ/permit pathway, project stage, fire-strategy status and SCD NOC status are unconfirmed; therefore this matrix does not conclude compliance.
3. Automatic sprinkler protection is not selected. **3006.2 Item 1** recognizes **Section 903.3.1.1 or 903.3.1.2**. Fire-service-access and occupant-evacuation packages (**3007.2**, **3008.2**) charge **Section 903.3.1.1 only**.
4. Occupied-floor height versus the lowest level of fire department vehicle access is unconfirmed. Section **3007** applies only **where required by Section 403.6.1**. This matrix does **not** import 403.6.1 height, car count or car capacity.
5. Storey count versus grade plane, hoistway height versus **23 m**, mixed-use podium, open parking garage, multiple levels of exit discharge, and whether occupant-evacuation elevators are elected are unconfirmed.
6. Table **3001.3** is flattened concatenated HTML with duplicated Type/Standard rows. The **3007.8** standby token reads `Type 60/Class 72`. Both are **Verify source**.
7. Group I-1 Condition 2, I-2 and I-3 hoistway-protection triggers, below-grade transportation-station escalator width, automotive lifts, belt manlifts, conveyors, personnel/material hoists and Group H quantity limits are omitted from project-use tables.
