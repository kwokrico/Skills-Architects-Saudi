### Scope and assumptions

1. Group R-2 and high-rise status are project statements, not independently verified classifications.
2. The exact Riyadh AHJ/permit pathway, project stage and SCD NOC status are unconfirmed; therefore this matrix does not conclude compliance.
3. Automatic sprinkler protection, mixed-use podium, storey count and structural system are unconfirmed. Chapter 20 does not branch on NFPA 13 versus 13R or on occupancy group.
4. Charging **2002.1** names **AA ASM 35** and **AA ADM 1** with **no edition year**. Bibliography names ADM1-15 (2015) and ASM 35-00 (2000). Neither commentary year is adopted as a charging substitution.
5. General Comments state that specialty products (storefront framing, architectural hardware) are outside this chapter and that HVAC/plumbing aluminum is addressed in SBC 501 / SBC 701. Those commentary statements are not treated as numbered code requirements and no 501/701 values are imported.
6. Typical tower aluminum (windows, curtain wall, railings, composite panels) is not given millimetre geometry in this chapter. Only aluminum used **for structural purposes** is charged to the named AA standards, with nominal loads from Chapter 16.
