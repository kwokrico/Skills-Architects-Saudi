### Scope and assumptions

1. Group R-2 and high-rise status are project statements, not independently verified classifications.
2. The exact Riyadh AHJ/permit pathway, project stage, fire-strategy status and SCD NOC status are unconfirmed; therefore this matrix does not conclude compliance.
3. Automatic sprinkler protection, mixed-use podium, storey count and construction type are unconfirmed. Chapter 24 does not branch on NFPA 13 versus 13R.
4. Unit-level versus common-area splits in 2405.3 Exceptions 4–5 and 2406.4.2 Exception 4 apply only inside individual Group R-2 dwelling units. They do not relax corridors, lobbies, amenity rooms or other public-use glazing.
5. The Group R-3 skylight-without-curb exception (2405.4) is not applied to this tower. Greenhouse screening Exception 3 and racquetball/squash court glazing (2408.2) are omitted from lead tables unless those uses are added.
6. Table 2406.2(1), Table 2406.2(2) and the sloped-glass dead-load formula for glass sloped **30 degrees or less** from horizontal are OCR-unresolved. No reconstructed category class or D-load is a design-release value. Section **2404.3.4** is missing from the extract (jump 2404.3.3 to 2404.3.5).
7. Glass thickness for wind and dead load is ASTM E1300 against Chapter 16; this chapter does not pick the millimetre for vertical or sloped lites except where a numbered thickness is stated (louvers, screens, guards, elevator vision panels).
