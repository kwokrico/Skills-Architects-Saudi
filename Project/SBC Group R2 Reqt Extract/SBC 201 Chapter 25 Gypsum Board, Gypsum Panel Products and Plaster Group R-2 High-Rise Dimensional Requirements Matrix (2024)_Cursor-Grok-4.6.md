### Scope and assumptions

1. Group R-2 and high-rise status are project statements, not independently verified classifications.
2. The exact Riyadh AHJ/permit pathway, project stage, fire-strategy status and SCD NOC status are unconfirmed; therefore this matrix does not conclude compliance.
3. Automatic sprinkler protection is unconfirmed. Chapter 25 does not branch gypsum or plaster millimetres on NFPA 13 versus 13R.
4. Construction type is unconfirmed. A typical R-2 tower is Type I or II concrete or steel; wood-frame gypsum shear walls, wood-joist gypsum diaphragm ceilings and reinforced gypsum concrete roof decks are not assumed and are omitted from Sections 4–12.
5. Exterior cement plaster (stucco), interior three-coat plaster, exposed-aggregate finish, wood furring and studless partitions are unconfirmed product choices. Those rows are Conditional.
6. Riyadh is treated as a dry (B) climate-zone basis for 2510.6.1. Moist (A) / marine (C) 2510.6.2 millimetres and drainage-efficiency percentages are omitted unless a coastal or moist-zone site is opened in the gap register.
7. Tables 2506.2, 2507.2, 2508.1, 2509.2 and 2511.1.1 are material/standard name maps. They are cited by standard designation; no millimetre is invented from those tables.
8. Fire-resistance-rated gypsum fastening, joint treatment beyond 2508.5, and plaster coat thickness in ASTM C842 / C926 cannot be concluded from this chapter extract alone.
