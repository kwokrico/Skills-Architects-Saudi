### Scope and assumptions

1. Group R-2 and high-rise status are project statements, not independently verified classifications.
2. The exact Riyadh AHJ/permit pathway, street-lot-line survey, public-right-of-way owner, project stage and SCD NOC status are unconfirmed; therefore this matrix does not conclude compliance.
3. Automatic sprinkler protection, mixed-use podium, storey count and construction type are unconfirmed. Chapter 32 does not branch on NFPA 13 versus 13R or on occupancy group.
4. This chapter governs projections **across a street lot line into the public right-of-way**. It does not set interior lot-line setbacks or fire-separation distance.
5. Whether any tower elevation sits on a street, alley or highway lot line is unconfirmed. If the building is set fully behind the lot line, the 3202 projection caps do not create a right to occupy the sidewalk.
6. Commentary pointers to Sections 1011.11 and 1014, Ministry of Transportation street-establishment notes, and figure captions (including the “2.5 meter” figure label) are not adopted as code cells.
