Agency tables were read; General Comments and Purpose are commentary and were excluded.

- **Status:** Source-only architectural advisory matrix for design coordination. It is not a stamped compliance statement, specification, SCD NOC, SBPS approval, legal opinion, or permission to construct.
- **Outbound-source rule:** No value in this matrix has been imported from NFPA, ASME, ICC A117.1, ASTM test methods, UL listings, SBC 304/305, Chapter 9/10/11/16, commentary examples, or the existing chapter summary. Where Chapter 35 names a companion, this matrix records the listed designation and year without supplying that companion’s criteria.

### Scope and assumptions

1. Group R-2 and high-rise status are project statements, not independently verified classifications.
2. The exact Riyadh AHJ/permit pathway, project stage and SCD NOC status are unconfirmed; therefore this matrix does not conclude compliance.
3. Automatic sprinkler protection (**NFPA 13—13** versus **NFPA 13R—13**), storey count, mixed-use podium, amenity program, structural system and façade combustibility are unconfirmed. Both listed sprinkler companions are shown until the project locks one. **NFPA 13D—13** is omitted as a one- and two-family path.
4. Chapter 35 is an edition list. It does not publish millimetre geometry, sprinkler densities, fire-resistance hours, elevator dimensions or accessibility clearances.
5. Listed standards are for guidance until adopted and/or approved by **SASO** or **SBCNC**. Application is through **Section 102.4**, whose text is not in this chapter.

## 9. Project-use controls

1. Use **Verified** rows for initial scoping after the row trigger and branch are confirmed.
