### Scope and assumptions

1. Group R-2 and high-rise status are project statements, not independently verified classifications.
2. The exact Riyadh AHJ/permit pathway, project stage, fire-strategy status and SCD NOC status are unconfirmed; therefore this matrix does not conclude compliance.
3. Type of construction is unconfirmed. An occupied floor above **23 m** is treated as putting foam plastic in Type I–IV exterior walls onto the 2603.5 path **if** foam is specified. Type V framed walls, Type VB plastic glazing, and 2612 plastic-composite decks are not assumed for the tower shaft.
4. Automatic sprinkler protection is not selected. Where this chapter names Section **903.3.1.1**, both the sprinklered and unsprinklered branches are shown. NFPA 13 versus 13R is not assumed.
5. Foam in the envelope, plastic veneer, light-transmitting wall panels/glazing/skylights, plenums, FRP ornament and reflective plastic core insulation are unconfirmed.
6. Tables 2603.12.1, 2603.12.2, 2603.13.1 and 2603.13.2 are OCR-destroyed. Tables 2607.4 and 2609.4 are concatenated. Section 2603.4.1.4 has no numbered heading in the extract. Affected rows are **Verify source**.
7. Coolers/freezers, covered-mall signs, greenhouses, solar collectors on buildings not over three stories, Type V sill-plate spray foam, R-3 patio/skylight/garage exceptions and plastic lumber decks are omitted from lead tables unless a later gap register opens those uses.
