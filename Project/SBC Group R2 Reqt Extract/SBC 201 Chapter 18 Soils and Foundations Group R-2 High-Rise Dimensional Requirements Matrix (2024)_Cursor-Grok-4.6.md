### Scope and assumptions

1. Group R-2 and high-rise status are project statements, not independently verified classifications.
2. The exact Riyadh AHJ/permit pathway, project stage and SCD NOC status are unconfirmed; therefore this matrix does not conclude compliance.
3. This attached chapter **does not contain** soils, excavation, groundwater, retaining-wall, shallow-foundation or deep-foundation criteria. Those requirements are charged to **SBC 303**.
4. SBC 303 is not attached to this extract. No bearing, frost, pile, drainage, excavation or retaining number is adopted.
5. Foundation type, basement/podium extent, groundwater and geotechnical investigation status are unconfirmed.
6. The Chapter 18 cross-reference table is concatenated OCR. No reconstructed 18xx-to-SBC 303 pairing is adopted as a design-release map.
