- **Outbound-source rule:** No value in this matrix has been imported from Chapter 7, Chapter 9, Chapter 16 Section 1612, Chapter 26 (including 2603.9 / 2603.10 / 2604.2 numeric trim and thermal-barrier limits), Section 718, Section 720, Section 410, Section 602.4, Section 2304.11, SBC 801 Sections 807.5.1–807.5.6, ASCE 24, commentary examples, Figure page-0824 10-percent area calculations, or an existing chapter summary. ### Scope and assumptions

1. Group R-2 and high-rise status are project statements, not independently verified classifications.
2. The exact Riyadh AHJ/permit pathway, project stage, fire-strategy status and SCD NOC status are unconfirmed; therefore this matrix does not conclude compliance.
3. Automatic sprinkler protection is not selected. Table 803.13, 803.5.2, 803.15.2 Exception 1 and 804.4.2 branch on Section **903.3.1.1** or **903.3.1.2**. Both branches are shown; NFPA 13 versus 13R is not assumed.
4. Construction type is unconfirmed. High-rise R-2 is typically Type I or II, which turns on Section 805 combustible-floor rules, but Type I/II is not verified here.
5. Mixed-use podium, amenity assembly, dormitory sleeping-unit program and flood-hazard designation are unconfirmed. Group A finish rows, 806.2 Exception 1 (**75 percent**) and 806.6 pyroxilin are omitted until the gap register opens those uses.
6. Table 803.13 is flattened HTML. Only the R-2 six-letter run (`R-2CCCBBC` delimited by `R-3`) is adopted in project-use. Other occupancy class cells were inventoried as **Verify source** and are not published.
7. Foam plastic as finish or trim is charged to Chapter 26. Commentary millimetre, density and coverage figures for 2604.2 are not adopted.
8. Interior-finish listings, NFPA/ASTM/UL reports and SCD acceptance of decorative fabrics cannot be concluded from this chapter extract alone.
